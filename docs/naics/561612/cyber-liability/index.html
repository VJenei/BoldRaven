Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561612

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561612, Security Guards and Patrol Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Post orders and site information describe how to defeat client security.

    WHY
    Guard companies hold post orders, patrol routes, alarm response procedures, access codes, camera locations, and vulnerability assessments for client sites. That documentation is a complete description of how each site is protected and where it is not.
    LOSS
    Exposure of post orders and site security plans hands attackers exactly what they need to defeat protection at client premises, making the guard company the origin of a physical breach at a client site.
    ASK
    Do your systems hold post orders and access codes for client premises?
  2. 02

    Officer scheduling and licensing determine lawful coverage.

    WHY
    Guard officers require state licensing, training, and in some settings armed permits, with staffing schedules ensuring contracted coverage. Posting an unlicensed officer is a regulatory violation and a liability exposure.
    LOSS
    Uncovered posts and unlicensed staffing where scheduling and credential records are unavailable, with contract breach and regulatory action, and after any incident the licensing record is examined first.
    ASK
    How do you verify officer licensing and training status before assigning a post?
  3. 03

    Incident reports document what happened and support claims.

    WHY
    Officers produce incident reports, patrol logs, and activity records that clients rely on and that become evidence in litigation involving injuries, thefts, and assaults on client premises.
    LOSS
    Litigation over an incident at a client site where the patrol logs and incident reports cannot be produced, which in a negligent security claim effectively concedes that the protection was inadequate.
    ASK
    Could you produce patrol logs and incident reports for a client site from two years ago?
