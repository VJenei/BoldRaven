Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 424410

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 424410, General Line Grocery Merchant Wholesalers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Independent grocers cannot restock from anywhere else.

    WHY
    General line wholesalers supply independent supermarkets and convenience stores with the majority of what they sell, on scheduled deliveries against orders placed electronically from store level. Stores carry days of stock. There is no alternative supplier who can take on a store at a day's notice.
    LOSS
    Empty shelves across dozens or hundreds of stores within days. Grocery retail cannot operate with gaps, and stores that lose customers during an outage do not recover them quickly.
    ASK
    How many retail stores depend on your delivery schedule for the majority of their inventory?
  2. 02

    Food traceability is a federal requirement with a 24 hour clock.

    WHY
    Under FSMA Section 204, entities that hold foods on the Food Traceability List must maintain traceability lot codes and Key Data Elements and provide records to FDA in an electronic sortable spreadsheet within 24 hours of request. The compliance date is July 20, 2028. Wholesalers sit at the point in the chain where traceback either succeeds or fails.
    LOSS
    A traceback that stalls at your records widens an outbreak investigation and a recall to every product and every store you supply, rather than the single implicated lot.
    ASK
    Can you produce lot-level receipt and shipment records in a sortable electronic format?
  3. 03

    Cold chain integrity is monitored and is the condition of sale.

    WHY
    Refrigerated and frozen product moves through temperature controlled warehousing and delivery with continuous monitoring. Under the Sanitary Transportation rule, temperature control during transport must be specified and verified. Excursions require product disposition decisions.
    LOSS
    Loss of temperature monitoring forces disposal of product that cannot be verified as safe, and a warehouse-wide refrigeration control failure destroys inventory measured in millions.
    ASK
    How is temperature monitored in your warehouse and on your delivery vehicles?
