Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 483212

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 483212, Inland Water Passenger Transportation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Passenger vessels carry people who cannot leave if something goes wrong.

    WHY
    Dinner cruises, river ferries, and excursion vessels carry passengers on enclosed waters with limited egress. Passenger counting, safety equipment, and crew certification are Coast Guard regulated, and manifests determine the search and rescue response.
    LOSS
    An incident where passenger accounting is unavailable means responders do not know how many people to look for, and the operator cannot demonstrate it stayed within certificated capacity.
    ASK
    How is passenger count recorded and verified against your certificate of inspection?
  2. 02

    Booking and ticketing hold customer payment and event data.

    WHY
    Excursion and charter operators take advance bookings for cruises, weddings, and corporate events with deposits, stored payment methods, and guest lists. Bookings are made months ahead for dated events.
    LOSS
    Lost bookings for dated events that cannot be rescheduled, with deposits taken and customers arriving for cruises that were never recorded.
    ASK
    How many dated event bookings with deposits are you holding?
  3. 03

    Coast Guard cybersecurity rules reach US-flagged passenger vessels.

    WHY
    The Coast Guard cybersecurity rule effective July 16, 2025 applies to US-flagged vessels, requiring immediate incident reporting, training by January 12, 2026, and a Cybersecurity Officer, assessment, and approved plan by July 16, 2027.
    LOSS
    Operating restrictions during the season, which for a seasonal excursion operator removes the only period in which revenue is earned.
    ASK
    Have you assessed whether the Coast Guard cybersecurity rule applies to your vessels?
