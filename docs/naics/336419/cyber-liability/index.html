Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 336419

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 336419, Other Guided Missile and Space Vehicle Parts and Auxiliary Equipment Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Component technical data is export controlled.

    • Parts for missile and space vehicles are defence articles.
    • Any transfer, including remote access, requires authorisation.
    • Violations carry criminal exposure and debarment.
    ASK
    How is access to controlled technical data restricted and logged?
  2. 02

    Defence contracts require demonstrated cybersecurity compliance.

    • Contracts with controlled information require documented controls and a score.
    • Assessment requirements are a condition of award.
    • There is no commercial substitute for this work.
    ASK
    What is your supplier risk score, and what assessment level do your contracts require?
  3. 03

    A single part failure destroys the whole vehicle.

    • Components are produced under qualified processes with full traceability.
    • One part failure loses a vehicle and payload worth far more than the contract.
    • Without records every unit from the production is suspect.
    ASK
    How long are process and traceability records retained after delivery?
