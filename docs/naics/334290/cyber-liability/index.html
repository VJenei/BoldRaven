Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 334290

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 334290, Other Communications Equipment Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Alarm and life safety equipment must function when needed.

    WHY
    Fire alarm, security, and signalling equipment monitors buildings and transmits alarms to monitoring centres. Communication path failures mean alarms do not reach anyone, and listings govern the required reliability.
    LOSS
    Equipment defects preventing alarm transmission during a fire or intrusion, with fatalities or losses that the system existed to prevent and the manufacturer's listing and design examined.
    ASK
    Does your equipment transmit fire or security alarms to monitoring centres?
  2. 02

    Connected devices are deployed and rarely updated.

    WHY
    Communications devices are installed in buildings and infrastructure and left in place for years with network connectivity and firmware that owners do not track. Vulnerabilities persist for the life of the installation.
    LOSS
    A vulnerability exploited across deployed devices, giving attackers footholds inside customer networks, with the manufacturer required to produce updates for devices whose owners are difficult to reach.
    ASK
    How do deployed devices receive security updates, and who applies them?
  3. 03

    Equipment authorisation depends on the shipped configuration.

    WHY
    Radio and communications equipment requires FCC authorisation tied to a tested configuration, with component or firmware changes potentially invalidating the authorisation.
    LOSS
    Products shipped outside their authorised configuration, which cannot be lawfully marketed, requiring recall and re-authorisation with FCC enforcement exposure.
    ASK
    How are authorised hardware and firmware configurations controlled at build?
