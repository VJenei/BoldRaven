Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 331524

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 331524, Aluminum Foundries (except Die-Casting), needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Molten aluminium and moisture produce explosions.

    WHY
    Aluminium foundries melt and pour metal into sand and permanent moulds, with moisture in moulds, tools, or charge material causing steam explosions. This has killed foundry workers repeatedly.
    LOSS
    A molten metal explosion with worker fatalities and severe burns, investigated against moisture control and charge inspection procedures.
    ASK
    How is moisture controlled in your charge material, tooling, and moulds?
  2. 02

    Casting quality determines performance in customer applications.

    WHY
    Aluminium castings serve automotive, aerospace, and industrial applications with porosity, dimensional, and mechanical requirements verified by testing and radiography. Internal defects are not visible externally.
    LOSS
    Castings with internal porosity or wrong properties entering pressure or structural applications, failing in service with the defect invisible until failure.
    ASK
    How are radiographic and mechanical test results recorded per casting lot?
  3. 03

    Patterns and tooling are customer-specific assets held at the foundry.

    WHY
    Foundries hold customer patterns, core boxes, and permanent moulds with qualified process parameters. Transferring production requires moving tooling and requalifying.
    LOSS
    Customer production stopped with no alternative foundry tooled for the parts, and requalification required even if tooling can be moved.
    ASK
    How many customer-owned patterns and moulds are held at your foundry?
