Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 624410

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 624410, Child Care Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Your records identify exactly where children are during the day.

    WHY
    Child care providers hold children's names, ages, photographs, medical and allergy information, authorised pickup lists, and daily attendance, along with parent contact and payment details. The records identify which children are at a specific location at specific times.
    LOSS
    Exposure of children's identities, photographs, and attendance patterns, and of authorised pickup lists that exist precisely to prevent unauthorised persons collecting a child. This is a child safety exposure, not a data exposure.
    ASK
    Do your records include authorised pickup lists and children's photographs?
  2. 02

    Custody arrangements are recorded because some adults must be excluded.

    WHY
    Enrolment records document custody orders, restraining orders, and individuals who must not be permitted contact with a child. Those restrictions exist because a specific person poses a risk.
    LOSS
    Disclosure to a person the child was being protected from, including the child's location and daily schedule, which is a direct and foreseeable safety event.
    ASK
    Do your records identify individuals who are prohibited from collecting a child?
  3. 03

    Licensing requires staff screening and ratio documentation.

    WHY
    Child care licensing requires background checks including fingerprinting for all staff, documented training, immunisation records for children, and staff to child ratio records, inspected and required after any incident.
    LOSS
    Licensing action where screening and ratio records cannot be produced, and after any allegation of harm the absence of screening documentation is devastating to the provider and to parent confidence.
    ASK
    How do you evidence that every staff member's background check is current?
