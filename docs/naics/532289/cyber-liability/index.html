Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 532289

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 532289, All Other Consumer Goods Rental, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Rental agreements and customer data accumulate without a retention policy.

    WHY
    Consumer rental operations hold identity documents, payment cards, addresses, and rental histories for every customer, frequently retained indefinitely in a point of sale or rental management system with no deletion process.
    LOSS
    Breach notification covering customers from many years ago who no longer transact with you, expanding the affected population and the cost well beyond the active customer base.
    ASK
    How many years of customer records are sitting in your rental system?
  2. 02

    Inventory tracking is the only record of what is out and with whom.

    WHY
    Rental businesses depend on knowing what item is with which customer and when it is due back. Items are individually tracked and frequently high value relative to the rental rate.
    LOSS
    Inability to determine what inventory is out means items that are never recovered, and the loss is the full replacement value rather than the outstanding rental charge.
    ASK
    What is the total value of inventory currently out on rental?
  3. 03

    Damage disputes are resolved from condition records.

    WHY
    Rental businesses assess damage on return against condition documented at check-out, usually with photographs and a signed acknowledgement. Customers dispute damage charges routinely.
    LOSS
    Uncollectable damage charges and chargebacks where condition records cannot be produced, and card networks resolve disputes against the merchant without documentation.
    ASK
    How do you document item condition at check-out and return?
