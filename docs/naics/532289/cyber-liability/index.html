Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 532289

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 532289, All Other Consumer Goods Rental, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Reservations and agreements run the operation.

    • Bookings, agreements, and delivery schedules are recorded per customer.
    • Customers plan events and needs around those dates.
    • A lost booking is a customer with nothing on the day.
    ASK
    How are your reservations and rental agreements recorded?
  2. 02

    Customer records hold contact and payment data.

    • Records carry identity, contact, and payment card information.
    • Charges run automatically against those records.
    • Every affected customer must be notified.
    ASK
    What customer and payment data do you store?
  3. 03

    Inventory tracking determines what you can rent.

    • Availability, condition, and location run on connected systems.
    • Double bookings and missing items follow a systems failure.
    • Inventory you cannot track is inventory you cannot rent.
    ASK
    How is your rental inventory tracked?
