Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 213112

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 213112, Support Activities for Oil and Gas Operations, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    The largest company in this industry lost $35 million to ransomware in a single quarter.

    WHY
    Halliburton disclosed in an SEC filing that an unauthorised party accessed its systems on August 21, 2024, forcing it to take systems offline, including production planning and shipment tracking used to deliver customer service. The company recorded roughly $35 million of pretax cost and confirmed data was removed. The attack was attributed to RansomHub.
    LOSS
    For a smaller service company the proportional impact is worse, not better. Loss of job scheduling, field ticketing, and equipment tracking stops revenue immediately while crews and equipment remain on payroll.
    ASK
    What software do you use for job scheduling and field ticketing, and how long could you operate without it?
  2. 02

    Field tickets are the only record that work was performed.

    WHY
    Service revenue is captured on field tickets signed at the wellsite, then converted to invoices. Operators dispute unsupported tickets as a matter of routine. Electronic ticketing systems hold the signed record, the equipment used, and the hours claimed.
    LOSS
    Unbillable work across the outage period. A pressure pumping or wireline company can have several million dollars of unbilled tickets in the system at any time.
    ASK
    How much unbilled field ticket value is typically sitting in your system?
  3. 03

    You hold your customers' completion designs, which is their most valuable technical data.

    WHY
    Frac designs, fluid systems, proppant loading, stage spacing, and production results are the operator's confidential intellectual property, developed at great cost and closely guarded. Service companies hold this for many operators simultaneously and are bound by confidentiality provisions in every master service agreement.
    LOSS
    Third-party liability to multiple operators from one breach, plus loss of the master service agreements that give you access to the work.
    ASK
    How many operators' completion designs are held on your systems at once?
