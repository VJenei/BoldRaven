Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 611620

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 611620, Sports and Recreation Instruction, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold health and emergency information about children.

    WHY
    Sports instruction collects participant names, ages, medical conditions, allergies, emergency contacts, and signed waivers, frequently for minors. Coaches and instructors have direct supervised contact with children.
    LOSS
    Exposure of children's names, medical information, and session schedules, which is a child safety exposure and a health information disclosure, with parents and youth sports organisations reacting accordingly.
    ASK
    Do you collect medical and emergency information for minor participants?
  2. 02

    Signed waivers are the liability defence for an injury-prone activity.

    WHY
    Participation depends on executed liability waivers and medical acknowledgements, now generally captured electronically at registration. Sports instruction carries foreseeable injury risk and waivers are the primary defence.
    LOSS
    An injury claim where the executed waiver cannot be produced, removing the defence entirely in an activity where injuries are expected and damages for a child's injury are substantial.
    ASK
    Could you produce a signed waiver for a participant who registered two years ago?
  3. 03

    Coach screening is expected and sometimes legally required.

    WHY
    Youth sports organisations require background screening of coaches and staff, with records demonstrating compliance. Federal law imposes reporting obligations on youth-serving organisations in certain sports contexts.
    LOSS
    Inability to demonstrate that coaches were screened, which after any allegation of misconduct is devastating in litigation and to the organisation's standing with governing bodies.
    ASK
    How do you evidence that every coach in contact with children was screened?
