CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 334513, Instruments and Related Products Manufacturing for Measuring, Displaying, and Controlling Industrial Process Variables, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Your instruments are what industrial safety systems act on.

    WHY
    Process instruments measure pressure, temperature, level, and flow, feeding control and safety instrumented systems in refineries, chemical plants, and power stations. Safety systems shut plants down based on what your instruments report.
    LOSS
    Instruments reporting incorrect values, with safety systems failing to act or acting spuriously, and in a hazardous process the consequence is a release, fire, or explosion.
    ASK
    Are your instruments used in safety instrumented functions?
  2. 02

    Safety instrumented systems require certified functional safety.

    WHY
    Instruments used in safety functions are certified to IEC 61508 safety integrity levels with documented design, failure rate data, and change control. Certification depends on the shipped hardware and firmware configuration.
    LOSS
    Product shipped outside its certified configuration, invalidating the safety integrity level in installations that relied on it, requiring reassessment of every safety function using the instrument.
    ASK
    How are certified safety configurations controlled through build and firmware release?
  3. 03

    Instruments connect into plant control networks.

    WHY
    Modern instruments are networked with configuration and diagnostics over industrial protocols, and manufacturers provide configuration software and firmware that runs inside plant control networks.
    LOSS
    Vulnerabilities in instrument firmware or configuration software providing a path into plant control systems, with the manufacturer identified as the vector across every plant using the product.
    ASK
    How is instrument firmware authenticated before it can be installed?
