Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541613

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541613, Marketing Consulting Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Customer databases are handed over for analysis.

    WHY
    Marketing consultants receive customer lists, transaction histories, and behavioural data to segment and target campaigns. That data belongs to the client's customers, who have no relationship with the consultant and did not consent to the transfer.
    LOSS
    Breach exposing client customer databases, with notification obligations under state privacy laws and the client contractually passing the cost and liability to the consultant.
    ASK
    How many client customer databases are held on your systems?
  2. 02

    Unlaunched campaigns and positioning are competitively sensitive.

    WHY
    Consultants hold campaign plans, pricing strategy, product positioning, and launch timing before announcement. Competitors gain a direct advantage from advance knowledge of a campaign or price move.
    LOSS
    Leaked campaign and pricing strategy allows competitors to pre-empt the launch, with the client's marketing investment wasted and the loss claimed against the consultant.
    ASK
    Do you hold unlaunched pricing or campaign plans for any client?
  3. 03

    Privacy regulation now governs how customer data may be used.

    WHY
    State privacy laws impose obligations on processing personal data, including purpose limitation, opt-out rights for targeted advertising, and contractual requirements between controllers and processors. Consultants processing client data are subject to those contractual terms.
    LOSS
    Processing outside the agreed purpose or failing contractual data protection terms creates liability to the client and potential regulatory exposure, separate from any breach.
    ASK
    What do your client data processing agreements permit you to do with customer data?
