Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 326211

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 326211, Tire Manufacturing (except Retreading), needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Tire defects cause fatal crashes and enormous recalls.

    WHY
    Tires are safety-critical products where tread separation and blowouts cause loss of control at speed. Tire recalls involving defects have covered millions of units, produced fatalities, and resulted in federal legislation on defect reporting.
    LOSS
    A defect recall covering millions of tires with fatal crash litigation, NHTSA investigation, and reporting obligations under early warning requirements, at costs that have threatened the survival of manufacturers.
    ASK
    Could you identify every tire produced during a specific period at a specific plant?
  2. 02

    Federal law requires defect reporting and tire identification.

    WHY
    Manufacturers must submit early warning data to NHTSA on claims and incidents, and every tire carries an identification number encoding plant and production week, used to define recall scope.
    LOSS
    Recall scope that cannot be narrowed because production records are unavailable, expanding it across all production in a period, plus reporting failures which carry substantial civil penalties.
    ASK
    How is tire identification data linked to production and material records?
  3. 03

    Curing and building processes are continuous and parameter-driven.

    WHY
    Tire building and curing run to recipes controlling component placement, cure time, temperature, and pressure. Cure parameters determine the properties of the finished tire, and undercure produces latent defects.
    LOSS
    Undercured tires shipped and in service, where the defect appears as separation after months of use, requiring recall of every tire from the affected curing period.
    ASK
    How are cure parameters recorded and linked to individual tires?
