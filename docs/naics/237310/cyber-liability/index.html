Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 237310

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 237310, Highway, Street, and Bridge Construction, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Public bids are submitted electronically and are decided by very small margins.

    WHY
    State DOT work is let through electronic bidding systems at a fixed time, with the low responsive bid taking the work. Estimating files hold unit prices, production rates, and subcontractor and supplier quotes. Highway bids are routinely decided by fractions of a percent.
    LOSS
    Disclosure of bid information loses the work and, if it reaches a competitor before a public letting, invites bid rigging investigation. Federal-aid procurement fraud carries criminal exposure.
    ASK
    Where are your unit prices and supplier quotes stored in the days before a letting?
  2. 02

    Certified payroll on federal-aid work contains every worker's Social Security number.

    WHY
    Davis-Bacon prevailing wage rules require weekly certified payroll for your workforce and every subcontractor, submitted to the agency. Those records carry names, addresses, classifications, and Social Security numbers. Submission is a condition of payment.
    LOSS
    A breach exposing the payroll records of every worker on every subcontractor across your projects, plus withheld progress payments if certified payroll cannot be filed on schedule.
    ASK
    How many workers' certified payroll records do you hold, including your subcontractors?
  3. 03

    Machine control and survey data drive the grade the work is built to.

    WHY
    Highway work runs GPS machine control on dozers, graders, and pavers from a digital terrain model, with automated machine guidance tied to the design surface. The model is the design. A corrupted or wrong model builds the road to the wrong grade over long distances before anyone notices.
    LOSS
    Rework of grade and pavement across large areas at the contractor's cost, plus schedule damage on a project with liquidated damages and lane rental charges.
    ASK
    Do you run GPS machine control from digital design models?
