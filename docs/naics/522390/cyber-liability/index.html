Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 522390

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 522390, Other Activities Related to Credit Intermediation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You handle borrower and account data for others.

    • Servicing, processing, and support involve complete financial records.
    • Clients remain regulated and pass those obligations to you.
    • Every affected consumer must be notified.
    ASK
    What borrower and account data do you handle for clients?
  2. 02

    Payments move against instructions you process.

    • Disbursements, collections, and transfers are processed on client instruction.
    • Fraudulent instructions divert client and consumer money.
    • Recovery after a wire is rarely possible.
    ASK
    How are payment instructions verified before funds move?
  3. 03

    Clients audit your controls as a service provider.

    • Financial institution clients examine service providers directly.
    • Security certifications and audit reports are contract conditions.
    • Losing certification removes clients you cannot replace.
    ASK
    What security certifications do your financial institution clients require?
