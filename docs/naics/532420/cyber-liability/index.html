Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 532420

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 532420, Office Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    The equipment you lease stores your customers' documents.

    WHY
    Multifunction printers and copiers retain images of scanned and printed documents on internal storage. Lessors manage these fleets remotely for meter reading, service, and supplies, with network access into customer environments.
    LOSS
    Customer documents recovered from returned or resold equipment, or a compromise through fleet management access reaching customer networks, making the lessor the source of the customer's breach.
    ASK
    How is storage sanitised and certified on equipment returned at end of lease?
  2. 02

    Meter data drives billing across the entire installed base.

    WHY
    Lease and service billing is calculated from meter readings collected automatically from devices, with supplies replenished and service dispatched from the same data. Manual meter collection across thousands of devices is not practical.
    LOSS
    Unbillable usage across the fleet and supply replenishment failures leaving customers unable to print, with lease funders requiring documentation you cannot produce.
    ASK
    How many devices report meter readings automatically to your systems?
  3. 03

    Lease portfolios are financed against contract documentation.

    WHY
    Equipment leases are frequently discounted or sold to third-party funders against the executed contract and payment stream. Funders require original documentation and verification of delivery and acceptance.
    LOSS
    Inability to produce executed lease documentation blocks funding of new contracts and creates repurchase obligations on existing ones, which is an immediate liquidity event.
    ASK
    What documentation must you produce to fund a lease with your funder?
