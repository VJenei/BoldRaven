Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561210

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561210, Facilities Support Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You operate building systems on the client's behalf.

    WHY
    Facilities support providers run HVAC, access control, life safety, and utility systems inside client premises, frequently with remote monitoring and vendor connections. Clients include government, healthcare, and data centre operations where environmental control is critical.
    LOSS
    Loss of environmental or access control at client facilities, with damage to client operations and equipment far exceeding the service contract value, plus security consequences where access control was involved.
    ASK
    Which client building systems can you operate remotely?
  2. 02

    Base and government support contracts carry security requirements.

    WHY
    Facilities support at federal and military installations requires personnel security clearances, controlled access, and contractual cybersecurity obligations under FAR and DFARS clauses. Facility layouts and system information are frequently controlled.
    LOSS
    Loss of contract eligibility and clearance sponsorship, plus disclosure of installation information that carries national security consequences and criminal exposure.
    ASK
    Do your contracts include federal safeguarding or clearance requirements?
  3. 03

    Your workforce has physical access to client premises around the clock.

    WHY
    Support staff hold keys, access credentials, and after-hours entry to client facilities, with assignment and credentialing records held in your systems along with staff background check results.
    LOSS
    Exposure of access credentials and staffing schedules provides a route into client premises, making the provider the origin of a physical security incident inside a client's business.
    ASK
    How many client facilities can your staff credentials open?
