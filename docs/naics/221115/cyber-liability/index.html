Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 221115

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 221115, Wind Electric Power Generation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    The turbine manufacturer holds remote access to your fleet, and manufacturers get attacked.

    WHY
    Wind turbines run manufacturer SCADA with remote monitoring, curtailment, yaw and pitch control, and firmware management, typically retained by the OEM under a service agreement. Vestas, one of the largest turbine manufacturers, disclosed a cyber incident in November 2021 that compromised company data and forced systems offline.
    LOSS
    A compromise at the OEM or of the OEM's access path reaches every site under that service agreement at once. Fleet-wide curtailment or shutdown removes all revenue while turbine debt service continues.
    ASK
    Which parties hold remote access to your turbine control system, and under what agreement?
  2. 02

    Turbines must curtail and shut down on command for grid, wildlife, and weather reasons.

    WHY
    Wind sites curtail on ISO dispatch, shut down for icing and high wind, and in many permits must feather during bat activity or specific wind speeds at night. Compliance is demonstrated from SCADA records. Overspeed protection is the last line before structural damage.
    LOSS
    Failure to curtail as permitted is an environmental permit violation with penalties and possible operating restrictions. Loss of shutdown control in a wind event risks turbine damage measured in millions per unit.
    ASK
    Do your permits require curtailment under specific conditions, and how is compliance evidenced?
  3. 03

    Power purchase agreements settle on metered output and availability.

    WHY
    Revenue comes from a PPA or merchant sales settled on interval meter data, with availability guarantees from the service provider and production forecasts submitted to the ISO. Forecast errors carry imbalance charges in some markets.
    LOSS
    Uncontested settlement and imbalance charges during the outage, plus inability to claim against the availability guarantee without operational data.
    ASK
    How do you verify your monthly PPA settlement against your own production data?
