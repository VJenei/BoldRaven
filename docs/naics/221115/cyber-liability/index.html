Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 221115

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 221115, Wind Electric Power Generation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Turbines are controlled and updated remotely across the fleet.

    • Turbine controllers, pitch, and yaw systems are managed from a central platform.
    • Manufacturers and operators both hold remote access for diagnostics and updates.
    • Loss of control at speed can destroy a turbine outright.
    ASK
    Who holds remote access to your turbine control systems?
  2. 02

    Grid reliability rules apply to facilities of your size.

    • Federal reliability standards impose documented security requirements on covered facilities.
    • Compliance is audited with penalties assessed per day of violation.
    • Findings attach to the registered entity.
    ASK
    Which of your systems fall within grid reliability security requirements?
  3. 03

    Metered output is what you actually get paid for.

    • Generation is metered and settled under power purchase agreements.
    • Renewable credits are issued from that recorded output.
    • Corrupted generation data is production you cannot bill or claim.
    ASK
    How is your generation metering data recorded and reported for settlement?
