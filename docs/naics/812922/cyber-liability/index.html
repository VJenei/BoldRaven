Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 812922

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 812922, One-Hour Photofinishing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Customer images pass through your systems and stay there.

    WHY
    Retail photofinishing receives customer images from devices and media, processed through kiosks and production systems that retain files after the order. Content is personal and frequently sensitive.
    LOSS
    Exposure of customer images retained on kiosks and production systems, which customers had no reason to believe were kept and which are frequently deeply personal.
    ASK
    How long do customer images remain on your kiosks and production systems after printing?
  2. 02

    Kiosks are self-service devices in public retail space.

    WHY
    Order kiosks accept media and device connections from customers in unsupervised retail environments, with cached data and network connections back to production systems.
    LOSS
    Kiosks used to capture customer data or as an entry point to the retail network, with the customer content on them exposed and the store network reached through a public-facing device.
    ASK
    How are your self-service kiosks isolated from your store network?
  3. 03

    Turnaround promises are the service proposition.

    WHY
    The format is defined by rapid turnaround, with customers waiting or returning within the hour. Production and payment run on connected systems in a retail setting.
    LOSS
    Orders that cannot be produced or collected, with customers who chose the format specifically for speed going elsewhere permanently.
    ASK
    What happens to waiting customers if production is unavailable?
