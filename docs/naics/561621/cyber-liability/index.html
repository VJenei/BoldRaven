Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561621

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561621, Security Systems Services (except Locksmiths), needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold the keys to your clients' security systems.

    • Alarm codes, camera access, and system credentials are held for every client.
    • That access disables the protection you installed.
    • An intruder with your access walks in unopposed.
    ASK
    What client alarm codes and system credentials do you hold?
  2. 02

    Monitoring must receive and act on every alarm.

    • Alarm signals arrive continuously and are dispatched to police and clients.
    • A missed signal means no response to a real event.
    • Clients rely on that response absolutely.
    ASK
    How are alarm signals received and dispatched from your monitoring centre?
  3. 03

    Installed systems are reachable from your platform.

    • Cameras, panels, and access systems connect back to your management platform.
    • One platform reaches every installation.
    • Compromise gives an intruder access and surveillance across your client base.
    ASK
    How many client systems can be reached from your management platform?
