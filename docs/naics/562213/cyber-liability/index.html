Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 562213

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 562213, Solid Waste Combustors and Incinerators, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Combustion and emissions control run continuously under permit limits.

    WHY
    Waste to energy and incineration facilities operate under strict emissions permits with continuous emissions monitoring for parameters including dioxins, mercury, and particulates. Combustion temperature and residence time are permit conditions, and air pollution control equipment is controlled automatically.
    LOSS
    Emissions exceedances reported and penalised, with a facility that cannot demonstrate compliant combustion required to cease accepting waste until resolved, and community and regulatory reaction that is immediate.
    ASK
    What are your permit consequences for a period of unmonitored emissions?
  2. 02

    Waste receipt cannot stop because the region has nowhere else to send it.

    WHY
    Combustion facilities serve municipal contracts with guaranteed tonnage and are frequently the primary disposal outlet for a region. Waste continues to be collected whether or not the facility can receive it.
    LOSS
    Regional waste backing up with no alternative capacity, contractual failure on put-or-pay municipal agreements, and emergency diversion costs that fall on the facility.
    ASK
    Where does the waste go if your facility cannot accept deliveries for a week?
  3. 03

    Energy generation and grid interconnection add obligations.

    WHY
    Waste to energy facilities sell power under agreements with availability obligations and interconnection requirements, and larger units may fall within bulk electric system reliability standards. Output is metered and settled.
    LOSS
    Failure to deliver contracted power alongside the waste disposal failure, with two sets of contractual damages arising from a single incident.
    ASK
    Do you sell power under an agreement with availability or capacity obligations?
