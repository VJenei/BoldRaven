Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 621210

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 621210, Offices of Dentists, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Practice management is the whole office.

    • Charts, imaging, scheduling, and billing run on one system.
    • Treatment plans depend on imaging and history.
    • Patients arrive whether or not the system is running.
    ASK
    What happens to scheduled patients if your practice system is unavailable?
  2. 02

    Patient records are federally protected.

    • Health privacy law requires notification of every affected patient.
    • Regulators investigate breaches over five hundred records.
    • Penalties are assessed per record.
    ASK
    How many patient records do you hold?
  3. 03

    Imaging and scans exist only digitally.

    • Radiographs and intraoral scans are the diagnostic record.
    • Retaking imaging means additional radiation exposure for the patient.
    • Some historical imaging cannot be recreated at all.
    ASK
    Where is your patient imaging stored and backed up?
