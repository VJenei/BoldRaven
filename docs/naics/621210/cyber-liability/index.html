Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 621210

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 621210, Offices of Dentists, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Dental practices are a favoured ransomware target with weak defences.

    WHY
    Dental offices hold complete patient records and imaging while operating as small businesses with limited IT support, frequently relying on a single practice management system and a local server. Attacks on dental practice software providers have affected hundreds of practices simultaneously.
    LOSS
    Schedules cancelled, imaging and treatment plans inaccessible, and claims unbillable, with a practice that has no capacity to fund forensic investigation and notification for its whole patient base.
    ASK
    Where is your practice management data stored, and when was the backup last tested?
  2. 02

    You are a HIPAA covered entity holding imaging and treatment records.

    WHY
    Dental records include radiographs, treatment histories, medical conditions relevant to care, and insurance information. As a covered entity the practice carries notification obligations to patients, HHS, and the media for larger breaches.
    LOSS
    HIPAA notification and OCR enforcement, with the risk analysis requirement being the most commonly cited failure and the practice unable to demonstrate one was ever performed.
    ASK
    Has your practice completed and documented a HIPAA security risk analysis?
  3. 03

    Imaging and CAD systems drive the clinical work.

    WHY
    Digital radiography, cone beam imaging, intraoral scanning, and chairside milling are networked systems producing and consuming patient-specific data. Restorations are designed and milled from scan data.
    LOSS
    Inability to image or fabricate restorations, with patients in temporary restorations waiting, and imaging histories lost that document conditions over years of treatment.
    ASK
    How many years of patient radiographs are stored on your imaging system?
