Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 621493

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 621493, Freestanding Ambulatory Surgical and Emergency Centers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Surgical schedules cannot proceed without patient records.

    WHY
    Surgical centres operate scheduled procedures requiring pre-operative assessments, consents, allergy and medication histories, implant records, and anaesthesia documentation. Proceeding without them is a patient safety decision, not an administrative one.
    LOSS
    Cancelled surgical schedules with patients prepared and fasted, staff and anaesthesia committed, and implants ordered, plus revenue lost on high-value procedures that cannot simply be rescheduled into a full calendar.
    ASK
    Would you proceed with a case if the pre-operative record were unavailable?
  2. 02

    Emergency centres cannot control when patients arrive.

    WHY
    Freestanding emergency facilities receive walk-in and ambulance patients continuously and are subject to EMTALA screening and stabilisation obligations regardless of system status. Diversion is not always possible and the obligation attaches once a patient presents.
    LOSS
    Patients treated without records or history, with clinical risk on every encounter, and EMTALA obligations that continue whether or not registration and clinical systems are functioning.
    ASK
    What are your EMTALA obligations to a patient who arrives while your systems are down?
  3. 03

    Controlled substances and implants require tracked records.

    WHY
    Anaesthesia and pain management involve controlled substances with DEA recordkeeping and reconciliation requirements, and implantable devices require unique device identifier tracking for recall purposes.
    LOSS
    DEA findings for unreconcilable controlled substance records, and inability to identify patients who received a recalled implant, which obstructs a safety recall for devices already in people.
    ASK
    Could you identify every patient who received a specific implant lot?
