Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 424590

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 424590, Other Farm Product Raw Material Merchant Wholesalers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Commodity contracts settle on grade determined by third parties.

    WHY
    Cotton, wool, tobacco, and similar raw materials are traded on grade and quality determined by classing offices, laboratories, or buyer inspection. Contracts specify quality thresholds with discounts and rejection rights. The grade data comes from outside your systems and drives settlement.
    LOSS
    Unresolvable settlement on delivered lots where classing and quality records are unavailable, on contracts commonly worth several hundred thousand dollars each.
    ASK
    How do you receive and retain grading or classing data for the lots you trade?
  2. 02

    Electronic warehouse receipts carry title to stored commodity.

    WHY
    Cotton and some other agricultural commodities are titled through electronic warehouse receipt systems authorised under the US Warehouse Act. The receipt is the ownership document and it exists only as a database record protected by account credentials.
    LOSS
    Fraudulent transfer of receipts or loss of account access during a pricing window, on holdings that can run to seven figures of contained value.
    ASK
    Do you hold electronic warehouse receipts, and who has access to those accounts?
  3. 03

    Export documentation determines whether the cargo moves.

    WHY
    Raw agricultural materials are heavily exported, requiring phytosanitary certificates, certificates of origin, quality certificates, and letter of credit documentation that must match exactly. Banks reject documents for trivial discrepancies.
    LOSS
    Documentary discrepancies under a letter of credit delay or prevent payment on a shipment already at sea, and demurrage accrues while the paperwork is corrected.
    ASK
    Do you ship against letters of credit requiring exact documentary compliance?
