Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 923130

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 923130, Administration of Human Resource Programs (except Education, Public Health, and Veterans' Affairs Programs), needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Benefit programmes hold complete financial and family data.

    WHY
    Agencies administering assistance programmes hold applicant Social Security numbers, income, household composition, immigration status, disability determinations, and banking details for benefit payment, across very large caseloads.
    LOSS
    Breach exposing complete household financial and immigration data for a population already in economic difficulty, with identity theft consequences they have no capacity to absorb or remediate.
    ASK
    How many benefit recipients' records with Social Security numbers do you hold?
  2. 02

    Benefit payments are targeted for organised fraud.

    WHY
    Assistance programmes disburse funds to individuals based on eligibility determinations, and have been targeted by large-scale organised fraud using stolen and synthetic identities, most visibly in unemployment insurance programmes.
    LOSS
    Large-scale fraudulent claims paid before detection, with public funds lost, legitimate claimants delayed by fraud controls, and legislative investigation into how the programme was compromised.
    ASK
    How do you verify claimant identity before benefits are disbursed?
  3. 03

    Interruption leaves recipients without income or food.

    WHY
    Recipients depend on benefit payments for food, rent, and essentials, with no reserve. Eligibility systems and payment issuance run on a schedule and beneficiaries plan around payment dates.
    LOSS
    Missed benefit issuance leaving households without food or rent money, which produces immediate hardship, emergency political response, and litigation on behalf of affected recipients.
    ASK
    What happens to recipients if a scheduled benefit issuance cannot be processed?
