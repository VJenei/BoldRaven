Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 522299

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 522299, International, Secondary Market, and All Other Nondepository Credit Intermediation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Loan portfolio data is the asset being bought and sold.

    WHY
    Secondary market participants acquire, pool, and sell loan portfolios where the value depends entirely on loan-level data covering borrower credit, collateral, payment history, and documentation completeness. Due diligence files move between counterparties containing complete borrower records.
    LOSS
    Loan pools that cannot be valued or sold because loan-level data cannot be produced, plus breach exposure covering borrowers of loans you may have owned only briefly.
    ASK
    How many borrowers' loan-level records do you hold across acquired portfolios?
  2. 02

    Cross-border activity brings sanctions and AML obligations.

    WHY
    International credit intermediation requires sanctions screening, anti-money laundering programmes, and beneficial ownership verification, with records demonstrating that screening occurred. Sanctions compliance is strict liability and enforcement is aggressive.
    LOSS
    Sanctions violations carrying substantial civil penalties on a strict liability basis, where the absence of screening records makes the violation impossible to mitigate.
    ASK
    How do you evidence sanctions and AML screening on each transaction?
  3. 03

    Settlement of large transactions runs on emailed instructions.

    WHY
    Portfolio purchases, participations, and syndications settle by wire against instructions exchanged between counterparties, law firms, and trustees. Amounts are very large and counterparties frequently transact infrequently with each other.
    LOSS
    A diverted settlement in the millions, where the counterparty's obligation is disputed and neither party's insurance responds cleanly to a deception loss.
    ASK
    How are settlement instructions verified on a portfolio transaction?
