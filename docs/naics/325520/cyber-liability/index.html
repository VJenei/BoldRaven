Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 325520

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 325520, Adhesive Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Bond failure appears after the customer's product is built.

    • Adhesive performance determines whether assembled products hold together.
    • Formulation and process control determine that performance.
    • Failures appear weeks later across everything built with the batch.
    ASK
    How are your batch formulations verified and tested before release?
  2. 02

    Formulations are proprietary and hard to replicate.

    • Adhesive chemistry is developed for specific substrates and conditions.
    • That formulation is the reason customers buy from you.
    • Once disclosed it cannot be recovered.
    ASK
    Where are your formulations stored, and who can access them?
  3. 03

    Customers qualify your adhesive into their assembly process.

    • Manufacturers validate your specific product into their production.
    • Substitution requires requalification and retesting.
    • A supply gap stops their assembly, not just your shipment.
    ASK
    How long would a customer need to qualify a substitute for your adhesive?
