Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 483113

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 483113, Coastal and Great Lakes Freight Transportation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Jones Act trades serve customers with no alternative carrier.

    WHY
    Domestic coastwise and Great Lakes trades are restricted to qualifying US-flag vessels, and the number of vessels serving a given trade is small. Customers in Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and Great Lakes bulk trades have limited or no alternative capacity.
    LOSS
    A disruption removes capacity that cannot be replaced from the international fleet, with downstream shortages in island and remote markets and contractual failure to shippers with no other option.
    ASK
    How many other carriers could serve your customers if you could not sail?
  2. 02

    Coast Guard cybersecurity requirements are now in force.

    WHY
    US-flagged vessels and MTSA-regulated facilities fall under the Coast Guard rule effective July 16, 2025, requiring immediate incident reporting to the National Response Center, training by January 12, 2026, and a Cybersecurity Officer, assessment, and approved plan by July 16, 2027.
    LOSS
    Detention and Captain of the Port orders halting operations, which in a small trade removes a meaningful share of national capacity.
    ASK
    Are your vessels US-flagged and therefore subject to the Coast Guard cyber rule?
  3. 03

    Cargo operations and stability calculations are computer-managed.

    WHY
    Loading, ballast, and stability are calculated by loading computers, and departure requires a verified stability condition. Bulk and articulated tug-barge operations manage cargo transfer through automated systems with pollution prevention interlocks.
    LOSS
    A stability or transfer error causing structural damage, capsizing, or a pollution incident, which under the Oil Pollution Act carries removal costs and damages well beyond the vessel value.
    ASK
    How is your loading and stability computer secured and backed up?
