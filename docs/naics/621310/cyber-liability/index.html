Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 621310

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 621310, Offices of Chiropractors, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Small practices hold complete patient records with minimal protection.

    WHY
    Chiropractic offices are typically small businesses holding patient histories, imaging, treatment plans, and insurance information on a practice management system with no dedicated IT support and frequently no tested backup.
    LOSS
    Total loss of patient records and scheduling with no capacity to fund recovery, plus HIPAA notification obligations across the entire patient base that the practice must pay for itself.
    ASK
    Who is responsible for your practice's backups, and when were they last verified?
  2. 02

    HIPAA obligations apply in full regardless of practice size.

    WHY
    Chiropractors transmitting claims electronically are covered entities with the same security rule obligations as a hospital, including risk analysis, safeguards, and breach notification to patients, HHS, and the media for larger breaches.
    LOSS
    OCR enforcement examining the risk analysis, safeguards, and business associate agreements, where small practices are frequently found to have none of them documented.
    ASK
    Do you have signed business associate agreements with every vendor that touches patient data?
  3. 03

    Personal injury cases make records evidence in litigation.

    WHY
    Chiropractic practices treat personal injury patients whose records become evidence in litigation and insurance claims, subject to subpoena and requiring production years afterward. Treatment documentation supports the patient's claim and the practice's billing.
    LOSS
    Records unavailable when subpoenaed, undermining patients' claims and exposing the practice to allegations that documentation never supported the billing.
    ASK
    How often are your records subpoenaed for personal injury litigation?
