Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 621498

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 621498, All Other Outpatient Care Centers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Outpatient centres hold clinical records under full HIPAA obligations.

    WHY
    Centres in this category deliver clinical services and maintain patient records, diagnoses, and treatment documentation as covered entities, with security rule, risk analysis, and breach notification obligations regardless of size.
    LOSS
    HIPAA notification across the patient population with OCR enforcement examining the risk analysis and safeguards, which smaller centres frequently cannot evidence.
    ASK
    When was your last documented HIPAA security risk analysis?
  2. 02

    Care delivery stops when clinical systems stop.

    WHY
    Outpatient care depends on scheduling, clinical documentation, orders, and results reporting through connected systems. Patients arrive for appointments booked weeks ahead and treatment courses that cannot be interrupted.
    LOSS
    Cancelled clinics with patients whose treatment courses are interrupted, and no ability to bill for care that cannot be documented.
    ASK
    How many patients are scheduled at your centre in a typical week?
  3. 03

    Payer documentation supports every claim years later.

    WHY
    Claims require documented medical necessity, orders, and treatment records, audited by Medicare and commercial payers years after service with extrapolated findings applied across the claim population.
    LOSS
    Extrapolated recoupment across thousands of claims where supporting documentation is unavailable, routinely exceeding the value of the claims actually reviewed.
    ASK
    How many years of clinical documentation are you required to retain for audits?
