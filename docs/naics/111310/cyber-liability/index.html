Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111310

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111310, Orange Groves, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Freeze protection uses pumps and wind machines that are started remotely.

    WHY
    Citrus is protected from freeze events by microsprinkler irrigation and wind machines. These are started based on remote temperature telemetry and are controlled by app or SCADA, usually overnight. The decision window is under an hour.
    LOSS
    One unprotected freeze night destroys the fruit crop and can kill young trees. Grove replanting costs $8,000 to $15,000 per acre and takes 4 to 7 years to return to production.
    ASK
    Are your frost protection pumps or wind machines started or monitored remotely?
  2. 02

    Greening management is run from multi-year block-level records.

    WHY
    HLB, or citrus greening, is managed through tree-by-tree nutrition programs, psyllid scouting maps, injection records, and multi-year block yield and mortality data. This data is held in grove management software and directs the treatment program.
    LOSS
    Loss of block records requires restarting the nutrition and psyllid program without historical reference on a disease that kills trees faster than the data can be rebuilt.
    ASK
    Do you use grove management software to track block-level treatment and yield history?
  3. 03

    Payment is calculated from pound solids measured in the processor's laboratory.

    WHY
    Processing oranges are paid on pound solids, determined by Brix and juice yield measured at the processor and recorded in the processor's system. Fresh fruit is paid on packout percentage calculated at the packinghouse. The grower has no independent measurement.
    LOSS
    Unresolved settlement across a season. If the processor cannot receive fruit, there is no alternative outlet for a perishable crop that cannot be held on the tree.
    ASK
    Is your payment based on grade or yield data generated by the buyer?
