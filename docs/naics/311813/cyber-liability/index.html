Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311813

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311813, Frozen Cakes, Pies, and Other Pastries Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Frozen storage failure condemns high value finished goods.

    WHY
    Finished frozen bakery product is held in freezer storage and distributed frozen, with temperature monitored continuously. Product that thaws cannot be refrozen and sold, and finished bakery goods carry substantial value per pallet.
    LOSS
    A freezer control or monitoring failure condemning finished inventory, with the loss falling on completed product rather than raw materials and disposal costs on top.
    ASK
    What is the value of finished goods in your freezer right now?
  2. 02

    Allergen control spans a wide ingredient range.

    WHY
    Bakery products combine wheat, milk, egg, soy, tree nuts, peanuts, and sesame across shared production and packaging lines, with allergen control depending on scheduling, cleaning, and label accuracy.
    LOSS
    An allergen recall with anaphylaxis exposure and a scope determined by whether production and distribution records can identify affected lots across foodservice and retail customers.
    ASK
    How many major allergens are handled across your production lines?
  3. 03

    Seasonal demand concentrates into holiday periods.

    WHY
    Frozen dessert and pastry demand concentrates heavily around holidays, with production running months ahead and retailers placing orders for delivery into narrow windows before the holiday.
    LOSS
    Missed holiday delivery windows leaving product that has no market after the date, with production cost sunk and retail shelf space lost for the season.
    ASK
    What share of your annual volume ships into holiday windows?
