Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333613

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333613, Mechanical Power Transmission Equipment Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Components fail at load and throw parts.

    • Couplings, clutches, chain, and belt drives transmit power in machinery.
    • Failure at load releases stored energy and throws components.
    • The driven equipment stops immediately.
    ASK
    What load ratings do your components carry, and how are they verified?
  2. 02

    Ratings depend on material and process control.

    • Rated torque depends on material, heat treatment, and machining accuracy.
    • A component that looks correct may not meet its rating.
    • The failure appears under load in the customer's machine.
    ASK
    How are material and heat treatment records maintained per production lot?
  3. 03

    Distributors stock your parts for breakdowns.

    • Components are stocked and sold for breakdown replacement.
    • The customer's machine is already stopped waiting for the part.
    • A supply gap extends every one of those breakdowns.
    ASK
    How quickly do distributors expect replenishment on breakdown items?
