Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333514

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333514, Special Die and Tool, Die Set, Jig, and Fixture Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Every tool is built from customer data on CNC equipment.

    WHY
    Dies, jigs, and fixtures are designed from customer part and assembly models and machined from CNC programmes. The design and programming effort per tool is measured in weeks.
    LOSS
    Loss of tool designs and machining programmes with tools in build, requiring redesign from customer models and delaying customer production programmes that are already scheduled around delivery.
    ASK
    How many active tool designs and CNC programmes are held on your network?
  2. 02

    Tools carry customer proprietary product geometry.

    WHY
    Dies and fixtures embody the customer's part design, frequently for products not yet in production. Automotive, aerospace, and defence customers impose confidentiality and, for defence, export control obligations.
    LOSS
    Exposure of customer product designs including any export controlled data, with contract breach and, where the data is controlled, a reportable violation.
    ASK
    Do you hold designs subject to export control or pre-launch confidentiality?
  3. 03

    Customer production cannot start without the tool.

    WHY
    Production tooling gates the start of the customer's manufacturing. There is no partial substitute and no alternative supplier without transferring the design and starting over.
    LOSS
    Tool delivery delayed with the customer unable to begin production, and consequential losses driven by their launch commitments rather than the price of the tool.
    ASK
    What does a delayed tool cost your customer relative to the tool price?
