Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 531312

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 531312, Nonresidential Property Managers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You operate the building systems tenants' businesses depend on.

    WHY
    Managers run HVAC, elevators, life safety, and access control on behalf of owners, frequently through remotely accessible building automation platforms with vendor support connections. Tenants have no control over these systems but depend on them completely.
    LOSS
    Environmental or access control failure damaging tenant operations, with claims under leases and potentially for tenant business interruption that far exceeds the management fee.
    ASK
    Which building systems can be operated remotely, and by whom?
  2. 02

    Trust accounting and CAM reconciliation handle owners' and tenants' money.

    WHY
    Managers collect rent and common area maintenance charges, pay operating expenses, and reconcile annually with tenants who have audit rights. Trust accounts are regulated and reconciliation errors are pursued by tenants.
    LOSS
    Unreconcilable CAM charges triggering tenant audits and repayment demands across the rent roll, plus trust account exposure where owner funds cannot be accounted for.
    ASK
    Do your leases give tenants the right to audit CAM reconciliations?
  3. 03

    Tenant and lease data is confidential to each tenant.

    WHY
    Managers hold lease terms, financial statements provided by tenants, sales reports for percentage rent, and operational information about each tenant's business. That information is confidential and commercially sensitive between tenants who are sometimes competitors.
    LOSS
    Exposure of tenant financials and lease economics damages the owner's position with every tenant and creates claims from tenants whose confidential business data was disclosed.
    ASK
    Do you hold tenant sales reports or financial statements under confidentiality obligations?
