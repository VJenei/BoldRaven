Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541910

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541910, Marketing Research and Public Opinion Polling, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Respondents participate on an explicit promise of confidentiality.

    WHY
    Survey and panel research collects opinions, behaviours, and personal characteristics from identified respondents who agreed to participate on the basis that responses would be confidential or anonymised. Political and sensitive topic research collects views respondents would not state publicly.
    LOSS
    Exposure of linked respondent identities and responses breaks the specific promise that obtained participation, causes personal harm where topics are sensitive, and destroys panel participation rates permanently.
    ASK
    Can respondent identities be linked to their individual responses in your data?
  2. 02

    Client research reveals strategy before it is executed.

    WHY
    Research is commissioned to test products, pricing, messaging, and positioning before launch. The research itself tells a competitor what the client is planning and what tested well or badly.
    LOSS
    Disclosure of client research hands competitors both the strategic direction and the testing results, with claims against the research firm under the engagement confidentiality terms.
    ASK
    Do you hold unreleased product or pricing research for clients?
  3. 03

    Panel databases are large aggregations of personal data.

    WHY
    Research firms maintain panels with demographic profiles, participation histories, incentive payment details, and accumulated response data across many studies for hundreds of thousands of individuals.
    LOSS
    Breach of the panel database exposes a rich profile of each panellist accumulated across studies, with notification obligations and the collapse of the panel that is the firm's operating asset.
    ASK
    How many panellists' profiles and response histories are held in your database?
