Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 522180

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 522180, Savings Institutions and Other Depository Credit Intermediation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    The 36-hour incident notification rule applies to you.

    WHY
    Banking agencies require notification to the primary federal regulator within 36 hours of determining that a computer-security incident has materially affected or is reasonably likely to materially affect operations, service delivery, or financial sector stability. The obligation applies regardless of institution size.
    LOSS
    Late notification becomes a supervisory finding, and the resulting examination reviews the information security programme, vendor oversight, and board reporting in full.
    ASK
    Does your incident response plan include the 36-hour regulatory notification requirement?
  2. 02

    Mortgage and consumer lending files hold complete personal financial records.

    WHY
    Depository lenders hold loan applications with Social Security numbers, tax returns, pay stubs, bank statements, appraisals, and title documents, retained for the life of the loan and beyond. A mortgage file is the most complete financial profile of an individual that exists outside a credit bureau.
    LOSS
    Breach notification across a borrower base whose files enable complete identity takeover, with GLBA obligations and state notification laws applying simultaneously.
    ASK
    How many years of closed loan files with full borrower documentation do you retain?
  3. 03

    Smaller institutions depend on a few vendors for everything.

    WHY
    Community-scale institutions outsource core processing, digital banking, card services, and often IT management. Regulators expect documented vendor due diligence and contractual security requirements, and a vendor failure presents to customers as an institution failure.
    LOSS
    Complete service outage caused by a vendor, with the institution carrying the regulatory obligation, the customer relationship damage, and the recovery cost.
    ASK
    How many critical functions depend on a single outsourced provider?
