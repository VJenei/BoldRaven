Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 611310

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 611310, Colleges, Universities, and Professional Schools, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Universities hold research that foreign governments actively target.

    WHY
    Federal agencies have repeatedly warned that academic research, particularly in defence-adjacent, biotechnology, and advanced technology fields, is a priority collection target for foreign intelligence services. Universities are deliberately open environments with international collaboration built into their operating model.
    LOSS
    Theft of federally funded research transfers years of work with no recoverable damages, and where export controlled data is involved, an intrusion by a foreign actor may itself constitute an unauthorised export with criminal exposure.
    ASK
    Is any of your research subject to export control or federal safeguarding requirements?
  2. 02

    The institution holds records for everyone who ever attended.

    WHY
    Universities retain student records permanently for transcript purposes, holding education records under FERPA, financial aid files, health and counselling records, disability files, and payment data across generations of students, plus employee and research subject data.
    LOSS
    Breach notification across alumni spanning decades, with counselling and health centre records engaging health privacy obligations and financial aid files providing complete family financial profiles.
    ASK
    How many former students' records are held permanently in your systems?
  3. 03

    Academic medical centres bring HIPAA into the same environment.

    WHY
    Universities with medical, dental, nursing, and veterinary programmes operate clinical services subject to HIPAA, frequently on networks shared with academic and research computing. Clinical, research, and education data coexist in one institution.
    LOSS
    A single incident triggering HIPAA notification, FERPA obligations, and research sponsor reporting simultaneously, each with separate regulators and separate timelines.
    ASK
    Are your clinical systems segregated from academic and research networks?
