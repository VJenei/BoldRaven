Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311821

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311821, Cookie and Cracker Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    A single malware event cost one snack manufacturer over $100 million.

    WHY
    Mondelez, a major snack and biscuit manufacturer, suffered severe disruption from the NotPetya malware in 2017 with losses reported in excess of $100 million, and subsequently litigated for years with its insurer over whether a war exclusion applied to the claim. High volume snack manufacturing depends entirely on connected production and distribution systems.
    LOSS
    Production and distribution halted across multiple plants with retail supply interrupted, and the additional risk that a nation-state attributed attack triggers war exclusion disputes with insurers.
    ASK
    Does your policy contain a war or hostile act exclusion, and how would it apply to a nation-state attack?
  2. 02

    Allergen control and labelling drive recalls in this category.

    WHY
    Cookie and cracker lines handle wheat, milk, egg, soy, peanuts, tree nuts, and sesame, with products frequently marketed to children. Labelling and packaging errors are among the most common recall causes.
    LOSS
    An allergen recall in a product consumed by children, with injury exposure and a recall scope determined by production and distribution traceability.
    ASK
    How is packaging verified against the formulation running on the line?
  3. 03

    Continuous ovens and packaging lines run at very high rates.

    WHY
    Mixing, forming, baking, cooling, and packaging run continuously at high throughput with product in process across the whole line. Oven and packaging control determine both quality and food safety.
    LOSS
    Product in process lost on an unplanned stop, plus off-specification product produced before detection, on lines running thousands of units per minute.
    ASK
    How many units per hour does your line produce, and how much is in process at any moment?
