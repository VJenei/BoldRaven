Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 337110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 337110, Wood Kitchen Cabinet and Countertop Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Every kitchen is cut to a specific set of measurements.

    WHY
    Cabinets and countertops are made to individual room dimensions from field measurements or templates, cut on CNC equipment from job-specific programmes. Parts cut to wrong dimensions fit no other job.
    LOSS
    Jobs cut to wrong dimensions, with material scrapped, installation crews sent home, and homeowners or builders left with unfinished kitchens while the job is remade.
    ASK
    How are field measurements captured and transferred into your cutting programmes?
  2. 02

    Formaldehyde emissions from composite panels are federally regulated.

    WHY
    Composite wood products are subject to EPA TSCA Title VI formaldehyde emission standards with certification, labelling, and records required through the supply chain. Records must be retained and produced on request.
    LOSS
    Inability to produce compliance records for panels used, which is a TSCA violation with penalties, and product that cannot be sold or must be recalled from distribution.
    ASK
    How do you retain formaldehyde compliance certifications for panel purchases?
  3. 03

    Installation is scheduled against builder and remodel dates.

    WHY
    Cabinets are delivered and installed at a point in a construction sequence, with countertop templating following cabinet installation and appliances, plumbing, and flooring behind that.
    LOSS
    Delivery missed with the construction sequence stalled, and builders assessing delay costs across trades that cannot proceed until the kitchen is installed.
    ASK
    What trades are scheduled behind your installation date?
