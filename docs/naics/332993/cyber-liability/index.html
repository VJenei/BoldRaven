Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 332993

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 332993, Ammunition (except Small Arms) Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    The plant makes military explosive ordnance under contract.

    WHY
    Large calibre ammunition, bombs, and warheads are produced under defence contracts with explosives licensing, security requirements, and technical data subject to export control. Contracts carry cybersecurity requirements as a condition of award.
    LOSS
    Compromise of contract technical data, which is an export control violation and a national security matter, with contract termination and debarment exposure in addition to investigation.
    ASK
    What ITAR controlled technical data does your operation hold?
  2. 02

    Explosive processing operations are lethal when control is lost.

    WHY
    Melt pour, pressing, and loading operations handle high explosives with automated equipment, temperature control, and remote operation. Loss of temperature or process control initiates the explosive.
    LOSS
    An explosion during processing with fatalities and destruction of production buildings, and an investigation examining whether process control and safety systems were functioning.
    ASK
    What automated controls hold explosive processing within safe limits?
  3. 03

    Lot traceability determines the scope of any ordnance problem.

    WHY
    Ordnance carries lot markings traceable to components, processing, and testing. When a round malfunctions in the field, the lot record determines what else must be withdrawn from service.
    LOSS
    A malfunction with casualties where lot records cannot bound the problem, requiring suspension of every lot from the period across deployed units.
    ASK
    How are lot records maintained and for how long after delivery?
