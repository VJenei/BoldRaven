Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 449110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 449110, Furniture Retailers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    In-store financing brings federal information security obligations.

    WHY
    Furniture retailers offering promotional financing arrange credit and are financial institutions under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, subject to the FTC Safeguards Rule. Since June 9, 2023 that requires a written security programme, a qualified individual, multi-factor authentication, encryption of customer information, and an incident response plan.
    LOSS
    FTC enforcement plus notification for credit applications containing Social Security numbers, income, and bank details, on a customer base that applied for financing at the counter.
    ASK
    Do you offer in-store financing, and do you have a written information security programme?
  2. 02

    Special orders are committed months ahead against a customer deposit.

    WHY
    Custom upholstery, finishes, and configurations are ordered to the customer's selection with deposits taken and lead times of eight to twenty weeks. The order record holds the fabric, finish, and configuration selected. Nothing about a custom piece can be reconstructed from memory.
    LOSS
    Lost order records mean furniture ordered wrong or not at all, with customers who paid deposits months earlier and no way to reproduce what they selected.
    ASK
    How many customer special orders with deposits are outstanding right now?
  3. 03

    Delivery scheduling is the last impression and the most common complaint.

    WHY
    Furniture retail schedules home delivery windows, coordinates warehouse pick and load, and manages assembly and damage claims. Customers take time off work for a delivery window. Routing and scheduling run entirely on software.
    LOSS
    Missed delivery windows across a full schedule, generating chargebacks, cancelled sales, and public complaints in a category where reviews drive most new customers.
    ASK
    How many home deliveries do you schedule in a typical week?
