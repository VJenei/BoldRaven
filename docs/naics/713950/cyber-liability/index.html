Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 713950

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 713950, Bowling Centers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Scoring, lane control, and point of sale run as one system.

    WHY
    Centres operate integrated scoring, lane management, reservations, and point of sale, with leagues, parties, and open play all booked and charged through it. Lanes cannot be assigned or scored without the system.
    LOSS
    The centre cannot operate lanes or take payment, with leagues and party bookings unable to proceed on scheduled nights that represent the reliable revenue base.
    ASK
    Could you run league night if your scoring and lane system were unavailable?
  2. 02

    League play is the revenue backbone and it is scheduled by season.

    WHY
    Leagues commit for full seasons with standings, handicaps, averages, and payouts tracked across weeks. Bowlers' averages carry across seasons and determine handicaps and prize eligibility.
    LOSS
    Loss of league standings and averages mid-season, which cannot be reconstructed and undermines prize distribution and handicap calculation for hundreds of bowlers.
    ASK
    How many weeks of league standings and averages are held in your system?
  3. 03

    Party bookings and customer data include minors.

    WHY
    Centres book birthday parties and youth leagues, holding parent contact details, payment methods, and children's names and ages, with events scheduled weeks ahead.
    LOSS
    Payment data exposure alongside children's booking details, and lost bookings for dated parties that families planned around.
    ASK
    How many dated party bookings are you holding at any time?
