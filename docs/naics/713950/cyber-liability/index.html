Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 713950

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 713950, Bowling Centers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Scoring and lane systems run the operation.

    • Lane assignment, scoring, and pin equipment run on connected systems.
    • Leagues and customers arrive expecting lanes to work.
    • Without the system you cannot assign lanes or score games.
    ASK
    How are your lane and scoring systems operated?
  2. 02

    Card payments run continuously at the desk and bar.

    • Point of sale terminals process cards through trading hours.
    • Card security requirements apply to every location.
    • A breach brings assessments, forensics, and bank claims.
    ASK
    How does your point of sale system handle card data?
  3. 03

    League bookings commit lanes for months.

    • Leagues book recurring slots across whole seasons.
    • League revenue is the base the business runs on.
    • A lost booking record disrupts every league night.
    ASK
    How are your league bookings and schedules recorded?
