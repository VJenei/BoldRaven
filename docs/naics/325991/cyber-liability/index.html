Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 325991

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 325991, Custom Compounding of Purchased Resins, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Compound formulations are customer specific and proprietary.

    • Additive packages and ratios are developed for each customer application.
    • Those formulations are held only in your systems.
    • Losing them means the compound cannot be reproduced.
    ASK
    Where are your compound formulations stored?
  2. 02

    Moulders set their process to your exact compound.

    • Melt flow, filler content, and additives determine moulding behaviour.
    • Customers qualify your compound into their production.
    • Off specification compound ruins their moulding run.
    ASK
    How are your compound specifications tested and recorded per lot?
  3. 03

    Contamination in compound follows into every part moulded.

    • Cross contamination between compounds affects colour and properties.
    • Changeover and cleaning records establish separation.
    • Contaminated compound produces defective parts across the customer's run.
    ASK
    How are your changeover and cleaning records documented between compounds?
