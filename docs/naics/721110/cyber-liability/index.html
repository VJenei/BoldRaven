Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 721110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 721110, Hotels (except Casino Hotels) and Motels, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Hotel guest databases have produced some of the largest breaches on record.

    WHY
    The Marriott and Starwood breach disclosed in 2018 involved the guest reservation database and affected hundreds of millions of records including passport numbers, resulting in regulatory fines and a multi-state settlement. Hotels hold identity, passport, payment, and stay history data at enormous scale and retain it for years.
    LOSS
    Notification across a guest base spanning countries, with passport data exposure engaging foreign regulators alongside US state law, card brand liability, and settlements that in this sector have reached the tens of millions.
    ASK
    How many years of guest records with passport and payment data do you retain?
  2. 02

    Property management and key systems control physical access to rooms.

    WHY
    The property management system drives reservations, check-in, folio charging, and electronic key encoding. Room keys are issued by that system, and door locks are managed through it. Guests cannot check in or enter rooms without it.
    LOSS
    A full hotel unable to check guests in or issue keys, with guests arriving after travel and no manual alternative at scale, plus unauthorised key issuance being a guest safety exposure.
    ASK
    How would you check in and issue room keys if your property management system failed?
  3. 03

    Franchise and brand standards impose security requirements.

    WHY
    Franchised properties operate under brand standards specifying systems, PCI compliance, and increasingly data security requirements, with the brand holding audit rights. Guest data flows to brand reservation and loyalty systems.
    LOSS
    Brand action for standards failures alongside the breach, and where guest data flowed to brand systems, questions of responsibility between franchisee and franchisor that are resolved in litigation.
    ASK
    What data security requirements does your franchise agreement impose?
