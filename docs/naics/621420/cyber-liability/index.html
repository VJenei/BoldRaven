Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 621420

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 621420, Outpatient Mental Health and Substance Abuse Centers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Substance use records carry protection stricter than HIPAA.

    WHY
    Federally assisted substance use disorder programmes are subject to 42 CFR Part 2, which restricts disclosure more tightly than HIPAA, generally requires specific patient consent, and exists because fear of disclosure prevents people from seeking treatment.
    LOSS
    Part 2 violations alongside HIPAA exposure, and patient harm including loss of employment, custody, and professional licences from disclosure of treatment that patients specifically sought under confidentiality protection.
    ASK
    Are your programme records subject to 42 CFR Part 2?
  2. 02

    Treatment interruption is a clinical emergency for some patients.

    WHY
    Medication assisted treatment for opioid use disorder requires continuity, with interruption causing withdrawal and relapse risk. Opioid treatment programmes dispense under DEA registration with dispensing records required for every dose.
    LOSS
    Patients unable to receive medication, with withdrawal, relapse, and overdose risk, and DEA obligations around dispensing records that cannot be met while systems are unavailable.
    ASK
    What happens to your medication assisted treatment patients if dispensing records are unavailable?
  3. 03

    Patient populations are among the most vulnerable to disclosure harm.

    WHY
    Patients include people in recovery, people under court order, and people whose employment or professional licences depend on their treatment remaining private. Records document diagnoses, drug screening results, and legal involvement.
    LOSS
    Direct personal harm to identified patients affecting employment, custody, and legal standing, with claims reflecting those consequences and a treatment population that will avoid care in future.
    ASK
    How many of your patients would face employment or legal consequences from disclosure?
