Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 315210

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 315210, Cut and Sew Apparel Contractors, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You produce other companies' branded product.

    WHY
    Contractors cut and sew to customer patterns and specifications, holding brand owners' designs, patterns, and grading before those styles reach market. Counterfeit production from contractor patterns is a recognised problem.
    LOSS
    Design and pattern disclosure enabling counterfeit production of customer styles, with brand owners holding the contractor responsible and terminating the relationship.
    ASK
    How many brand owners' patterns and designs are held on your systems?
  2. 02

    Labour compliance is audited by brand customers.

    WHY
    Brand owners audit contractors for wage, hour, safety, and worker documentation compliance as a condition of production, with social compliance audits examining payroll and time records directly.
    LOSS
    Failed social compliance audits where payroll and time records cannot be produced, with brands terminating production immediately given their own reputational exposure.
    ASK
    How do you produce payroll and hours records for a brand's social compliance audit?
  3. 03

    Production schedules are set by retail delivery dates.

    WHY
    Contractors produce against cut tickets tied to customer delivery commitments to retailers. The cutting, sewing, and shipping schedule leaves no slack before the retail date.
    LOSS
    Missed delivery causing the brand to miss its retail window, with cancelled orders and chargebacks passed down to the contractor who caused the delay.
    ASK
    How many days between your production completion and your customer's retail delivery date?
