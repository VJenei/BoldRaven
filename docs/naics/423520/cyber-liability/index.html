Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 423520

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 423520, Coal and Other Mineral and Ore Merchant Wholesalers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Trades settle on assay and quality data against large contract values.

    WHY
    Coal, ore, and mineral trades are priced on quality specifications with sampling and assay at load or discharge, provisional invoicing, and final settlement when assays are agreed. Contracts run into millions per cargo with penalty and rejection thresholds.
    LOSS
    Unresolvable provisional settlements across cargoes, and quality disputes decided against you where sampling and assay records are unavailable.
    ASK
    How are assay results received and matched to specific cargoes?
  2. 02

    Logistics chains are long, expensive, and coordinated electronically.

    WHY
    Product moves by rail, barge, and vessel through terminals with demurrage clocks running at every transfer. Nominations, bills of lading, and stockpile positions are managed in trading and logistics systems.
    LOSS
    Demurrage on vessels runs $20,000 to $50,000 per day, and rail car detention accumulates across hundreds of cars when loading and release instructions cannot be issued.
    ASK
    What is your daily demurrage exposure when a vessel or unit train is waiting?
  3. 03

    Trading positions and counterparty exposure need continuous visibility.

    WHY
    Merchants hold long and short physical positions, forward contracts, and sometimes financial hedges against a book that must be marked and monitored. Counterparty credit exposure is tracked per trade.
    LOSS
    An unmanaged position through a price move, or counterparty exposure exceeding limits without visibility, either of which can exceed annual profit in a single event.
    ASK
    How is your trading position and counterparty exposure monitored daily?
