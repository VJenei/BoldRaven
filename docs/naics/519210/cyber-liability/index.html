Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 519210

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 519210, Libraries and Archives, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Borrowing records are protected in most states.

    • Patron records show what individuals read and researched.
    • State law protects library records from disclosure.
    • That protection exists because the records reveal beliefs and interests.
    ASK
    What patron borrowing and account records do you hold?
  2. 02

    Digital collections and catalogues are the service.

    • Catalogue, circulation, and digital collections run on connected systems.
    • Patrons cannot find, borrow, or access anything without them.
    • Public access stops entirely, not partially.
    ASK
    What happens to patron access if your catalogue and circulation systems fail?
  3. 03

    Digitised collections may exist nowhere else.

    • Archives hold digitised material from unique physical originals.
    • Some originals are fragile or already lost.
    • Losing the digital copy loses the record permanently.
    ASK
    What digitised material exists only in your systems?
