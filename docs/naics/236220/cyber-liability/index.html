Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 236220

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 236220, Commercial and Institutional Building Construction, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    General contractors sit at the centre of the money and are targeted there.

    WHY
    A general contractor receives owner payments and disburses to dozens of subcontractors and suppliers every month, with lien waivers, joint check agreements, and bank detail changes flowing constantly by email. The volume of legitimate payment changes is exactly what makes a fraudulent one invisible.
    LOSS
    Diverted subcontractor payments of $100,000 to $2M per event. The subcontractor remains unpaid, files a lien against the owner's property, and the general contractor pays twice while in breach of the prime contract.
    ASK
    How many subcontractor payments does your office release in a typical month?
  2. 02

    The project runs on a cloud platform that holds everything.

    WHY
    Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud, or a similar platform holds drawings, submittals, RFIs, daily logs, photographs, safety records, and the schedule. Every party on the project works from it. Access is granted to subcontractors, architects, engineers, and the owner, which is a very large set of credentials.
    LOSS
    Loss of access to the current drawing set and RFI log stops field work, and the daily logs and photographs that defend against later delay and defect claims are the exact records that disappear.
    ASK
    How many external users have access to your project management platform?
  3. 03

    Bid and estimating data is competitively fatal if disclosed.

    WHY
    Estimating files hold your labour rates, productivity assumptions, markup, and subcontractor quotes across every pursuit. On public work, bids are submitted electronically ahead of a fixed letting time. Knowing a competitor's number is decisive.
    LOSS
    Lost work across every active pursuit, plus bid protest and potential procurement fraud investigation if leaked information touches a public letting.
    ASK
    Where are your estimating files and subcontractor quotes stored before a bid is submitted?
