Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541940

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541940, Veterinary Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Practice management systems run the clinic entirely.

    WHY
    Veterinary practices depend on practice management software for appointments, medical records, prescriptions, laboratory results, imaging, and billing. Veterinary chains and independent practices have both been affected by ransomware that halted clinical operations.
    LOSS
    Inability to access patient histories, medication records, and allergy information while animals present for treatment, which forces clinical decisions without records and delays care for emergency cases.
    ASK
    How would your veterinarians access a patient's medication history if the system were down?
  2. 02

    Controlled substance records are a DEA requirement.

    WHY
    Veterinary practices hold DEA registrations for controlled substances used in anaesthesia and pain management, with requirements for accurate records, biennial inventory, and reporting of losses. Diversion by staff is a recognised problem in veterinary settings.
    LOSS
    DEA action for records that cannot be reconciled, and where diversion is suspected the practice must demonstrate its controls, which requires records that exist and are complete.
    ASK
    How are your controlled substance logs maintained and reconciled?
  3. 03

    Client payment data and animal records accumulate for years.

    WHY
    Practices hold owner contact details, stored payment methods for recurring wellness plans, and complete medical histories for pets over their lifetimes. Wellness plan billing is automatic and continuous.
    LOSS
    Breach exposing client payment data across the practice, plus loss of medical histories that cannot be reconstructed and that clients rely on for continuity of care.
    ASK
    How many clients have payment methods stored for recurring wellness plans?
