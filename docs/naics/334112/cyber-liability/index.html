Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 334112

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 334112, Computer Storage Device Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Storage device firmware controls access to customer data.

    WHY
    Drives and storage systems run firmware handling encryption, wear levelling, and access. Firmware defects have exposed data on self-encrypting drives that customers believed were protected.
    LOSS
    An encryption or access defect in shipped firmware, with customers who relied on hardware encryption for regulatory compliance discovering their data was never protected, across every drive of the model.
    ASK
    Does your firmware implement encryption customers rely on for compliance?
  2. 02

    Enterprise storage is managed over networks by your software.

    WHY
    Storage arrays ship with management software, remote support connections, and call-home telemetry linking customer systems to the manufacturer. Those interfaces sit adjacent to the customer's primary data.
    LOSS
    A management interface vulnerability or compromise of support connections giving access to customer storage systems, which is direct access to their entire data estate.
    ASK
    What remote access do you retain to storage systems at customer sites?
  3. 03

    Storage failure destroys data customers cannot recreate.

    WHY
    Firmware defects in storage have caused simultaneous failures across drives with the same power-on hours, taking out multiple drives in a redundant array at once and destroying the data.
    LOSS
    A firmware defect causing correlated drive failures across arrays, with customers losing data that redundancy was supposed to protect and claims covering the data, not the drives.
    ASK
    How is firmware tested against long-duration and wear-related failure modes?
