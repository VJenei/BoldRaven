Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 212290

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 212290, Other Metal Ore Mining, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Processing runs continuously under automated control.

    • Crushing, grinding, and separation run as a controlled sequence.
    • Recovery depends on holding setpoints without interruption.
    • A stop loses material in process and takes days to restart.
    ASK
    Is your processing plant run by a central control system?
  2. 02

    Tailings and water systems are monitored for public safety.

    • Dam instrumentation, water balance, and discharge quality are monitored automatically.
    • Regulators require that monitoring and its records.
    • A failure without warning becomes a community event.
    ASK
    Is your tailings and water monitoring automated with alarms?
  3. 03

    Resource data sets the value of the asset.

    • Drill results, assays, and resource models determine what the deposit is worth.
    • That data is confidential until formally disclosed.
    • Theft or early release changes valuation and competitive position.
    ASK
    Where are your drill results and resource models stored?
