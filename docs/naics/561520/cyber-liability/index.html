Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561520

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561520, Tour Operators, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You take deposits far in advance for dated departures.

    WHY
    Tour operators collect deposits and final payments months ahead of departure for trips with fixed dates, then pay suppliers, airlines, and hotels on their own schedules. Client funds are held throughout, and in some jurisdictions trust or bonding requirements apply.
    LOSS
    Diverted client payments or supplier payments, leaving travellers without confirmed arrangements and the operator liable for both the funds and the trip, with insolvency exposure where client money is not recoverable.
    ASK
    What value of customer deposits are you holding for future departures?
  2. 02

    Traveller data includes passports, health information, and emergency contacts.

    WHY
    Tours require passport details for visas and manifests, dietary and medical information for accommodation, insurance details, and emergency contacts. Adventure and expedition operators collect medical fitness declarations.
    LOSS
    Breach exposing passport and medical data across the traveller base, with the medical component engaging health information sensitivity and notification obligations across many jurisdictions.
    ASK
    Do you collect medical or dietary information from travellers?
  3. 03

    Departures happen whether or not your systems work.

    WHY
    Trips depart on fixed dates with suppliers, guides, and transport booked. Manifests, rooming lists, and supplier confirmations must reach the ground operators before travellers arrive.
    LOSS
    Travellers arriving to arrangements that were never confirmed, with the operator paying twice for accommodation and transport and facing claims for the trip's value and the disruption.
    ASK
    How far ahead do supplier confirmations and manifests need to reach your ground operators?
