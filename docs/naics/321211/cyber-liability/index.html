Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 321211

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 321211, Hardwood Veneer and Plywood Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Formaldehyde emission standards apply to composite wood products.

    WHY
    Hardwood plywood is regulated under EPA formaldehyde emission standards requiring third-party certification, testing, labelling, and record retention. Non-compliant product cannot lawfully be sold or imported.
    LOSS
    Product that cannot be certified is unsaleable and, if distributed, subject to enforcement and recall, with EPA penalties and the certification body's involvement.
    ASK
    How do you maintain formaldehyde emission testing and certification records?
  2. 02

    Veneer and lay-up production is programme driven.

    WHY
    Veneer slicing, clipping, and composing, and panel lay-up follow specifications for grade, species, thickness, and construction. Panel construction determines structural and appearance performance.
    LOSS
    Panels produced to wrong construction or grade, unsaleable at specification and sold as downgrade, on production runs representing substantial veneer and press time.
    ASK
    How are panel construction specifications loaded into production?
  3. 03

    Hardwood species sourcing carries legality documentation.

    WHY
    The Lacey Act prohibits trade in illegally harvested timber and requires import declarations of species and country of harvest, with due care expected. Hardwood species from certain regions attract particular scrutiny.
    LOSS
    Lacey Act exposure including seizure and criminal penalties where species and origin cannot be documented, which in this industry has produced significant enforcement actions.
    ASK
    Can you document species and harvest origin for your veneer supply?
