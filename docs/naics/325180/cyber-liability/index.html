Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 325180

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 325180, Other Basic Inorganic Chemical Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Inorganic chemical processes involve acutely hazardous materials.

    WHY
    Manufacturing chlorine, acids, and other basic inorganics involves acutely toxic and corrosive materials in continuous processes with safety systems preventing release. Chlorine in particular is toxic at low concentration and travels with the wind.
    LOSS
    A toxic gas release requiring community evacuation and causing injury or death, which for chlorine and similar materials is a mass casualty exposure with regulatory and criminal consequences.
    ASK
    What acutely hazardous materials are handled in quantity at your facility?
  2. 02

    Risk management planning and process safety are federally required.

    WHY
    Facilities with threshold quantities of regulated substances must have risk management plans and process safety management programmes, with hazard analysis, mechanical integrity, and emergency response documentation reviewed by EPA and OSHA.
    LOSS
    Enforcement following an incident where process safety documentation is incomplete, with penalties, consent decrees, and criminal referral where knowing violations are found.
    ASK
    Which regulated substances at your site trigger risk management planning?
  3. 03

    Customers depend on continuous chemical supply.

    WHY
    Basic chemicals supply water treatment, manufacturing, and processing customers who consume continuously with limited storage. Water utilities in particular depend on chlorine and treatment chemicals with days of supply.
    LOSS
    Water utilities and manufacturers without treatment chemicals, which for drinking water systems creates a public health situation within days.
    ASK
    Do you supply water utilities or other customers with days of chemical storage?
