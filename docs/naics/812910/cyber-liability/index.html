Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 812910

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 812910, Pet Care (except Veterinary) Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold animals in your care and their owners' access details.

    WHY
    Boarding, daycare, grooming, and pet sitting operations hold pet health and behaviour records, vaccination proof, emergency veterinary authorisations, and for pet sitting, home addresses, alarm codes, and key arrangements for houses that will be empty.
    LOSS
    Exposure of home access details and dates when houses will be unoccupied, which is a burglary targeting resource, and animal health records unavailable when an emergency occurs in your care.
    ASK
    Do you hold home access codes for clients whose homes are empty while you care for their pets?
  2. 02

    Vaccination and health records prevent outbreaks in group settings.

    WHY
    Boarding and daycare require proof of vaccination and record behaviour, medication schedules, and dietary needs. Group settings transmit disease rapidly, and medication errors in boarding are a foreseeable harm.
    LOSS
    A disease outbreak among boarded animals where vaccination status could not be verified, or medication errors for animals whose schedules were unavailable, with claims from owners and reputational damage that is immediate in a community business.
    ASK
    How would staff verify a boarded animal's medications and vaccination status without records?
  3. 03

    Recurring bookings and stored payment drive the operation.

    WHY
    Daycare and grooming operate on recurring schedules with stored payment methods, and boarding is booked heavily around holidays with deposits taken months ahead.
    LOSS
    Holiday boarding bookings lost with owners arriving to no reservation at the point they are leaving for travel, plus stored payment data exposure across the client base.
    ASK
    How many holiday boarding reservations are you holding with deposits taken?
