Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 336110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 336110, Automobile and Light Duty Motor Vehicle Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Vehicles are connected computers that can be reached remotely.

    WHY
    Vehicles contain dozens of electronic control units, telematics units with cellular connections, and over the air update capability. Remote compromise of vehicle systems has been publicly demonstrated and led to a recall of 1.4 million vehicles.
    LOSS
    A remote vulnerability affecting vehicle control functions, with an NHTSA recall of every vehicle of the affected models, a software campaign to vehicles in customers' hands, and accident liability where the vehicles were in motion.
    ASK
    Can vehicle software be updated or accessed over a cellular connection?
  2. 02

    Assembly plants run just in time with no inventory buffer.

    WHY
    Vehicle assembly runs on sequenced delivery with hours of parts on hand. A plant produces a vehicle every minute and stopping it idles thousands of workers and hundreds of suppliers simultaneously.
    LOSS
    Plant systems unavailable with assembly stopped, at losses measured in millions per day per plant, and supplier shipments continuing to arrive with nowhere to go.
    ASK
    What does an hour of stopped assembly cost at one of your plants?
  3. 03

    Vehicles collect detailed data about their drivers.

    WHY
    Connected vehicles collect location, driving behaviour, and in-cabin data transmitted to manufacturer platforms, with state privacy laws and FTC attention focused on how that data is shared, particularly with insurers and data brokers.
    LOSS
    Driver data exposed or shared without proper consent, with state privacy enforcement, class actions across a large owner base, and FTC action where sharing practices were not disclosed.
    ASK
    What data do your vehicles transmit, and with whom is it shared?
