Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541219

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541219, Other Accounting Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Bookkeeping access means authority over client bank accounts.

    WHY
    Outsourced accounting and bookkeeping providers hold banking credentials, accounting system access, and often payment authority for client businesses. That access is granted broadly because it is necessary to do the work.
    LOSS
    Fraudulent payments made from client accounts using access held by the bookkeeper, where clients discover the loss only at reconciliation and hold the provider responsible for the access that enabled it.
    ASK
    How many client bank accounts can your staff access or initiate payments from?
  2. 02

    Client financial records are the basis for tax and lending.

    WHY
    Providers maintain the books that support tax returns, financial statements, and lender reporting. Clients frequently have no independent copy, and reconstructing years of transactions is expensive or impossible.
    LOSS
    Clients unable to file taxes, satisfy lender reporting, or defend an audit because their records are gone, with the resulting penalties and financing consequences claimed against the provider.
    ASK
    Do your clients hold an independent copy of the books you maintain for them?
  3. 03

    The FTC Safeguards Rule reaches accounting practices.

    WHY
    Practices providing tax preparation or financial services are financial institutions under GLBA and subject to the amended FTC Safeguards Rule, requiring a written programme, a qualified individual, multi-factor authentication, encryption, and incident response.
    LOSS
    FTC enforcement for programme deficiencies assessed independently of whether they caused the incident, at a firm size that typically has no dedicated compliance function.
    ASK
    Is multi-factor authentication enforced on every system holding client financial data?
