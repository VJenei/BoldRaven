Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 457110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 457110, Gasoline Stations with Convenience Stores, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Fuel dispensers are the most persistently attacked payment terminals in retail.

    WHY
    Card skimmers installed inside dispensers capture data continuously and are difficult to detect from outside the pump. The liability shift for automated fuel dispensers moved counterfeit fraud liability to merchants who had not upgraded to EMV, and unattended terminals remain a primary target because nobody is watching them.
    LOSS
    Fraud liability on every counterfeit transaction traced to your site, plus card brand assessments and forensic costs. A single compromised dispenser can expose thousands of cards before discovery.
    ASK
    How often are your dispensers physically inspected for skimming devices?
  2. 02

    Tank gauges have been repeatedly found exposed on the public internet.

    WHY
    Automatic tank gauges monitor fuel levels and detect leaks, and are connected for remote monitoring by the operator and the fuel supplier. Security researchers have repeatedly identified thousands of internet-exposed tank gauges across US fuel retail with little or no authentication.
    LOSS
    Manipulated level or alarm data can mask a leak, which becomes a reportable release with soil and groundwater remediation running into hundreds of thousands, or cause an overfill during delivery.
    ASK
    Is your tank gauge system reachable remotely, and by whom?
  3. 03

    Age-restricted sales are tested by deliberate compliance checks.

    WHY
    Tobacco, nicotine, and in many states alcohol sales require verified age at the register, with electronic identification scanning creating the record. FDA and state agencies conduct undercover buys and act on failures.
    LOSS
    Licence suspension removes the categories that drive traffic and margin, and repeated failures put the fuel and lottery licences at risk in the same action.
    ASK
    Does your point of sale scan and log identification for age-restricted sales?
