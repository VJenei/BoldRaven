Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 457110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 457110, Gasoline Stations with Convenience Stores, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Pumps are the most attacked payment terminal there is.

    • Unattended fuel dispensers are targeted with internal and wireless skimmers.
    • Card security requirements apply to every dispenser and register.
    • Skimming campaigns run across whole chains before detection.
    ASK
    How are your dispensers inspected and secured against skimming devices?
  2. 02

    Fuel systems are monitored for leaks automatically.

    • Tank monitoring and leak detection run continuously under regulation.
    • Records must be retained and produced to regulators.
    • An undetected leak contaminates soil and groundwater.
    ASK
    How is your tank monitoring and leak detection recorded and alarmed?
  3. 03

    Age restricted sales carry verification obligations.

    • Tobacco, alcohol, and other restricted sales require verification and records.
    • Compliance checks are conducted by regulators.
    • Violations affect the licences that let you sell.
    ASK
    How is age verification performed and recorded at your registers?
