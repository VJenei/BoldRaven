Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 423460

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 423460, Ophthalmic Goods Merchant Wholesalers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Prescription data is health information.

    WHY
    Optical laboratories and distributors receive patient prescriptions with names, prescriber details, and lens specifications in order to produce and ship eyewear. That is protected health information and typically makes the distributor a business associate under HIPAA.
    LOSS
    A breach requires patient notification and HHS reporting, with direct enforcement against the distributor as a business associate, on top of the optical practices' own obligations and their claim against you.
    ASK
    Do you receive patient names alongside prescriptions from optical practices?
  2. 02

    Lens production is made to order with no substitutable stock.

    WHY
    Prescription lenses are surfaced, coated, and edged to an individual prescription and frame, driven by job files through automated laboratory equipment. Every job is unique. There is no inventory to ship instead.
    LOSS
    A laboratory outage means every patient in the pipeline waits, with practices unable to give a delivery date and patients without glasses. Job files that are lost must be reordered from the practice.
    ASK
    How many patient jobs are in your production pipeline at any moment?
  3. 03

    Contact lenses and eyewear are regulated devices with verification duties.

    WHY
    Contact lenses are medical devices and sellers must verify prescriptions with the prescriber under the Contact Lens Rule, retaining records of verification for three years. FTC enforces this and has brought substantial penalty actions.
    LOSS
    Civil penalties for verification records that cannot be produced, assessed per violation across a large volume of transactions.
    ASK
    How do you record prescription verification for contact lens sales?
