Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 713920

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 713920, Skiing Facilities, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Lift access control and ticketing gate the whole mountain.

    WHY
    Access is controlled by RFID passes read at lift gates, with season passes, day tickets, and lesson bookings all linked to guest records. Guests arrive in a concentrated morning window and cannot ski without validated access.
    LOSS
    Guests unable to access lifts on a peak day, with refunds, crowd management issues at base areas, and a limited number of operating days in which revenue can be earned.
    ASK
    How would guests access lifts if your gate system could not validate passes?
  2. 02

    Snowmaking and lift operations are controlled industrial systems.

    WHY
    Snowmaking systems are centrally controlled across the mountain with water, air, and temperature management, and lifts operate under control systems with safety interlocks. Snowmaking windows are narrow and determined by weather.
    LOSS
    Missed snowmaking windows that cannot be recovered, leaving terrain unopenable for the season, and lift control failures that are a passenger safety exposure with state tramway authority involvement.
    ASK
    How many snowmaking hours would you lose if central control were unavailable during a cold window?
  3. 03

    Season pass programmes hold payment data and personal information.

    WHY
    Passes are sold months ahead with photographs, stored payment methods for payment plans, and family and minor records, with pass products frequently linked across multiple resorts.
    LOSS
    Exposure of passholder payment and photographic data across a large base, with multi-resort programmes multiplying the affected population beyond your own guests.
    ASK
    How many season passholders have payment methods stored with you?
