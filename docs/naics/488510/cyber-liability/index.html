Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 488510

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 488510, Freight Transportation Arrangement, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    A cyberattack on a major forwarder shut down global operations for weeks.

    WHY
    Expeditors International, one of the largest freight forwarders, shut down most operating systems globally after a February 2022 cyberattack, halting bookings, customs filings, and warehouse operations for roughly three weeks. The company reported substantial costs and was later sued by a customer for cargo and supply chain losses. Forwarders and brokers hold the data that makes freight move.
    LOSS
    Bookings, customs entries, and load tendering stop simultaneously while cargo sits at origin and destination. Customers claim demurrage, detention, and production losses that vastly exceed the forwarding margin.
    ASK
    How many customer shipments are in transit at any moment that depend on your systems?
  2. 02

    Brokers are the primary channel for cyber-enabled cargo theft.

    WHY
    Cargo theft losses across the United States and Canada reached nearly $725 million in 2025, with strategic theft close to a third of incidents. Criminals compromise broker load board credentials, clone motor carrier numbers, create fictitious carriers, and use double brokering to obtain loads. The broker's own account is the tool used to steal the freight.
    LOSS
    Loads tendered to fraudulent carriers through your compromised account, with the shipper claiming against you as the party that selected the carrier. Average theft values are now around $274,000.
    ASK
    How do you vet and authenticate a carrier before tendering a load?
  3. 03

    Customs filings carry direct legal responsibility.

    WHY
    Licensed customs brokers file entries on behalf of importers, with responsibility for classification, valuation, and duty declarations under a power of attorney. Filings are time-bound and errors carry penalties. Brokers hold importer records subject to recordkeeping requirements for five years.
    LOSS
    Missed or erroneous entries producing penalties and detained cargo, and inability to produce records during a customs audit where the broker is directly liable.
    ASK
    How many importers do you file entries for under power of attorney?
