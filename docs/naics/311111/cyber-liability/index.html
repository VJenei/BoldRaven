Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311111

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311111, Dog and Cat Food Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Pet food recalls kill animals and move fast.

    WHY
    Pet food is regulated by FDA with facility registration, preventive controls, and recall authority. Contamination events including aflatoxin, salmonella, and vitamin D excess have killed and sickened large numbers of animals, with recalls traced through production lot records.
    LOSS
    A recall that cannot be narrowed to affected lots covers all production in a period, multiplying the cost many times, plus wrongful death claims from owners whose pets died and reputational damage that persists for years.
    ASK
    Could you identify every customer and retailer that received a specific production lot?
  2. 02

    Formulation and batching are automated and nutritionally critical.

    WHY
    Production runs on automated batching systems weighing and blending ingredients including vitamin and mineral premixes to a formulation. Errors in micronutrient addition, particularly vitamin D, have caused mass animal illness and death.
    LOSS
    A batching or formulation error distributed across production before analytical testing detects it, with animals harmed and a recall that reaches every bag produced during the affected period.
    ASK
    How is micronutrient addition verified during batching?
  3. 03

    Ingredient traceability supports every recall decision.

    WHY
    Manufacturers must trace ingredients from supplier lot through production to finished goods, with FSMA preventive controls requiring supply chain programmes for hazards controlled by suppliers. Contamination frequently originates upstream.
    LOSS
    An ingredient contamination event where the affected finished lots cannot be identified, requiring recall of everything and preventing any claim against the supplier whose ingredient caused it.
    ASK
    Can you trace a supplier ingredient lot forward to every finished product it entered?
