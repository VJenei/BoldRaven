Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 334510

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 334510, Electromedical and Electrotherapeutic Apparatus Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Federal law requires cybersecurity in device submissions.

    • Devices with software must include vulnerability plans and a software bill of materials.
    • Regulators refuse submissions that lack them.
    • Market entry is blocked for products already funded.
    ASK
    Does your device meet the federal cybersecurity submission requirements?
  2. 02

    Device compromise reaches patients directly.

    • Devices deliver therapy and monitor patients over network connections.
    • Vulnerabilities in infusion, cardiac, and monitoring equipment have prompted safety communications.
    • Product is removed from clinical use until every unit is remediated.
    ASK
    Can device settings or therapy delivery be changed over a network connection?
  3. 03

    Devices carry patient data into your systems.

    • Connected devices transmit patient data to your platforms for monitoring and support.
    • That places you in a health data custodian relationship with notification duties.
    • Hospital customers face their own obligations and will pursue you.
    ASK
    Does patient data from your devices reach systems you operate?
