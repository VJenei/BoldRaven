Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 488190

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 488190, Other Support Activities for Air Transportation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Maintenance records determine whether aircraft can fly.

    • Work performed, parts installed, and inspections are documented per aircraft.
    • Regulators inspect those records directly.
    • An aircraft without records cannot legally return to service.
    ASK
    How are your maintenance and inspection records maintained per aircraft?
  2. 02

    Parts traceability establishes airworthiness.

    • Parts require traceability and airworthiness documentation.
    • Regulators treat undocumented parts as unapproved.
    • Parts installed without records must be removed.
    ASK
    What traceability documentation accompanies the parts you install?
  3. 03

    Aircraft on the ground cost the operator by the hour.

    • Operators lose revenue for every hour an aircraft is out of service.
    • Your scheduling and parts systems determine turnaround.
    • A systems failure extends every aircraft you have in work.
    ASK
    What does an hour of aircraft downtime cost your customers?
