Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 488190

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 488190, Other Support Activities for Air Transportation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Ground handling failures stop the airline, not just the handler.

    WHY
    Ground handlers load, fuel, de-ice, and turn aircraft against schedules measured in minutes, coordinated through airline systems and their own resource management platforms. Weight and balance data produced by the handler goes directly into the aircraft's load sheet.
    LOSS
    Delayed or cancelled departures across every carrier you handle, with airline claims for delay costs and the station contract at risk. Airlines move handling contracts quickly after a service failure.
    ASK
    How many airline departures depend on your staff and systems each day?
  2. 02

    Weight and balance and load data feed a flight safety calculation.

    WHY
    Handlers produce load plans, cargo and baggage weights, and distribution data that determine the aircraft's centre of gravity. Errors in this data have caused loss of control accidents. The load sheet is a legal document signed before departure.
    LOSS
    An out of trim aircraft is a hull and fatality exposure, and the handler's data is the origin of the error in any investigation.
    ASK
    How is load and weight data transmitted from your staff to the aircraft?
  3. 03

    Airside access and fuel operations are security regulated.

    WHY
    Staff require airport security credentials with background vetting, and fuelling operations involve quality control records, fuel quantity accounting, and hydrant system controls. TSA and airport security programmes govern access to the airside.
    LOSS
    Loss of airside access authorisation for your workforce halts operations entirely, and fuel quality documentation failures create an airworthiness exposure across every aircraft fuelled.
    ASK
    How many of your staff hold airport security credentials, and how is that tracked?
