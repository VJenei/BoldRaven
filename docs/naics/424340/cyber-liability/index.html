Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 424340

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 424340, Footwear Merchant Wholesalers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Footwear is sold in size runs and broken assortments are unsellable.

    WHY
    Footwear inventory is managed by style, colour, and size curve, with retailers ordering complete runs. Partial size availability makes an order commercially useless. Allocation across accounts during a shortage is a system-driven decision.
    LOSS
    Loss of size-level inventory visibility means orders shipped incomplete and returned, or allocation decisions that leave key accounts unable to sell.
    ASK
    How do you manage inventory at the size and colour level across your accounts?
  2. 02

    Retail customers impose vendor compliance requirements with automatic penalties.

    WHY
    Footwear ships to retailers under routing guides specifying carton labelling, packing, advance ship notices, and delivery windows, with chargebacks applied automatically for deviation. Drop-ship and marketplace channels require order and tracking data within hours.
    LOSS
    Chargebacks that can reach several percent of invoice value, plus scorecard damage leading to reduced orders in the following season.
    ASK
    What percentage of your invoice value is lost to vendor compliance chargebacks?
  3. 03

    Brand and counterfeit control depends on distribution records.

    WHY
    Branded footwear is heavily counterfeited and diverted, with brand owners policing authorised channels. Distribution records demonstrate goods went to authorised retailers, and are the evidence in an unauthorised distribution dispute.
    LOSS
    Loss of a brand distribution agreement over unprovable channel compliance, removing the product lines the business is built on.
    ASK
    Do your brand agreements restrict which channels you may sell into?
