Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 238290

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 238290, Other Building Equipment Contractors, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Access control and security system installers hold the keys to their clients' buildings.

    WHY
    This category includes access control, CCTV, elevator, and specialty equipment contractors. Access control installers hold credential databases, door schedules, and administrative access to systems that physically unlock buildings. Camera systems provide live views inside client premises.
    LOSS
    A compromise providing physical access to client facilities, or exposure of camera systems, is a security breach for the client and for everyone in their building. This is direct third-party liability, not a data inconvenience.
    ASK
    Do you retain administrative access to client access control or camera systems?
  2. 02

    Elevator and conveyance work is inspected and life safety critical.

    WHY
    Elevator installation and service is governed by ASME A17.1 with state inspection, testing records, and maintenance control programme documentation required to keep the unit in service. Controllers are software driven and increasingly connected for remote monitoring.
    LOSS
    A unit taken out of service for missing documentation, which in a high rise is a building-wide problem, and after an entrapment or injury the maintenance record is the first thing examined.
    ASK
    Where is your maintenance control programme documentation kept for each unit?
  3. 03

    Specialty equipment is commissioned to a configuration only you hold.

    WHY
    Installed equipment is programmed and commissioned to a site-specific configuration, and the backup of that configuration frequently exists only on the contractor's laptop or server. The client cannot reproduce it from the manual.
    LOSS
    After a controller failure the system cannot be restored to its commissioned state, requiring full recommissioning at the contractor's cost and leaving the client without the system in the meantime.
    ASK
    Where are the commissioned configuration backups for systems you have installed?
