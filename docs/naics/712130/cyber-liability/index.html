Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 712130

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 712130, Zoos and Botanical Gardens, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Animal life support systems run continuously.

    • Climate, water, filtration, and feeding systems run under automated control.
    • Animals die within hours when those systems fail.
    • Losses are irreplaceable and publicly visible.
    ASK
    Are your animal life support systems monitored with automatic alarms?
  2. 02

    Animal records support welfare and regulatory compliance.

    • Medical, breeding, and husbandry records are kept per animal.
    • Regulators and accrediting bodies inspect those records.
    • Animals without records cannot be transferred or bred.
    ASK
    How are your animal medical and husbandry records maintained?
  3. 03

    Visitor operations and membership run the revenue.

    • Ticketing, membership, and admissions run on connected systems.
    • Members and visitors hold accounts with payment details.
    • Every affected member must be notified.
    ASK
    How many member accounts with payment details do you hold?
