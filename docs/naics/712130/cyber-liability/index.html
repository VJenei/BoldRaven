Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 712130

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 712130, Zoos and Botanical Gardens, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Life support systems keep animals and collections alive.

    WHY
    Aquariums and zoos run life support systems controlling water quality, temperature, filtration, and oxygenation, with greenhouse climate control for botanical collections. Animals in aquatic exhibits have very short tolerance for life support failure.
    LOSS
    Mass mortality in aquatic or climate-controlled exhibits, involving animals that are frequently endangered, individually irreplaceable, and the subject of intense public attachment and media attention.
    ASK
    How long can your aquatic exhibits survive a life support control failure?
  2. 02

    Animal records support veterinary care and conservation breeding.

    WHY
    Institutions maintain individual animal records covering identification, medical history, genetics, and breeding recommendations, contributed to regional and international studbooks that manage genetic diversity of endangered species across institutions.
    LOSS
    Loss of individual animal medical and genetic records compromises veterinary care and breeding programme decisions for endangered species, with consequences extending beyond the institution to the wider conservation programme.
    ASK
    How are your animal medical and studbook records maintained and shared?
  3. 03

    Membership and admissions carry high transaction volume.

    WHY
    Institutions sell admissions, memberships with recurring billing, education programmes, and events, holding visitor and member payment and contact data at scale, plus children's data from camps and school programmes.
    LOSS
    Payment and member data exposure at scale, with children's programme data adding sensitivity, and notification costs falling on a non-profit budget.
    ASK
    Do you run children's camps or school programmes with minors' data?
