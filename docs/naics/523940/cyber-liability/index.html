Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 523940

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 523940, Portfolio Management and Investment Advice, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    SEC rules now require customer notification within 30 days.

    WHY
    Amendments to Regulation S-P require registered investment advisers and broker-dealers to adopt incident response programmes and to notify affected individuals as soon as practicable and no later than 30 days after becoming aware that sensitive customer information was or was likely accessed without authorisation, with obligations extending to service providers.
    LOSS
    SEC enforcement for late or absent notification and for an inadequate incident response programme, examined separately from the breach itself.
    ASK
    Does your incident response programme meet the 30-day notification requirement under Regulation S-P?
  2. 02

    Client wealth data attracts targeted social engineering.

    WHY
    Advisers hold client identity, account balances, holdings, banking details, and family and estate information for wealthy individuals. Attackers impersonate clients to request wire transfers, and impersonate advisers to instruct custodians. The adviser's email is the channel for both.
    LOSS
    Fraudulent distribution requests executed from client accounts, where the adviser is responsible for verifying authenticity and bears the loss when a fabricated instruction is acted on.
    ASK
    How do you authenticate a client request to move funds out of an account?
  3. 03

    Books and records obligations cover electronic communications.

    WHY
    Registered advisers must preserve required books and records including communications relating to advice, in specified formats and for defined periods, available for SEC examination. The Commission has pursued extensive enforcement over off-channel communications and recordkeeping failures.
    LOSS
    Recordkeeping violations that regulators pursue independently of any client harm, and which have produced very substantial penalties across the industry.
    ASK
    How are your electronic communications captured and preserved for examination?
