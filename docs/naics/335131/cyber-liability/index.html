Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 335131

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 335131, Residential Electric Lighting Fixture Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Lighting fixtures cause fires when they are not built to their listing.

    WHY
    Fixtures carry safety agency listings tied to specific wiring, insulation, thermal design, and components. Fixtures built outside the listed configuration overheat and ignite the ceiling assembly around them.
    LOSS
    Fixtures causing house fires, with a CPSC recall, property damage and injury claims from every affected home, and listing agency withdrawal of the certification.
    ASK
    How are listed component substitutions controlled in your build process?
  2. 02

    Smart lighting connects to home networks and cloud platforms.

    WHY
    Connected residential fixtures include wireless radios, mobile applications, and cloud accounts, sitting on home networks with credentials and usage data. Poorly secured devices are recruited into botnets.
    LOSS
    A vulnerability across deployed fixtures giving access to home networks or building a botnet from them, with FTC exposure for products marketed as secure and firmware remediation to devices in millions of homes.
    ASK
    Do your fixtures connect to home networks or a cloud platform you operate?
  3. 03

    Consumer products carry CPSC reporting duties.

    WHY
    Residential fixtures are consumer products with a 24 hour reporting obligation once information about a potential hazard is obtained, and recall reach depends on distribution records.
    LOSS
    A recall that must cover all production because distribution records cannot identify affected lots, with civil penalties added where reporting was late.
    ASK
    Could you identify which retailers received a specific production run?
