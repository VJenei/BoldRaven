Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 423140

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 423140, Motor Vehicle Parts (Used) Merchant Wholesalers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Federal reporting on salvage vehicles is mandatory and traceable.

    WHY
    Salvage yards and used parts operations must report vehicles obtained for salvage or dismantling to the National Motor Vehicle Title Information System monthly. Inventory systems hold VIN, part, and donor vehicle records. Stolen vehicle and chop shop investigations run directly against these records.
    LOSS
    Failure to report or produce NMVTIS records draws federal and state enforcement, and an inability to demonstrate lawful acquisition of donor vehicles raises the presumption of trafficking in stolen property.
    ASK
    How do you maintain and submit your NMVTIS salvage reporting?
  2. 02

    Parts are sold through interchange systems that must know what you have.

    WHY
    Used parts are sold nationally through interchange and locator networks that publish live inventory to body shops and other yards. Part grading, donor mileage, and interchange numbers determine whether the part fits and what it is worth. If you are not in the network you do not get the order.
    LOSS
    Loss of inventory visibility removes you from the locator network, sending orders to competing yards while your inventory sits.
    ASK
    Which parts locator networks publish your inventory?
  3. 03

    Environmental compliance is documented per vehicle.

    WHY
    Dismantling requires removal and documented handling of fuel, oil, refrigerant, mercury switches, and airbags, with refrigerant recovery by certified technicians and records retained. State permits impose fluid management and stormwater obligations verified from records.
    LOSS
    Environmental enforcement for handling that cannot be evidenced, plus stormwater violations assessed per day, on a site where contamination remediation cost can exceed the value of the business.
    ASK
    How do you record fluid and refrigerant recovery for each vehicle processed?
