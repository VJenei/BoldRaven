Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 926140

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 926140, Regulation of Agricultural Marketing and Commodities, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Market data and reports move commodity prices.

    WHY
    Agencies collect and publish market reports, production estimates, and inventory data that commodity markets trade on. Advance access to an unreleased report is directly tradeable and the release timing is controlled precisely for that reason.
    LOSS
    Premature or unauthorised access to unreleased market data creating trading advantage and market manipulation exposure, with the integrity of the reporting system called into question.
    ASK
    Do you hold market reports before their scheduled public release time?
  2. 02

    Licensing and inspection govern who may trade and handle commodities.

    WHY
    Agencies license warehouses, dealers, and handlers, conduct inspections and grading, and maintain bonding and financial responsibility records. Licensed status determines who may lawfully operate.
    LOSS
    Licensing and inspection records unavailable, with enforcement stalled and licensed entities unable to demonstrate standing, plus grading and inspection results that determine commodity value.
    ASK
    What licensing and inspection records does your agency maintain?
  3. 03

    Producer and business data is commercially sensitive.

    WHY
    Agencies hold producer production data, marketing records, and business financial information submitted for programme participation, licensing, and enforcement. Individual production data reveals commercial positions.
    LOSS
    Exposure of producer and handler commercial data, which reveals positions and volumes that participants submitted under statutory compulsion rather than voluntarily.
    ASK
    What commercial data do regulated entities submit to you under compulsion?
