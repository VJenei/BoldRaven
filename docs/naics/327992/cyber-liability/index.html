Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 327992

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 327992, Ground or Treated Mineral and Earth Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Crystalline silica exposure is regulated and consequential.

    WHY
    Grinding and processing mineral products generates respirable crystalline silica requiring engineering controls, exposure monitoring, and medical surveillance under OSHA rules. Silicosis is irreversible and progressive.
    LOSS
    Worker silicosis claims arising years after exposure, where the absence of exposure monitoring and medical surveillance records leaves the employer without a defence.
    ASK
    How are worker silica exposure monitoring and medical surveillance records maintained?
  2. 02

    Product specification determines usability in customer processes.

    WHY
    Ground minerals are supplied to specifications for particle size distribution, purity, and surface treatment, qualified into customer formulations for coatings, plastics, and rubber. Variation affects the customer's product properties.
    LOSS
    Off-specification mineral causing formulation failures at customers, with their batch losses and finished product problems claimed against the supplier.
    ASK
    How is particle size distribution and purity verified for each production lot?
  3. 03

    Combustible dust hazards exist in mineral processing.

    WHY
    Some mineral and earth products generate combustible dust in grinding and handling, requiring collection systems with explosion protection and housekeeping programmes. Dust collector explosions propagate through connected systems.
    LOSS
    A dust explosion propagating through collection ductwork, destroying processing areas with worker fatalities and OSHA enforcement.
    ASK
    Have your dust hazards been assessed and are collection systems explosion protected?
