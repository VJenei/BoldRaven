Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311221

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311221, Wet Corn Milling and Starch Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Wet milling is a continuous chemical process that cannot be stopped cleanly.

    WHY
    Steeping, separation, refining, and drying run continuously under distributed control, producing starch, sweeteners, oil, and feed products simultaneously. An unplanned stop leaves product in process across the whole plant.
    LOSS
    Days of lost production at a plant processing very large volumes, with product in process lost and restart taking further days, while customers on contract supply have no alternative source.
    ASK
    How long does a full plant restart take after an uncontrolled shutdown?
  2. 02

    Sweetener and starch customers run continuous operations too.

    WHY
    High fructose corn syrup and starch customers include beverage and food manufacturers running continuous lines with limited on-site storage, supplied by tank truck and rail on scheduled deliveries.
    LOSS
    Customer production lines stopping for lack of sweetener or starch, with claims reflecting their lost production rather than the value of the material not delivered.
    ASK
    How many days of ingredient inventory do your customers hold?
  3. 03

    Food safety and allergen control apply across multiple product streams.

    WHY
    Plants produce food, feed, and industrial products from the same corn stream, with segregation required between food and non-food grades and, for identity-preserved and non-GMO streams, additional segregation and documentation.
    LOSS
    Cross-contamination between product streams or loss of identity preservation documentation, with food grade product unable to be certified and premium identity-preserved product reverting to commodity value.
    ASK
    How is segregation between food, feed, and identity-preserved streams documented?
