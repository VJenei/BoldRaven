Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 331221

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 331221, Rolled Steel Shape Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Structural shapes are certified to properties buildings depend on.

    WHY
    Rolled shapes are produced to specifications with yield strength, tensile strength, and chemistry certified by mill test report. Structural engineers design assuming those properties, and code requires certified material.
    LOSS
    Material below specification fabricated into structures, which is a latent structural deficiency requiring assessment of every building containing steel from the affected production.
    ASK
    How is material certification tied to specific rolled production?
  2. 02

    Rolling mills run continuously with heavy equipment at speed.

    WHY
    Rolling runs heated material through stands at high speed with roll gap, tension, and temperature controlled together. Cobbles and equipment damage occur when control is lost, and reheat furnaces run continuously.
    LOSS
    Equipment damage and lost production, with a rolling mill representing very substantial capital and repair taking weeks while orders go unfilled.
    ASK
    How is mill control monitored, and what happens on a control failure at rolling speed?
  3. 03

    Fabricators cut to your dimensions on project schedules.

    WHY
    Steel shapes are supplied to fabricators cutting and assembling to project drawings with erection dates set. Rolling schedules for specific shapes may run only periodically.
    LOSS
    Fabricators without steel for projects with erection scheduled, and rolling schedules that may not repeat for weeks meaning the material cannot simply be reordered.
    ASK
    How often does your mill roll a specific shape and size?
