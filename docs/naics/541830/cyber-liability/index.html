Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541830

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541830, Media Buying Agencies, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You move very large sums of client money to media vendors.

    WHY
    Media buying agencies aggregate client budgets and pay publishers, networks, and platforms, frequently holding client funds in advance of payment. Monthly media payments run into millions and vendor payment details are handled routinely.
    LOSS
    Diverted media payments in the millions, where the media did not run, the publisher is unpaid, and the agency is liable to both the client and the vendor.
    ASK
    What is the value of media payments leaving your accounts in a typical month?
  2. 02

    Buying data reveals client strategy and negotiated rates.

    WHY
    Agencies hold negotiated rates with publishers and platforms across many clients, plus flight schedules and budget allocations. Rate structures are confidential to the publisher and the agency, and across clients they reveal comparative positions.
    LOSS
    Exposure of negotiated rates damages the agency's buying position across every publisher relationship simultaneously and creates client questions about differential treatment.
    ASK
    How would your publishers react to their negotiated rates becoming visible to each other?
  3. 03

    Campaigns run on schedules that cannot be rebooked.

    WHY
    Media placements are bought for specific dates, programmes, and inventory, frequently around events, seasons, or product launches. Inventory sold is gone and cannot be recreated later at the same value.
    LOSS
    Placements that fail to run or run incorrectly, where the media value is lost entirely and make-goods are unavailable for premium inventory.
    ASK
    How much of your client media is tied to dated events or seasonal inventory?
