Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 115111

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 115111, Cotton Ginning, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Every bale you gin is tracked by number and ownership.

    • Bale tags link module, grower, classing result, and warehouse receipt.
    • Ownership and payment for each bale follow that record.
    • A broken record chain stops payment for the grower and for you.
    ASK
    How are your bale numbers, gin records, and warehouse receipts recorded?
  2. 02

    The gin runs continuously through a short season.

    • Modules arrive faster than they can be ginned during peak season.
    • Gin control systems run the stands, driers, and presses.
    • A day down in season backs up every grower behind you.
    ASK
    How many days does your ginning season run, and what is a day of downtime worth?
  3. 03

    You hold grower money and settlement records.

    • Seed and lint proceeds are settled back to growers through your accounts.
    • Payments move by electronic transfer to many growers.
    • A payment fraud takes grower money, not just yours.
    ASK
    How are grower settlement payments authorised and released?
