Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 485210

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 485210, Interurban and Rural Bus Transportation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Ticketing is the entire revenue mechanism and it is online.

    WHY
    Intercity bus operators sell almost entirely through websites and apps with advance reservations, seat assignment, and stored payment methods. Passengers arrive at unstaffed stops with a mobile ticket. Without the reservation system there is no way to sell or validate travel.
    LOSS
    Ticket sales stop entirely while buses continue to run against fixed costs, and passengers holding tickets cannot be validated or accommodated.
    ASK
    What share of your tickets are sold and validated electronically?
  2. 02

    Rural routes serve passengers with no alternative transport.

    WHY
    Intercity and rural service is often the only public transport connecting small communities, used by people without cars for medical appointments, family, and work. Some routes operate under federal rural transportation programmes with service obligations attached.
    LOSS
    Service disruption strands passengers in communities with no alternative, and where federal programme funding carries service obligations, failure to operate affects the subsidy.
    ASK
    Do any of your routes operate under federal or state service obligations?
  3. 03

    Driver qualification and hours records are federally required.

    WHY
    Passenger carriers must maintain driver qualification files, drug and alcohol testing with Clearinghouse queries, hours of service records, and vehicle maintenance files. Passenger carrier crashes attract intense FMCSA and NTSB attention.
    LOSS
    An unsatisfactory safety rating removes operating authority. After a passenger carrier crash, the driver file and hours records determine the litigation outcome and the values involved are very large.
    ASK
    How are driver hours and qualification records maintained and retrieved?
