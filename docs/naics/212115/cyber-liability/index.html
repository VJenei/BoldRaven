Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 212115

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 212115, Underground Coal Mining, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Federal law requires electronic tracking and two-way communication with miners underground.

    WHY
    The MINER Act requires post-accident two-way wireless communication and electronic tracking of every person underground. Mines also run atmospheric monitoring systems for methane and carbon monoxide, belt monitoring, and main fan monitoring. These are legally required to operate, not optional conveniences.
    LOSS
    Loss of tracking or communication systems requires withdrawing miners and stopping production until service is restored. MSHA enforcement follows, and a section idled runs $100,000 or more per day.
    ASK
    Which communication and tracking systems do you rely on to keep miners underground legally?
  2. 02

    Ventilation and gas monitoring are the difference between a working mine and an explosion.

    WHY
    Main fans, booster fans, and the atmospheric monitoring system control and verify airflow that dilutes methane. Longwall and continuous miner sections, belt drives, and pumps are supervised through mine-wide control networks. Methane accumulation is the central hazard of underground coal.
    LOSS
    Loss of monitoring forces immediate withdrawal. A genuine ventilation control failure is a catastrophic life safety event with liability far beyond the property loss.
    ASK
    Is your atmospheric monitoring system connected to the same network as your business systems?
  3. 03

    The hoist is the only way people and coal leave the mine.

    WHY
    Shaft hoists run under programmable control with safety interlocks, overspeed protection, and monitoring. Hoisting is the single point of access for personnel, supplies, and in many mines the coal itself. Slope belts serve the same role and are similarly controlled.
    LOSS
    Hoist unavailability halts the mine completely. Repairs to hoist control and drive systems take weeks, and there is no alternative route for production.
    ASK
    How is your hoist control system isolated from your corporate network?
