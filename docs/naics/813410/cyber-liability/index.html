Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 813410

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 813410, Civic and Social Organizations, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Member records and dues billing hold personal and payment data.

    WHY
    Clubs and civic organisations hold member contact details, family information, dues payment methods, and event participation records, frequently accumulated over decades with no retention policy and administered by volunteers.
    LOSS
    Payment and member data exposure across a long-standing membership, with notification obligations falling on an organisation run largely by volunteers with no capacity to manage it.
    ASK
    Who administers your membership system, and are they a volunteer?
  2. 02

    Volunteer administration means weak and shared access.

    WHY
    Systems are administered by rotating volunteer officers with shared credentials, personal email accounts, and handovers that rarely include revoking previous access. Institutional continuity is poor by design.
    LOSS
    Access retained by former officers and shared credentials that no one can inventory, with a compromise impossible to scope because nobody knows who had access.
    ASK
    Could you list everyone who currently has access to your membership system?
  3. 03

    Events and facilities generate payment and participant data.

    WHY
    Organisations run events, facility rentals, and youth programmes, taking payment and holding participant records including, for youth activities, children's data and volunteer background checks.
    LOSS
    Payment data exposure alongside children's programme records, with safeguarding documentation that the organisation must produce if any allegation arises.
    ASK
    Do you run youth programmes with children's records and volunteer screening?
