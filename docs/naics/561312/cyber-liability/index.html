Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561312

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561312, Executive Search Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Confidential searches exist precisely because nobody can know.

    WHY
    Executive search firms run assignments to replace sitting executives, staff unannounced business units, and support transactions, with the existence of the search itself being the sensitive fact. Candidates are approached while employed elsewhere and would be damaged by disclosure.
    LOSS
    Disclosure that a search exists destabilises the client organisation and exposes candidates to dismissal by their current employers. Both the client and the candidates have direct claims and the firm's reputation for discretion is its only asset.
    ASK
    How many confidential searches are you currently running for roles that are still occupied?
  2. 02

    Candidate assessments contain frank and damaging material.

    WHY
    Search files include reference notes, interview assessments, compensation history, and candid evaluations of senior individuals, together with reasons candidates were rejected. That material is written on the assumption it will never be seen by the subject.
    LOSS
    Exposure of candid assessments causes personal and professional harm to named senior individuals, with defamation and privacy claims and the destruction of the trust the profession depends on.
    ASK
    What would happen if your written candidate assessments became visible to their subjects?
  3. 03

    Client relationships and off-limits agreements are the operating framework.

    WHY
    Search firms operate under off-limits arrangements restricting approaches to client employees, and hold client organisational charts, succession plans, and compensation structures. Those constraints and that data define the practice.
    LOSS
    Exposure of client succession plans and compensation structures, which is commercially damaging to the client and reveals internal judgements about named executives.
    ASK
    Do you hold client succession plans or organisational assessments?
