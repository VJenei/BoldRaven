Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541191

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541191, Title Abstract and Settlement Offices, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Settlement offices are the primary target of real estate wire fraud.

    WHY
    Settlement agents hold escrow funds and issue wiring instructions to buyers, sellers, and lenders. Criminals compromise email at the settlement office or a party to the transaction and substitute banking details at the moment of closing. Federal agencies have repeatedly identified this as one of the highest loss fraud categories in the country.
    LOSS
    Escrow diverted at closing, commonly $100,000 to several million, with the shortfall preventing the closing and claims from buyer, seller, lender, and underwriter simultaneously.
    ASK
    How are wiring instructions delivered to buyers, and how is a change verified?
  2. 02

    Escrow accounts hold other people's money and are audited.

    WHY
    Settlement offices hold escrow and trust funds under state regulation and underwriter requirements, with reconciliation, audit, and licensing obligations. Balances can be very large depending on closing volume on any given day.
    LOSS
    An escrow shortfall triggers immediate regulatory and underwriter intervention, licence exposure, and the obligation to restore funds belonging to consumers and lenders regardless of how the loss occurred.
    ASK
    What is the typical balance in your escrow account, and how frequently is it reconciled?
  3. 03

    Title files hold complete financial and identity records.

    WHY
    Settlement files contain Social Security numbers, bank account details, loan documents, deeds, and identity documents for buyers and sellers, retained for years under underwriter and state requirements.
    LOSS
    Breach notification across a file population whose records support complete identity and account takeover, with regulators in this sector having already taken action over exactly this exposure.
    ASK
    How many years of closed settlement files are retained in your systems?
