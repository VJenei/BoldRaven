Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 524128

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 524128, Other Direct Insurance (except Life, Health, and Medical) Carriers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Specialty carriers hold underwriting data that reveals client vulnerabilities.

    WHY
    Specialty lines underwriting collects detailed information about insured operations, security arrangements, loss history, and known exposures. For cyber, crime, and kidnap and ransom lines, that file describes exactly where the insured is weak.
    LOSS
    Exposure of underwriting files hands attackers a directory of insured organisations, their vulnerabilities, and their coverage limits, which has been observed as a targeting method against insureds.
    ASK
    Do your underwriting files describe your insureds' security controls and coverage limits?
  2. 02

    State insurance data security laws apply across every licensed state.

    WHY
    Insurance data security laws require a written information security programme, risk assessment, third-party oversight, and notification of cybersecurity events to the commissioner, typically within 72 hours. Carriers licensed in many states face parallel obligations.
    LOSS
    Simultaneous notification obligations across multiple states with differing requirements, followed by examination and potential restrictions on the licence.
    ASK
    How many state licences do you hold, and how would you notify them all within 72 hours?
  3. 03

    Policy administration failure means coverage cannot be confirmed.

    WHY
    Policy issuance, endorsement, and certificate production run through administration systems that insureds and their counterparties rely on to prove coverage exists. Contracts, licences, and closings frequently require evidence of insurance on demand.
    LOSS
    Insureds unable to obtain certificates or confirm coverage, blocking their own transactions and contracts, with claims against the carrier for the resulting commercial losses.
    ASK
    How would an insured obtain a certificate of insurance if your systems were unavailable?
