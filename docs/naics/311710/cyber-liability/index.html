Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311710

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311710, Seafood Product Preparation and Packaging, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Seafood HACCP is mandatory and specific.

    WHY
    FDA requires seafood processors to operate HACCP plans addressing hazards including histamine, pathogens, and parasites, with monitoring and verification records. Histamine in scombroid species and Listeria in ready to eat seafood are the principal hazards.
    LOSS
    Product without HACCP monitoring records treated as adulterated, plus histamine or Listeria reaching consumers with illness claims and FDA enforcement including import alerts for imported product.
    ASK
    How are your HACCP monitoring records captured for each production lot?
  2. 02

    All three seafood categories are on the Food Traceability List.

    WHY
    Finfish, crustaceans, and molluscan shellfish are covered by FSMA Section 204, requiring traceability lot codes and Key Data Elements at Critical Tracking Events with 24-hour record production to FDA, compliance date July 20, 2028. Molluscan shellfish also require NSSP tag retention.
    LOSS
    A traceback that stalls at the processor, expanding a recall or harvest area closure well beyond the implicated lots and placing the processor at the centre of the investigation.
    ASK
    Can you link a finished seafood lot back to harvest area and date?
  3. 03

    Species substitution and import monitoring are enforced.

    WHY
    Imported species covered by the Seafood Import Monitoring Program require harvest and chain of custody reporting, and species substitution is pursued as both a regulatory and a fraud matter. Records establish what the product actually is.
    LOSS
    Import shipments refused entry and mislabelling findings carrying regulatory penalties and civil fraud claims from customers who paid for a different species.
    ASK
    Do you process species covered by the Seafood Import Monitoring Program?
