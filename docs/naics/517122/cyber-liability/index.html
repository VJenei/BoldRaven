Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 517122

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 517122, Agents for Wireless Telecommunications Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Retail agents are where SIM swap fraud is actually executed.

    WHY
    Authorised dealers and agents perform activations, upgrades, and SIM changes in stores using carrier systems. Insider-assisted and socially engineered SIM swaps at retail locations are a documented and persistent problem, which is why the FCC imposed authentication requirements on SIM changes and port-outs.
    LOSS
    A fraudulent SIM swap performed at your location leads to the customer's financial accounts being drained, with claims against the agent and immediate termination of the carrier agreement.
    ASK
    What authentication is required before your staff process a SIM change?
  2. 02

    Agents hold credential access to carrier customer systems.

    WHY
    Store staff use carrier-provided credentials to view and modify customer accounts, including personal details, plan information, and device changes. Turnover in retail is high and credential hygiene is often weak.
    LOSS
    Compromised or misused agent credentials expose carrier customer data, making the agent the source of a breach affecting the carrier's customers rather than its own.
    ASK
    How are carrier system credentials issued and revoked as staff join and leave?
  3. 03

    Commission and activation records determine whether you get paid.

    WHY
    Agents are paid commissions on activations, upgrades, and accessories, subject to chargeback if a customer cancels within a defined period. Reconciliation against carrier statements depends on your own activation records.
    LOSS
    Unrecoverable commissions and unverifiable chargebacks, where the carrier's statement becomes the only version of events and disputes are resolved against you.
    ASK
    How do you reconcile carrier commission statements against your own activation records?
