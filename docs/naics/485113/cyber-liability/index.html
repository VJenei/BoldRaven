Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 485113

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 485113, Bus and Other Motor Vehicle Transit Systems, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Dispatch and vehicle location systems run the entire service.

    WHY
    Bus transit operates through computer-aided dispatch and automatic vehicle location that assigns operators to runs, tracks vehicles, drives passenger information displays, and manages detours in real time. Operators receive assignments through these systems.
    LOSS
    Service reverts to fixed timetables with no real-time management, and riders lose arrival information entirely. Missed connections and stranded riders generate immediate political consequences for a public agency.
    ASK
    How are operator assignments and vehicle locations managed during service?
  2. 02

    Fare collection failure means carrying passengers for free.

    WHY
    Fare boxes, smart cards, and mobile ticketing are networked to a central system for validation and revenue accounting. When validation is unavailable the practical choice is to carry riders free rather than suspend service.
    LOSS
    Fare revenue lost for the duration with no retroactive recovery, on a service where farebox recovery is already a small fraction of operating cost and every dollar is budgeted.
    ASK
    What is your daily farebox revenue, and could you collect it manually?
  3. 03

    Paratransit obligations are legally enforceable.

    WHY
    Transit agencies must provide complementary paratransit service under the Americans with Disabilities Act, scheduled through reservation and routing systems holding rider disability information and trip histories. Denials and missed trips are tracked and reportable.
    LOSS
    Missed paratransit trips are ADA compliance failures affecting riders who have no alternative, with FTA oversight and civil rights complaints following.
    ASK
    How are paratransit trips reserved and scheduled?
