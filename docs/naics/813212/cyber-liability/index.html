Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 813212

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 813212, Voluntary Health Organizations, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Donor and beneficiary records are sensitive.

    • Records carry donor giving history and beneficiary health information.
    • Beneficiary records may reveal diagnoses.
    • Every affected donor and beneficiary must be notified.
    ASK
    What donor and beneficiary records do you hold?
  2. 02

    Donation processing runs continuously.

    • Online and recurring donations are processed through connected systems.
    • Campaigns drive concentrated giving periods.
    • A processing failure during a campaign loses the giving entirely.
    ASK
    How are your online and recurring donations processed?
  3. 03

    Fraudulent appeals damage trust permanently.

    • Fraudsters impersonate charities to solicit donations.
    • Donors give believing it reaches your cause.
    • The reputational damage falls on you, not the fraudster.
    ASK
    How would donors verify that an appeal genuinely comes from you?
