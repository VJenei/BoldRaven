Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 327390

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 327390, Other Concrete Product Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Precast structural elements are engineered for specific buildings.

    WHY
    Precast beams, panels, and structural elements are engineered and cast for a specific project with reinforcement, connections, and dimensions from approved shop drawings. Each piece is unique to its location in the structure.
    LOSS
    Elements cast to wrong drawings that do not fit or do not carry design loads, requiring recasting with curing time and crane schedules missed, and structural risk if installed.
    ASK
    How are approved shop drawings released into your casting operation?
  2. 02

    Erection sequences depend on delivery in the right order.

    WHY
    Precast is erected in sequence with cranes scheduled, and pieces must arrive in erection order. Storage at site is limited and out of sequence delivery stops the crane.
    LOSS
    Erection stopped with crane and crew standing, at costs of thousands per hour, and a construction schedule where precast erection is on the critical path.
    ASK
    What does a crane and erection crew cost per day on your projects?
  3. 03

    Prestressing and reinforcement determine structural capacity.

    WHY
    Prestressed elements are tensioned to specified forces with release timing determined by concrete strength, all recorded. Errors in tensioning or release produce elements that do not perform as designed.
    LOSS
    Elements with incorrect prestress installed in structures, which is a latent structural deficiency requiring assessment of every piece from the affected production.
    ASK
    How are prestressing forces and release strengths recorded per casting bed?
