Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 335312

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 335312, Motor and Generator Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Motors drive processes that stop when they fail.

    WHY
    Electric motors drive pumps, fans, and machinery across industry, with failures stopping the process immediately. Large motors are built to order with long lead times and no shelf spare.
    LOSS
    Motor failures across an installed base or production halted with customers unable to obtain replacements, and process plants stopped for the months a replacement motor takes to build.
    ASK
    What is the lead time on your larger built-to-order motors?
  2. 02

    Motors in hazardous locations must not become ignition sources.

    WHY
    Motors for classified hazardous areas carry explosion-proof or increased safety certifications depending on enclosure construction, clearances, and temperature limits verified in the build.
    LOSS
    Motors built outside certified construction installed in hazardous areas, igniting a flammable atmosphere, with an explosion and an investigation examining the certification and build records.
    ASK
    How are hazardous location certified constructions controlled in your build process?
  3. 03

    Efficiency standards are federally mandated by motor type.

    WHY
    Department of Energy efficiency standards prescribe minimum efficiency for electric motors by type and rating, with compliance certification and enforcement. Non-compliant motors cannot be lawfully sold.
    LOSS
    Motors manufactured or sold below required efficiency, which is a DOE violation with penalties and inventory that cannot be sold, plus certification records that must be produced.
    ASK
    How do you certify and track efficiency compliance by motor model?
