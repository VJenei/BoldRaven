Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 335929

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 335929, Other Communication and Energy Wire Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Wire and cable carry power and their listings prevent fires.

    WHY
    Building wire and cable carry safety listings covering insulation, temperature rating, and flame spread. Cable that does not meet its listing overheats in walls and plenums and spreads fire through the building.
    LOSS
    Non-conforming cable installed in buildings, which is a latent fire hazard requiring identification and replacement of cable already inside walls and ceilings, with listing agency and recall involvement.
    ASK
    How are listing test results linked to specific production runs?
  2. 02

    Counterfeit and substandard cable is a known industry problem.

    WHY
    Cable is counterfeited with false listing marks and undersized conductors, and industry programmes exist specifically to identify it. Manufacturers whose marks are copied face the failures and the reputational damage.
    LOSS
    Counterfeit cable bearing your listing marks causing fires, with investigation, brand damage, and the cost of tracing and acting against the counterfeit supply.
    ASK
    How can genuine cable be distinguished from counterfeits carrying your marks?
  3. 03

    Copper content makes production data and inventory valuable.

    WHY
    Cable production consumes very large quantities of copper priced daily, with purchasing, hedging, and inventory managed through systems that carry substantial financial exposure.
    LOSS
    Fraudulent payment or purchasing transactions in a business where single copper purchases run into millions, and inventory records manipulated to conceal diversion.
    ASK
    What is the value of a typical copper purchase, and how is payment authorised?
