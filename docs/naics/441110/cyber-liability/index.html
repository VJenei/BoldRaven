Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 441110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 441110, New Car Dealers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    A single attack on a dealer software vendor shut down roughly 15,000 dealerships.

    WHY
    In June 2024 ransomware against CDK Global, the dominant dealer management system provider, took down sales, service, parts, finance, and payroll functions across approximately 15,000 North American dealerships for about three weeks. Dealers reverted to paper and many could not deliver vehicles or process repair orders at all. The Anderson Economic Group estimated direct losses to dealers of roughly $1 billion.
    LOSS
    A dealership cannot sell, service, or bill without its DMS. Three weeks of degraded operation at a mid-size store is several hundred thousand dollars of gross profit, and the vendor's insurance does not cover your loss.
    ASK
    Which dealer management system do you run, and how long could the store operate without it?
  2. 02

    Federal law treats your dealership as a financial institution.

    WHY
    Because dealers arrange financing and leasing, they are financial institutions under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and are subject to the FTC Safeguards Rule. Full compliance has been required since June 9, 2023, including a written information security programme, a designated qualified individual, multi-factor authentication, encryption of customer information, and a written incident response plan. Since May 2024 breaches affecting 500 or more consumers must be reported to the FTC.
    LOSS
    FTC enforcement for a programme that cannot be evidenced, on top of the breach itself. This is a regulatory obligation most dealers do not realise they carry until an examiner or a plaintiff's lawyer raises it.
    ASK
    Do you have a written information security programme and a designated qualified individual under the FTC Safeguards Rule?
  3. 03

    Every credit application in your system is a complete identity.

    WHY
    Deal jackets hold driver licence images, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, income, employment, bank details, and full credit reports for every customer and co-signer, retained for years. A dealership accumulates thousands of complete identities and pulls credit bureau data directly.
    LOSS
    A breach of the customer file is a mass identity theft event with notification in every state of residence, class litigation, and credit bureau access agreements at risk.
    ASK
    How many years of customer credit applications are stored in your system?
