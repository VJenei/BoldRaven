Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 532282

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 532282, Video Tape and Disc Rental, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Federal law provides statutory damages for disclosing video rental records.

    WHY
    The Video Privacy Protection Act specifically prohibits knowing disclosure of personally identifiable information about a consumer's video rentals or purchases, and provides liquidated damages of $2,500 per violation. It is one of very few privacy statutes with per-person statutory damages and no requirement to prove actual harm.
    LOSS
    Statutory damages multiplied across every customer whose rental history was disclosed, which converts a modest customer base into a very large claim without any proof of individual harm.
    ASK
    How many customers' rental histories are stored in your system?
  2. 02

    Rental histories reveal personal information customers assume is private.

    WHY
    Viewing histories indicate interests, beliefs, and sexuality, which is precisely why Congress legislated specifically to protect these records. Customer files link that history to name, address, and payment method.
    LOSS
    Personal harm to customers from disclosure of viewing history, combined with the statutory damages framework that makes this category of data uniquely expensive to lose.
    ASK
    How long do you retain individual customer rental histories?
  3. 03

    The point of sale system runs the entire operation.

    WHY
    Inventory, membership, rental terms, due dates, late fees, and payment all run through a single system. Physical media rental depends on knowing what is out, with whom, and when it is due.
    LOSS
    Inability to check out or receive returns, and loss of the record of what inventory is currently in customers' hands, which is unrecoverable without customer cooperation.
    ASK
    How would you know what inventory is currently rented out without your system?
