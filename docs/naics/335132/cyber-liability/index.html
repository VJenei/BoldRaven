Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 335132

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 335132, Commercial, Industrial, and Institutional Electric Lighting Fixture Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Emergency lighting is what gets people out.

    • Emergency lighting and exit signs must operate when power fails.
    • Listings, battery duration, and testing are mandated by fire code.
    • Failure happens during the evacuation it exists for.
    ASK
    Do you manufacture emergency or exit lighting with listed battery duration?
  2. 02

    Networked lighting controls sit inside building networks.

    • Lighting control systems include sensors, gateways, and building network connections.
    • You supply and sometimes support that software.
    • You become the identified path into the customer's corporate network.
    ASK
    Do your lighting controls connect to customer building networks?
  3. 03

    Fixtures are made for specific projects and dates.

    • Configurations, voltages, and finishes are specified per project.
    • Delivery is sequenced to ceiling installation.
    • Wrong or late fixtures stall ceiling and electrical work.
    ASK
    How are project configurations verified before fixtures are released to build?
