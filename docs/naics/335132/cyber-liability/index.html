Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 335132

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 335132, Commercial, Industrial, and Institutional Electric Lighting Fixture Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Emergency and exit lighting is life safety equipment.

    WHY
    Emergency lighting and exit signs must operate during power failure to allow occupants to evacuate, with listings, battery duration requirements, and periodic testing mandated by fire code.
    LOSS
    Emergency lighting failing during an evacuation, with occupants unable to find exits in a fire, and the manufacturer's listing and production records examined after fatalities.
    ASK
    Do you manufacture emergency or exit lighting with listed battery duration?
  2. 02

    Networked lighting controls sit inside building networks.

    WHY
    Commercial lighting increasingly uses networked control systems with sensors, gateways, and building network connections, supplied and sometimes supported by the fixture manufacturer.
    LOSS
    Lighting control systems used as an entry point into customer building and corporate networks, with the manufacturer identified as the path and remediation required across every installation.
    ASK
    Do your lighting controls connect to customer building networks?
  3. 03

    Fixtures are engineered and scheduled for specific projects.

    WHY
    Commercial fixtures are made to project schedules with specified configurations, voltages, and finishes, delivered to construction sites on a sequence tied to ceiling installation.
    LOSS
    Wrong configuration or delayed fixtures at construction sites, with ceiling and electrical work stalled and the manufacturer exposed to delay claims on the project schedule.
    ASK
    How are project configurations verified before fixtures are released to build?
