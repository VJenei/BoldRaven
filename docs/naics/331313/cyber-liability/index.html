Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 331313

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 331313, Alumina Refining and Primary Aluminum Production, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Reduction cells cannot be interrupted without freezing.

    WHY
    Aluminium smelting runs electrolytic cells continuously at high current, with molten metal and cryolite bath maintained by the current itself. Power interruption of a few hours freezes the cells, and restarting a frozen potline requires rebuilding cells individually.
    LOSS
    A frozen potline requiring cell rebuilds costing tens of millions and taking months, with the smelter producing nothing throughout and the fixed cost base continuing.
    ASK
    How long can your cells survive a power or control interruption before freezing?
  2. 02

    Molten metal and caustic processes are hazardous continuously.

    WHY
    Smelting handles molten aluminium and cryolite, and alumina refining uses hot caustic under pressure. Molten metal water contact produces explosions and caustic releases cause severe burns.
    LOSS
    A molten metal explosion or caustic release with worker fatalities, plus equipment destruction in the affected area and investigation of the controls that should have prevented it.
    ASK
    How are molten metal handling areas protected against water ingress?
  3. 03

    Power supply is the largest cost and is contracted.

    WHY
    Smelters consume enormous quantities of electricity under long-term supply agreements with interruptibility provisions. Power management and load control are integrated with production and are financially decisive.
    LOSS
    Inability to manage load against contracted terms, with demand charges and interruption obligations mismanaged, on a cost that represents the largest single input to the operation.
    ASK
    How is your power load managed against your supply agreement terms?
