Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 321992

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 321992, Prefabricated Wood Building Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Buildings are engineered for a specific site and loads.

    • Designs account for wind, snow, and seismic loads at the site.
    • Engineering files drive automated cutting and fabrication.
    • A building fabricated to wrong loads is under-designed where it stands.
    ASK
    How are site specific engineering designs released into fabrication?
  2. 02

    Components must arrive complete and in erection order.

    • Packages are fabricated for one building and erected in sequence.
    • Crews and cranes are scheduled to the delivery date.
    • Missing components stop erection entirely.
    ASK
    What does an erection crew and crane cost per day on your projects?
  3. 03

    Fabrication runs from digital data to automated equipment.

    • Cutting, drilling, and assembly run from design files.
    • Errors are built into material that cannot be uncut.
    • Remake delays the project by the full fabrication lead time.
    ASK
    How does engineering data reach your fabrication equipment?
