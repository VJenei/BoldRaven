Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 424930

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 424930, Flower, Nursery Stock, and Florists' Supplies Merchant Wholesalers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Fresh flowers have days of life and the holidays do not move.

    WHY
    Cut flowers move from farm to wholesaler to florist within days, largely imported by air with cold chain maintained throughout. Valentine's Day and Mother's Day concentrate enormous volume into fixed dates that cannot be shifted.
    LOSS
    Product that misses its window is a total loss, and a systems failure in the days before a major holiday destroys the two most important weeks of the year.
    ASK
    What share of your annual volume moves in the two weeks around Valentine's Day and Mother's Day?
  2. 02

    Imported plant material requires inspection and phytosanitary clearance.

    WHY
    Cut flowers and nursery stock are imported under APHIS inspection with phytosanitary certificates, and shipments are held or destroyed for pest findings. Documentation must match the shipment exactly and clear before product moves inland.
    LOSS
    Shipments held at the port of entry while paperwork is resolved, on product that is losing vase life every hour it sits.
    ASK
    How much of your product is imported and subject to inspection at entry?
  3. 03

    Cold chain determines how long the product lasts for the customer.

    WHY
    Vase life depends on unbroken cold chain from farm through wholesale to the florist. Coolers and refrigerated delivery are monitored, and every excursion shortens the life the retail customer gets.
    LOSS
    Product that fails prematurely at the retail florist generates credits and lost accounts, and a cooler control failure at the wholesale level condemns everything in it.
    ASK
    How is your cooler temperature monitored and alarmed?
