Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 212220

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 212220, Gold Ore and Silver Ore Mining, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Poured metal is portable, valuable, and tracked only by records.

    • Pour weights, assays, and shipment records establish how much metal exists.
    • Inventory records are the only check on what leaves the refinery.
    • Altered records hide theft until a physical count months later.
    ASK
    How is poured metal weighed, recorded, and reconciled against shipments?
  2. 02

    Cyanide circuits are held safe by automated control.

    • Leach, tailings, and detoxification systems run on continuous monitoring and control.
    • Loss of control means a release into water or air.
    • A cyanide release is a regulatory and community event.
    ASK
    Are your leach and tailings systems run by automated monitoring and controls?
  3. 03

    Reserve and exploration data determines the company's value.

    • Drill results, assays, and resource models are the basis of every valuation.
    • That data moves markets when it is disclosed.
    • Theft or early release affects share price and lease positions.
    ASK
    Where are your drill results and resource models stored?
