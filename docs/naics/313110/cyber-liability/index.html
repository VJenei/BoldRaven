Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 313110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 313110, Fiber, Yarn, and Thread Mills, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Spinning runs continuously and stopping breaks every end.

    WHY
    Spinning frames run thousands of spindles continuously, and an unplanned stop breaks ends across the machine requiring extensive piecing to restart. Throughput depends on continuous running with minimal interruption.
    LOSS
    Extended downtime with restart labour across hundreds of machines, and production commitments to weaving and knitting customers unmet on lead times that are already tight.
    ASK
    How long does it take to restart a spinning frame after an unplanned stop?
  2. 02

    Yarn specification determines everything downstream.

    WHY
    Yarn is produced to count, twist, strength, and evenness specifications, tested continuously. Downstream weavers and knitters set their machines to those parameters, and out of specification yarn causes breakage and fabric defects.
    LOSS
    Off-specification yarn causing production problems and fabric defects at customers, with claims for their production losses far exceeding the value of the yarn supplied.
    ASK
    How is yarn quality tested and recorded against each production lot?
  3. 03

    Fibre sourcing carries forced labour compliance obligations.

    WHY
    Cotton and other fibre supply chains are subject to import enforcement over forced labour, with shipments detained and the importer required to produce documentation tracing to the source. Isotopic testing is used to verify origin claims.
    LOSS
    Detained shipments and inability to demonstrate fibre origin, with goods excluded from entry and customers refusing material that cannot be documented.
    ASK
    Can you document the origin of the fibre in a specific yarn lot?
