Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333998

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333998, All Other Miscellaneous General Purpose Machinery Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Machines ship with control systems you write and support.

    WHY
    General purpose machinery includes PLCs, drives, and HMIs programmed by the builder, frequently with remote access for commissioning and support that reaches into customer plant networks.
    LOSS
    Remote support access used to reach customer networks, with production stopped at multiple plants and the manufacturer identified as the common entry point.
    ASK
    How many customer sites do you retain remote access to?
  2. 02

    Machine safety functions prevent operator injury.

    WHY
    Guarding, interlocks, and safety-rated control functions stop machine motion when a person is exposed. Those functions are designed and validated by the machine builder and documented for the customer.
    LOSS
    A safety function defect permitting motion with a person exposed, causing serious injury, with the builder's validation records examined and every machine of the design affected.
    ASK
    How are safety-rated control functions validated and documented per machine?
  3. 03

    Designs and control code exist only in your systems.

    WHY
    Machines are engineered to order with mechanical designs, electrical schematics, and control code produced once for that machine. Customers depend on the builder for support over the machine's life.
    LOSS
    Loss of design and control code with machines in build stopped and installed machines unsupportable, leaving customers with equipment nobody can modify or repair.
    ASK
    How many machine designs and control programmes exist only on your systems?
