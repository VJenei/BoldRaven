Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 323111

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 323111, Commercial Printing (except Screen and Books), needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold customers' confidential documents before publication.

    WHY
    Commercial printers receive financial statements, annual reports, legal notices, marketing campaigns, and product launch materials before they are public. Financial printing in particular involves material non-public information.
    LOSS
    Premature disclosure of financial results or unannounced campaigns, with securities implications for listed customers and contractual liability to every affected client.
    ASK
    Do you print financial or legal documents before they become public?
  2. 02

    Variable data printing holds customer mailing lists.

    WHY
    Direct mail and personalised printing requires customer databases with names, addresses, and frequently account, health, or financial information used for personalisation. Those individuals have no relationship with the printer.
    LOSS
    Breach exposing customer mailing databases containing account and personal data, with notification obligations flowing through the client and, where health or financial data is involved, sector-specific regulation.
    ASK
    What customer data is loaded into your variable data printing systems?
  3. 03

    Press deadlines are set by mail dates and event dates.

    WHY
    Print jobs run to delivery dates tied to mail drops, events, and publication schedules, with press time booked and postal induction scheduled. A missed press slot pushes everything behind it.
    LOSS
    Missed mail dates and event deliveries where the customer's campaign or event proceeds without the printed material, with claims measured by the campaign value rather than the print price.
    ASK
    What customer mail and event dates depend on your press schedule?
