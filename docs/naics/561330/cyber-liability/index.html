Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561330

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561330, Professional Employer Organizations, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold the entire workforce data of every client company.

    WHY
    PEOs act as co-employer, holding Social Security numbers, banking details, benefits enrolment, dependant information, medical and disability records, and payroll for the complete workforce of every client business. That is a concentration of employee data across hundreds of employers.
    LOSS
    Breach notification across the combined workforce of every client, where affected individuals have no relationship with the PEO and hold their own employer responsible, who in turn holds you responsible.
    ASK
    How many individual employees across all client companies are in your systems?
  2. 02

    Benefits administration makes you a HIPAA business associate and often a fiduciary.

    WHY
    PEOs administer health plans and retirement plans, handling protected health information under business associate agreements and, for retirement plans, operating in an environment where Department of Labor guidance addresses cybersecurity for plan fiduciaries and service providers.
    LOSS
    HIPAA enforcement directly against the PEO, plus fiduciary breach exposure where participant retirement accounts were compromised, which carries personal liability for fiduciaries.
    ASK
    Do you act as a fiduciary or business associate for client benefit plans?
  3. 03

    Payroll and tax remittance obligations run across many employers at once.

    WHY
    PEOs process payroll and remit employment taxes for all client employees on statutory deposit schedules. A failure affects every client employer's tax compliance simultaneously, and clients remain liable to taxing authorities.
    LOSS
    Missed tax deposits generating penalties across every client, with the PEO expected to indemnify while clients face authorities directly, and worker pay missed on the same cycle.
    ASK
    How many client employers' tax deposits depend on your next deposit date?
