Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111336

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111336, Fruit and Tree Nut Combination Farming, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Multiple crops extend the critical operating window across most of the year.

    WHY
    A combination operation harvests, packs, cools, and ships across most of the year, with different buyers, food safety schemes, labor peaks, and settlement systems for each commodity. There is no period in which a systems outage falls outside a harvest window.
    LOSS
    An outage at any point in an eight to ten month operating window occurs during an active harvest with perishable product on the tree or in the cooler.
    ASK
    In how many months of the year are you actively harvesting or shipping?
  2. 02

    Permanent plantings are destroyed, not just interrupted, by irrigation failure.

    WHY
    Orchards and vineyards have 15 to 40 year productive lives and multi-year establishment periods. They depend on remotely controlled irrigation and frost protection. Unlike annual crops, production cannot be resumed the following season.
    LOSS
    Tree or vine mortality from extended irrigation or freeze protection failure requires replanting at $8,000 to $20,000 per acre with 4 to 8 years to full production.
    ASK
    What is the replanting cost per acre for your orchard or vineyard blocks?
  3. 03

    Mixed commodities multiply traceability and certification requirements on shared systems.

    WHY
    Combination operations often grow crops on the FDA Food Traceability List alongside crops that are not covered, plus commodities with export residue restrictions. Each requires separate lot coding, records, and certification. FSMA 204 applies to the covered items on July 20, 2028 regardless of the other crops grown.
    LOSS
    A single records failure affects every commodity because the systems are shared, satisfying neither a traceback investigator nor a certification auditor.
    ASK
    Do you keep records for all of your crops in the same system?
