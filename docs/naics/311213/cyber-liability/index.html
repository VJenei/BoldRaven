Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311213

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311213, Malt Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Malting is a biological process measured in days.

    WHY
    Steeping, germination, and kilning proceed over several days under controlled moisture, temperature, and airflow. The grain is alive during germination and the process cannot be paused. Losing control mid-cycle ruins the batch.
    LOSS
    A germination or kilning batch ruined, representing several days of vessel capacity and the barley in it, with brewery customers who specified that malt for a production schedule.
    ASK
    How many days is a batch in process, and what happens if control is lost mid-cycle?
  2. 02

    Brewers specify malt to tight analytical parameters.

    WHY
    Malt is sold on analytical specification covering extract, colour, protein, and enzymatic activity, with brewers formulating recipes around those numbers. Out of specification malt changes the beer.
    LOSS
    Malt delivered outside specification affecting brewery production, with the brewer claiming for spoiled batches and the maltster losing a customer relationship built over years.
    ASK
    How are analytical results tied to specific malt lots shipped to customers?
  3. 03

    Mycotoxin testing protects the entire downstream chain.

    WHY
    Barley carries mycotoxin risk, particularly DON, which carries through malting into beer. Testing at intake and through processing determines whether grain can be malted and whether malt can be shipped.
    LOSS
    Contaminated malt reaching breweries and entering beer, with recall exposure across the brewer's production and claims traced back to the maltster's testing and acceptance decisions.
    ASK
    How do you document mycotoxin testing at barley intake?
