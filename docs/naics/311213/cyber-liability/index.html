Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311213

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311213, Malt Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Malting is a controlled biological process over days.

    • Steeping, germination, and kilning run to precise temperature and moisture schedules.
    • The process runs continuously and cannot be paused mid-batch.
    • A control failure ruins every vessel in process at once.
    ASK
    Is your malting process run by automated temperature and moisture controls?
  2. 02

    Brewers formulate their beer around your malt specification.

    • Extract, colour, and enzyme specifications are matched to customer recipes.
    • Substitution changes the beer, which brewers will not accept.
    • Off specification malt is rejected and the brewer's production stops.
    ASK
    How are your malt specifications tested and recorded per lot?
  3. 03

    Mycotoxin testing controls what can be sold.

    • Incoming barley and finished malt are tested for mycotoxins.
    • Brewers require those results before accepting delivery.
    • Contaminated malt cannot be sold at all and the barley is lost too.
    ASK
    What mycotoxin testing records do you maintain for barley and malt?
