Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111191

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111191, Oilseed and Grain Combination Farming, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Two crops means payment and contract activity across roughly eight months.

    WHY
    A combination operation markets corn and soybeans to different buyers under different contract structures. Delivery and settlement run from September through the following summer. Email traffic about contracts, delivery, and payment is active most of the year.
    LOSS
    Diverted settlement or prepay wires of $50,000 to $500,000 per event. A compromised mailbox is typically used more than once before the pattern is detected.
    ASK
    In how many months of the year do you send or receive payments over $50,000?
  2. 02

    Combination operations are heavy users of farm management software.

    WHY
    Rotating corn and soybeans across many fields and landlords requires field records, rotation history, variable-rate prescriptions, and landlord-by-landlord accounting. This is held in Operations Center, FieldView, Granular, or similar cloud platforms accessed with a single set of credentials.
    LOSS
    Loss of multi-year rotation and yield history used to support cash rent negotiations and landlord reporting.
    ASK
    How many landlords do you report field results to, and where is that data kept?
  3. 03

    Annual operating lines require financial documents sent electronically to lenders.

    WHY
    Large grain operations renew operating notes annually on financial statements, inventory positions, and marketing plans. Tax returns, balance sheets, and bank account details are submitted through lender portals or by email and remain in the sent folder.
    LOSS
    Loan renewal delayed because financials cannot be produced, plus account takeover using the financial documents recovered from a compromised mailbox.
    ASK
    Do you send tax returns or financial statements to your lender by email?
