Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111120

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111120, Oilseed (except Soybean) Farming, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Production is contracted to a small number of crush plants with scheduled delivery slots.

    WHY
    Canola, sunflower, flax, and safflower are grown on production contracts. Delivery slots are scheduled electronically. Act-of-God clauses cover weather but not a buyer's system failure. There is limited spot market depth to absorb production if the crusher cannot receive.
    LOSS
    Missed delivery windows require a contract buyout or a discounted distressed sale. Property policies do not respond because nothing was physically damaged.
    ASK
    Is your crop sold under contract to a single processor?
  2. 02

    Fall input prepay is made in a small number of large wire transfers.

    WHY
    Seed, fertilizer, and chemical are prepaid in the fall for pricing and tax reasons. Payment is usually one wire to a retailer or cooperative against an invoice received by email. Attackers alter the account details on a real invoice.
    LOSS
    A prepay wire of $150,000 to $400,000 sent to a criminal account. The supplier has not been paid and still expects payment, so the operation pays twice.
    ASK
    Do you prepay inputs by wire transfer, and what is the largest single payment you make in a year?
  3. 03

    Identity-preserved premiums depend on documentation you must produce on demand.

    WHY
    High-oleic and specialty oilseed contracts are identity-preserved. The grower must document seed lot, field isolation, field history, and segregated handling. These records are kept in spreadsheets or a grower portal. Without them the crop is graded as commodity.
    LOSS
    An identity-preserved premium of $0.60 to $2.00 per bushel is lost across the contracted acreage because the documentation chain cannot be produced for the buyer's auditor.
    ASK
    Do you grow any crop under an identity-preserved or specialty contract that requires recordkeeping?
