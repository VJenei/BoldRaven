Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 524127

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 524127, Direct Title Insurance Carriers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Closing wires are the most targeted transfer in the country.

    • Closing funds move by wire on a scheduled date.
    • Fraudsters send fake wiring instructions to buyers and agents.
    • A diverted closing wire takes the buyer's entire down payment.
    ASK
    How are wiring instructions delivered and verified before closing?
  2. 02

    You hold escrow funds belonging to other people.

    • Escrow accounts hold buyer, seller, and lender funds until disbursement.
    • State regulators examine escrow handling directly.
    • A shortfall must be restored from your own funds immediately.
    ASK
    How are escrow disbursements authorised and reconciled?
  3. 03

    Title and closing files hold complete transaction data.

    • Files carry identity, financial, property, and lender information.
    • That data supports identity theft and closing fraud.
    • Every affected party must be notified.
    ASK
    How many closing files with personal and financial data do you hold?
