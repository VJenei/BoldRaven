Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561599

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561599, All Other Travel Arrangement and Reservation Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold traveller identity and payment data.

    • Bookings carry identity, passport, and payment card details.
    • Travel history shows where individuals go and when.
    • Every affected traveller must be notified.
    ASK
    How many traveller records with identity and payment data do you hold?
  2. 02

    Bookings and confirmations run through your systems.

    • Reservations and confirmations are made and issued electronically.
    • Travellers rely on those confirmations at check-in.
    • A booking that cannot be confirmed leaves someone stranded.
    ASK
    How are your bookings made, confirmed, and recorded?
  3. 03

    Supplier payments move traveller money.

    • Payments to suppliers are made with traveller funds in advance.
    • Fraudulent instructions divert those payments.
    • Travellers arrive to find no booking exists.
    ASK
    How are supplier payment instructions verified before funds move?
