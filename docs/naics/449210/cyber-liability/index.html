Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 449210

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 449210, Electronics and Appliance Retailers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Serialised high value inventory is a persistent theft and fraud target.

    WHY
    Televisions, computers, and appliances are high value, serialised, and instantly resaleable. Organised retail crime targets this category specifically, and inventory systems record what is on hand, in transit, and reserved for pickup.
    LOSS
    Theft informed by inventory and pickup data, plus fraudulent order and pickup schemes where goods are released against compromised customer accounts.
    ASK
    How are online orders verified before goods are released for in-store pickup?
  2. 02

    Installation and delivery reach inside customers' homes and networks.

    WHY
    Retailers install appliances and configure connected devices, televisions, and networking equipment in customers' homes, with technicians holding customer contact details, addresses, access arrangements, and sometimes network credentials.
    LOSS
    A breach exposing customer addresses, scheduled delivery times, and details of the high value equipment installed, which is a targeting list for residential burglary.
    ASK
    Do your technicians record home access details or network credentials?
  3. 03

    Extended warranty and protection plans create long-lived customer records.

    WHY
    Protection plans are sold at the register and administered for years afterward, tying customer identity, payment details, and product serial numbers together in a record retained well beyond the sale.
    LOSS
    A breach of long-retained plan records exposes customers who bought years ago and have no current relationship with the store, expanding notification far beyond active customers.
    ASK
    How many years of protection plan customer records do you retain?
