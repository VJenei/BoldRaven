Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 332313

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 332313, Plate Work Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Pressure vessels are code-stamped and their failure is explosive.

    WHY
    Pressure vessels and plate work are fabricated to ASME code with design calculations, qualified welding procedures, non-destructive examination, and third-party inspection, culminating in a code stamp and data report. The stamp certifies the vessel is safe at pressure.
    LOSS
    A vessel failure at pressure, which is an explosive event with fatality potential, and the manufacturer's design, welding, and inspection records determining whether the vessel was ever fit for service.
    ASK
    How are code data reports and radiographic records retained after a vessel ships?
  2. 02

    Welding qualification and procedures are audited by inspectors.

    WHY
    Code fabrication requires qualified welding procedures, qualified welders, and a documented quality control system audited by the authorised inspection agency. The certificate of authorisation to apply the code stamp depends on it.
    LOSS
    Loss of the code stamp authorisation where the quality system and records cannot be demonstrated, which removes the ability to fabricate pressure equipment entirely.
    ASK
    What does your authorised inspector examine during a code audit?
  3. 03

    Vessels are engineered and fabricated for one installation.

    WHY
    Plate work is designed for specific service conditions, dimensions, and nozzle arrangements at a particular plant, with long fabrication lead times. Nothing is stock.
    LOSS
    Vessels fabricated to wrong designs that do not fit the installation or the service, with remake taking months and the customer's plant project delayed accordingly.
    ASK
    How long is the lead time to refabricate a vessel from scratch?
