Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 458320

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 458320, Luggage and Leather Goods Retailers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Card payments run continuously at the counter.

    • Point of sale terminals process cards through trading hours.
    • Card security requirements apply to every location.
    • A breach brings assessments, forensics, and bank claims.
    ASK
    How does your point of sale system handle and store card data?
  2. 02

    High value portable inventory moves quickly.

    • Premium goods carry high value in small volume.
    • Inventory records are the only control over shrinkage.
    • Diverted stock is resold within days.
    ASK
    How frequently is your high value inventory reconciled against records?
  3. 03

    Travel and holiday seasons drive the year.

    • Sales concentrate around travel seasons and the fourth quarter.
    • Inventory and staffing peak together.
    • Volume missed then cannot be recovered.
    ASK
    What share of your annual sales happens in your peak seasons?
