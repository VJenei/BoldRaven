Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 525990

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 525990, Other Financial Vehicles, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Structured vehicles depend entirely on data about their collateral.

    WHY
    Special purpose vehicles and structured finance entities hold pools of assets whose performance data drives waterfall calculations, investor distributions, and covenant tests. The vehicle has no operations other than the administration of that data.
    LOSS
    Distributions that cannot be calculated and covenant tests that cannot be performed, which in structured finance can itself constitute an event of default under the transaction documents.
    ASK
    What happens under your transaction documents if a payment date calculation cannot be performed?
  2. 02

    Investor reporting is contractual and dated.

    WHY
    Vehicles report to investors, trustees, and rating agencies on defined dates with prescribed content. Those reports are the only visibility investors have into the collateral, and rating agencies act on them.
    LOSS
    Missed or unsupportable investor reporting triggers trustee action, rating agency review, and investor claims, with consequences under the documents that are mechanical rather than negotiable.
    ASK
    What are your reporting deadlines to trustees and rating agencies?
  3. 03

    Underlying obligor data is personal data held at scale.

    WHY
    Vehicles holding consumer receivables, mortgages, or auto loans hold obligor-level data including identity, payment history, and account details, even though the vehicle itself has no customer relationship with those individuals.
    LOSS
    Breach notification to consumers who have never heard of the entity holding their data, with the servicer, sponsor, and vehicle all implicated and the notification obligation unclear until it is litigated.
    ASK
    How many individual obligors' records sit within the pools you hold?
