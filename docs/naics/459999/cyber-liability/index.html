Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 459999

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 459999, All Other Miscellaneous Retailers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    The point of sale system is the business and there is usually only one.

    WHY
    Specialty retailers run pricing, inventory, customer accounts, and payment through a single platform, typically with no IT function and often without a verified backup. The system prices every item in the store and no staff member can reproduce it.
    LOSS
    The store cannot transact at all, and for an owner-operated retailer with thin working capital, an extended closure is a solvency event rather than a disruption.
    ASK
    When did you last test that your point of sale backup actually restores?
  2. 02

    Card acceptance carries contractual liability regardless of size.

    WHY
    Every card-accepting merchant is bound by PCI DSS through the merchant agreement, fully mandatory in version 4.0 since March 31, 2025, including new requirements around e-commerce payment page scripts. There is no small merchant exemption from the contractual liability.
    LOSS
    Card brand assessments, forensic investigation, and reissuance costs assigned to the retailer by the merchant agreement, at amounts a small business cannot absorb.
    ASK
    Have you read who pays for a forensic investigation in your merchant agreement?
  3. 03

    Customer data accumulates for years without anyone deciding to keep it.

    WHY
    Specialty retailers accumulate customer contact details, purchase histories, special orders, and stored payment methods over many years, usually with no retention policy and no decision ever made to delete. The oldest data is the least protected and the most forgotten.
    LOSS
    Notification obligations covering customers from many years ago who no longer shop with you, expanding the cost and the reputational reach of a breach far beyond your active customer base.
    ASK
    How many years of customer records are sitting in your system right now?
