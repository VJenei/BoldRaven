Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 332813

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 332813, Electroplating, Plating, Polishing, Anodizing, and Coloring, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Plating baths are controlled processes and losing control ruins parts.

    WHY
    Plating lines run on controlled chemistry, current, and time, with automated hoists moving racks between tanks. Deviation produces parts with wrong thickness, poor adhesion, or contamination, visible only through testing.
    LOSS
    Full production runs plated out of specification, with customer parts scrapped at their full accumulated value and the customer's assembly stopped.
    ASK
    How are plating line parameters set and recorded per rack or lot?
  2. 02

    Hydrogen embrittlement in high strength parts is fatal to the part.

    WHY
    Plating high strength steel introduces hydrogen that must be baked out within a specified time after plating. Parts not baked in time fail without warning after installation, sometimes months later.
    LOSS
    High strength parts not baked within the window, failing in service in vehicles, aircraft, or structures, with delayed failure making every part from the affected period suspect.
    ASK
    How are plating completion and bake start times recorded for high strength parts?
  3. 03

    The operation is heavily regulated for chemical and environmental risk.

    WHY
    Plating uses cyanides, acids, chromium, and other regulated chemicals with wastewater pretreatment requirements, air permits, hazardous waste manifests, and monitoring obligations under federal and local rules.
    LOSS
    Discharge exceedances or missed monitoring with permit violations and penalties, and in a release event, community exposure to plating chemicals with evacuation and emergency response.
    ASK
    What continuous or periodic monitoring does your pretreatment permit require?
