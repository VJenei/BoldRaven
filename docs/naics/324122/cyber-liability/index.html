Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 324122

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 324122, Asphalt Shingle and Coating Materials Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Shingles carry wind and fire ratings that failure exposes.

    WHY
    Roofing shingles are tested and rated for wind resistance and fire classification, with those ratings relied on by building codes, specifiers, and insurers. Manufacturing consistency determines whether product meets the rating.
    LOSS
    Product failing wind or fire rating installed across thousands of homes, discovered in a storm event, with class litigation and warranty exposure across every roof containing affected production.
    ASK
    How is production quality tied to the wind and fire ratings you publish?
  2. 02

    Continuous coating lines run hot asphalt at high speed.

    WHY
    Shingle production runs continuous saturation and coating with hot asphalt, granule application, and cutting at high line speed. Hot asphalt handling carries fire risk and line control determines product weight and construction.
    LOSS
    A fire in the asphalt handling or coating section taking a line offline for months, plus off-specification product produced continuously until testing detects it.
    ASK
    How is asphalt temperature and coating weight monitored during production?
  3. 03

    Long warranties mean production records matter for decades.

    WHY
    Shingles carry warranties of twenty to fifty years, with claims arising long after production. Manufacturing and testing records for the production period are the evidence in a warranty or defect claim.
    LOSS
    Warranty and defect claims decades later where production and quality records cannot be produced, leaving the manufacturer unable to demonstrate the product met specification when made.
    ASK
    How long do you retain production quality records against your warranty period?
