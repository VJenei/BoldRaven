Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333511

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333511, Industrial Mold Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    The digital chain from part model to mold is the product.

    • Mold design, electrode design, and machining programmes take months per tool.
    • The tool exists as data before it exists as steel.
    • Losing that data restarts months of engineering.
    ASK
    How many active mold designs and machining programmes exist on your systems?
  2. 02

    You hold customer products before they launch.

    • Part models arrive before the customer's product is announced.
    • You hold the geometry of unreleased products.
    • Disclosure compromises their launch and breaches your agreement.
    ASK
    Do you hold part models for customer products not yet released?
  3. 03

    Tooling gates the customer's entire launch.

    • Production tooling is on the critical path for a molded product.
    • Launch, marketing, and retail commitments are built around tool delivery.
    • The exposure is their launch, not the price of the tool.
    ASK
    What is the typical lead time from order to first article on your tools?
