Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333511

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333511, Industrial Mold Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Mold design and machining data is the entire product.

    WHY
    Molds are machined from customer part models through mold design, electrode design, and CNC programming. The digital chain from part model to finished mold represents months of engineering per tool.
    LOSS
    Loss of mold design and machining data with tools in build, requiring designs to be recreated from customer models and months added to programmes where the customer's product launch depends on the tool.
    ASK
    How many active mold designs and machining programmes exist on your systems?
  2. 02

    Customer part models are confidential pre-launch designs.

    WHY
    Mold makers receive part models before the customer's product launches, frequently under agreements protecting the design. The tool maker holds the geometry of unreleased products.
    LOSS
    Exposure of unreleased customer product designs, with contractual liability for the breach and a customer whose product launch strategy is compromised before announcement.
    ASK
    Do you hold part models for customer products not yet released?
  3. 03

    Tooling programmes gate the customer's entire launch.

    WHY
    Production tooling is on the critical path for a molded product, with the customer's launch, marketing, and supply commitments built around tool delivery. Molds take months to build.
    LOSS
    Tool delivery missed by weeks with the customer's product launch delayed, and consequential exposure far beyond the tool price where launch commitments were made to retailers.
    ASK
    What is the typical lead time from order to first article on your tools?
