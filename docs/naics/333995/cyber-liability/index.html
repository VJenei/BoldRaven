Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333995

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333995, Fluid Power Cylinder and Actuator Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Cylinders hold loads and dropping them kills people.

    WHY
    Hydraulic and pneumatic cylinders hold and move loads on lifts, presses, and equipment. Failure of a rod, seal, or end cap releases the load and, on suspended equipment, drops it.
    LOSS
    A cylinder failure dropping a suspended load or press ram with fatal injury, and every cylinder from the affected production requiring identification across customer equipment.
    ASK
    Do your cylinders hold suspended loads in customer equipment?
  2. 02

    Pressure rating depends on material, welding, and testing.

    WHY
    Cylinder capacity depends on tube material, weld quality at end caps, and rod specification, verified by pressure testing before shipment. Failure occurs at pressure with stored energy release.
    LOSS
    Cylinders that were never properly tested failing at pressure, with injection injuries and equipment damage, and test records determining which units are affected.
    ASK
    How are pressure test results recorded against cylinder serial numbers?
  3. 03

    Equipment manufacturers run to schedule on your components.

    WHY
    Cylinders are supplied to construction, agricultural, and industrial equipment builders on production schedules with qualified designs and approved processes.
    LOSS
    Equipment assembly lines stopped for want of cylinders, with line-down charges and expedited freight far exceeding the component value.
    ASK
    Which equipment manufacturers run to schedule on your components?
