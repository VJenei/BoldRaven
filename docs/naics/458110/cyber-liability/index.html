Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 458110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 458110, Clothing and Clothing Accessories Retailers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Seasons expire and unsold inventory loses most of its value.

    WHY
    Apparel is bought months ahead against a season, with markdown cadence planned from sell-through data. Merchandising, allocation between stores, and markdown timing are driven entirely by point of sale data. Once the season passes the goods clear at a fraction of ticket.
    LOSS
    Blind allocation and markdown decisions during an outage leave inventory in the wrong stores at the wrong price, converting a normal season into a margin write-down.
    ASK
    How do you decide markdowns and inter-store transfers without sell-through data?
  2. 02

    E-commerce checkout is where card data is now stolen.

    WHY
    Apparel retail runs significant online volume, and digital skimming injects code into checkout pages to capture card data in transit. PCI DSS 4.0 became fully mandatory on March 31, 2025 and specifically added requirements to manage and monitor scripts on payment pages because of this technique.
    LOSS
    Card brand assessments, forensics, and reissuance for every card entered on the compromised page, which continues silently until someone detects the injected script.
    ASK
    Who has the ability to change code on your checkout page, and how is that monitored?
  3. 03

    Returns and loyalty fraud are systematic, not occasional.

    WHY
    Apparel carries very high return rates, and return fraud, wardrobing, and loyalty account takeover are organised activities. Return authorisation, gift card issuance, and loyalty balances are all system-controlled decisions.
    LOSS
    Systematic fraud against returns and stored value that continues until the pattern is identified, with losses concentrated in gift cards and loyalty balances that convert to merchandise immediately.
    ASK
    How much value is outstanding in your gift cards and loyalty balances?
