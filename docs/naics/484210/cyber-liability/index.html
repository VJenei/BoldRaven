Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 484210

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 484210, Used Household and Office Goods Moving, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold the complete contents and new address of every customer.

    WHY
    Movers create inventories of household contents, hold origin and destination addresses, move dates, and often storage locations. That data describes exactly what valuables a household owns, where they will be, and when the home will be empty.
    LOSS
    A breach exposing move schedules and household inventories is a targeting list for residential burglary, which is a materially different harm from ordinary data loss and one customers will not forgive.
    ASK
    Do your systems record household contents inventories alongside addresses and move dates?
  2. 02

    Corporate relocation accounts hold employee personal data.

    WHY
    Relocation contracts with corporate clients involve moving named employees, with contact details, family information, home addresses, and sometimes visa and immigration status held by the mover on the employer's behalf.
    LOSS
    Third-party liability to corporate clients for exposure of their employees' personal data, plus loss of relocation contracts that represent the stable, higher margin side of the business.
    ASK
    Do you hold employee data on behalf of corporate relocation clients?
  3. 03

    Estimates, inventories, and valuation elections decide every claim.

    WHY
    Interstate moves require written estimates, inventories with condition notations at origin and destination, and a documented valuation election determining liability per pound. Claims are decided from those documents, and federal rules govern the process.
    LOSS
    Damage claims resolved against the mover where inventories and valuation elections cannot be produced, and regulatory exposure where required documents were not retained.
    ASK
    How do you retain inventories and valuation elections after a move completes?
