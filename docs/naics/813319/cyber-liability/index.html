Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 813319

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 813319, Other Social Advocacy Organizations, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Membership indicates a position people may not want disclosed.

    WHY
    Advocacy organisations hold member and supporter lists where affiliation itself reveals political, social, or personal positions. Depending on the cause and the community, disclosure of membership carries employment, family, and safety consequences.
    LOSS
    Exposure of membership identifying individuals with a cause, causing personal and professional harm and, for organisations working on contested issues, targeted harassment of named supporters.
    ASK
    Would your members face any consequence if their affiliation became public?
  2. 02

    Opponents are motivated by exposure rather than payment.

    WHY
    Organisations on contested issues attract adversaries interested in internal communications, strategy, and supporter identities. Publication is the objective, which removes any possibility of resolving an incident through payment.
    LOSS
    Publication of internal material and supporter lists, which cannot be recalled and which produces harassment of named individuals that the organisation cannot protect them from.
    ASK
    Is there any party who would publish your internal records if they obtained them?
  3. 03

    Service recipients may be in vulnerable circumstances.

    WHY
    Advocacy organisations frequently provide direct services alongside campaigning, holding client records covering immigration status, domestic violence, legal matters, and health circumstances.
    LOSS
    Exposure of client circumstances with immigration, safety, and legal consequences for people who came to the organisation for help precisely because it was confidential.
    ASK
    Do you hold client records covering immigration status or safety circumstances?
