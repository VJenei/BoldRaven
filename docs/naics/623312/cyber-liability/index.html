Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 623312

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 623312, Assisted Living Facilities for the Elderly, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Residents depend on staff and systems for safety.

    • Medication, care, and emergency call systems run electronically.
    • Residents cannot always summon help without those systems.
    • A failure affects people with no alternative.
    ASK
    How many residents depend on your emergency call and medication systems?
  2. 02

    Medication administration runs on electronic records.

    • Medication orders and administration records run electronically.
    • Residents take many medications on precise schedules.
    • Medication errors in this population cause serious harm.
    ASK
    How is medication administration recorded and verified?
  3. 03

    Resident records hold health and financial data.

    • Records carry clinical, financial, and family contact information.
    • Health privacy obligations apply to much of it.
    • Every affected resident and family must be notified.
    ASK
    How many resident records do you hold?
