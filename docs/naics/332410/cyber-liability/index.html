Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 332410

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 332410, Power Boiler and Heat Exchanger Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Boilers fail explosively and are code-regulated for that reason.

    WHY
    Power boilers are fabricated to ASME code with design calculations, qualified welding, non-destructive examination, hydrostatic testing, and third-party inspection culminating in a code stamp. Boiler explosions historically killed so many people that the code exists because of them.
    LOSS
    A boiler failure at pressure and temperature, which is a catastrophic explosion with fatalities and destruction of the plant area, investigated against the fabrication and inspection records.
    ASK
    How are your code data reports, radiographs, and test records retained?
  2. 02

    Code stamp authorisation depends on a demonstrable quality system.

    WHY
    The certificate of authorisation to apply the code stamp requires a quality control manual, qualified procedures, and records audited by the authorised inspection agency and the certifying body.
    LOSS
    Loss of code stamp authorisation where the quality system cannot be evidenced, which removes the ability to fabricate boilers and pressure equipment entirely.
    ASK
    What would your authorised inspector find if they audited your records today?
  3. 03

    Equipment is engineered for one installation with long lead times.

    WHY
    Boilers and heat exchangers are designed for specific process conditions, duties, and connections at a particular plant, with fabrication taking months. Plant projects and outages are scheduled around delivery.
    LOSS
    Equipment fabricated to wrong specification requiring remake over months, with the customer's plant outage window missed and the project delayed a full cycle.
    ASK
    How long is the fabrication lead time for your typical equipment?
