Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 722514

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 722514, Cafeterias, Grill Buffets, and Buffets, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Food safety monitoring is continuous in a buffet operation.

    WHY
    Hot and cold holding temperatures must be monitored and logged throughout service, with time and temperature control being the primary food safety control in a buffet format. Health inspectors examine those logs directly.
    LOSS
    Product held out of temperature and served, causing foodborne illness across many diners simultaneously given the shared service format, with health department action and closure.
    ASK
    How are holding temperatures recorded and monitored during service?
  2. 02

    Volume dining concentrates transactions and risk.

    WHY
    Cafeteria and buffet formats serve high covers at low average ticket, with payment at entry or exit through point of sale systems. Institutional cafeterias frequently operate account-based payment for students or employees.
    LOSS
    Payment systems unavailable during a meal period, with hundreds of diners unable to be served or charged, and account-based systems that cannot post charges to student or employee accounts.
    ASK
    How many diners do you serve in a single meal period?
  3. 03

    Allergen information must be available at the service line.

    WHY
    Buffet formats require ingredient and allergen information to be available for each item, with recipes and ingredient data maintained centrally and displayed or provided on request. Diners self-serve without staff interaction.
    LOSS
    An allergen incident where ingredient information was unavailable or wrong at the service line, in a format where the diner had no opportunity to ask before serving themselves.
    ASK
    How is allergen information for each item made available to diners?
