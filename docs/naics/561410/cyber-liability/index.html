Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561410

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561410, Document Preparation Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You handle the documents clients consider most sensitive.

    WHY
    Document preparation services transcribe, format, and produce legal filings, medical reports, financial documents, and personal records. Material is sent because it must be processed, and it frequently contains the most confidential information a client holds.
    LOSS
    Disclosure of client documents including privileged legal material and medical records, with contractual liability and, for health information handled as a business associate, direct HIPAA exposure.
    ASK
    What categories of client documents pass through your systems?
  2. 02

    Medical transcription involves protected health information.

    WHY
    Transcription of clinical dictation makes the provider a business associate under HIPAA, handling patient identifiers, diagnoses, and treatment details. Work is frequently distributed to transcriptionists working remotely on their own equipment.
    LOSS
    HIPAA breach notification and OCR enforcement against the provider directly, plus contractual claims from every healthcare client whose patients were affected.
    ASK
    Do you transcribe clinical documentation under business associate agreements?
  3. 03

    Turnaround commitments feed client deadlines.

    WHY
    Document preparation supports court filings, medical record completion, and financial reporting with turnaround times measured in hours. Client deadlines are set by courts, regulators, and internal requirements.
    LOSS
    Missed client deadlines with consequences including court sanctions and regulatory penalties, claimed against the provider whose delay caused them.
    ASK
    What client deadlines depend on your turnaround times?
