Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333241

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333241, Food Product Machinery Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Your equipment performs the food safety critical steps.

    WHY
    Pasteurisers, retorts, fillers, and metal detectors execute the critical control points in food processing under HACCP. The machine controls temperature, time, and rejection, and the processor's food safety plan relies on the machine performing as specified.
    LOSS
    Equipment control defects allowing under-processed food through, with a foodborne illness outbreak, recall across the processor's production, and the equipment manufacturer named for the control failure.
    ASK
    Does your equipment control a critical limit in your customers' HACCP plans?
  2. 02

    Machines are connected for remote support and updates.

    WHY
    Food machinery ships with PLCs and HMIs, frequently with remote access for commissioning, diagnostics, and updates. That connection reaches into food plant control networks.
    LOSS
    Remote access used to reach customer plant networks, shutting down production lines and making the manufacturer the confirmed entry point for every plant with the same access arrangement.
    ASK
    How do your service engineers connect remotely to installed equipment?
  3. 03

    Sanitary design and materials are regulated for food contact.

    WHY
    Food contact surfaces must be of approved materials and cleanable design, with certification to industry sanitary standards. Equipment that harbours bacteria contaminates everything running through it.
    LOSS
    Equipment with non-conforming materials or design harbouring pathogens across a plant, with contamination in finished product and recall covering the whole period the equipment was in service.
    ASK
    How do you document sanitary design certification and food contact material compliance?
