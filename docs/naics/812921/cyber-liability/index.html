Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 812921

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 812921, Photofinishing Laboratories (except One-Hour), needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Customer images are personal and irreplaceable.

    WHY
    Photofinishing operations receive customer images including film, digital files, and legacy media for scanning and restoration. Much of it is family archive material with no other copy, and the content is inherently private.
    LOSS
    Loss of customer images that cannot be recreated, and exposure of private family and personal images, which customers experience as a violation quite separate from any financial loss.
    ASK
    How much irreplaceable customer image material is on your systems at any time?
  2. 02

    Order and customer data links images to identified people.

    WHY
    Orders link customer identity, contact details, addresses, and payment methods to specific image sets, held in production systems and retained for reorders.
    LOSS
    Exposure of images alongside the identity and address of the people in them, which is materially more harmful than either dataset alone.
    ASK
    How long are customer images retained after an order is fulfilled?
  3. 03

    Production systems drive automated printing and finishing.

    WHY
    Laboratories run automated printing, colour management, and finishing driven by order files, with volume concentrated around holidays and events. Orders are promised against delivery dates.
    LOSS
    Production halted with dated orders for holidays and events undelivered, on product that has no value after the occasion it was made for.
    ASK
    How much of your volume is tied to dated occasions and holidays?
