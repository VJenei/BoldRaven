Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 221118

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 221118, Other Electric Power Generation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Generation and storage systems are controlled remotely.

    • Units, inverters, and battery systems are managed from central platforms.
    • Settings and dispatch can be changed across the fleet at once.
    • Battery systems in particular fail into fire when controls are wrong.
    ASK
    Can your generation or storage systems be configured remotely?
  2. 02

    Grid reliability and interconnection rules apply.

    • Covered facilities must meet documented federal reliability security requirements.
    • Interconnection agreements add control and response obligations.
    • Non-compliance carries penalties per day against the registered entity.
    ASK
    Which of your systems fall within grid reliability security requirements?
  3. 03

    Revenue depends on metered output and dispatch records.

    • Generation and market participation are settled from recorded data.
    • Market bids and dispatch instructions arrive electronically.
    • Corrupted data means production you cannot bill.
    ASK
    How is your generation and market settlement data recorded?
