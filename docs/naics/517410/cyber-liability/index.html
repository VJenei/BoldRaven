Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 517410

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 517410, Satellite Telecommunications, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Satellite control systems are national security infrastructure.

    • Ground stations command and control spacecraft in orbit.
    • Loss of control can lose the spacecraft permanently.
    • Government and defence customers depend on those satellites.
    ASK
    What systems command and control your spacecraft?
  2. 02

    Customers have no terrestrial alternative.

    • Remote sites, maritime, and aviation customers rely on satellite as the only link.
    • Emergency and safety communications run over those links.
    • An outage leaves them with no communications at all.
    ASK
    What customers depend on your service as their only communications link?
  3. 03

    Government contracts impose security requirements.

    • Defence and government customers require documented cybersecurity compliance.
    • Those requirements are contract conditions.
    • Losing eligibility removes the highest value customers.
    ASK
    What security requirements do your government contracts impose?
