Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 624221

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 624221, Temporary Shelters, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Shelter location and resident identity are safety information.

    WHY
    Domestic violence and homeless shelters hold resident identities, locations, and circumstances, with domestic violence shelter addresses deliberately withheld from abusers. Federal funding conditions and state law impose strict confidentiality on this data.
    LOSS
    Disclosure of a resident's location to a person they fled, which is a foreseeable and potentially fatal outcome, and confidentiality violations that carry funding and legal consequences on top.
    ASK
    Is your shelter location or resident list deliberately withheld from specific individuals?
  2. 02

    Homeless management information systems hold sensitive client data.

    WHY
    HUD-funded programmes participate in homeless management information systems recording client identity, disability status, health conditions, veteran status, and service history, shared across participating agencies in a community.
    LOSS
    Exposure of client records including disability and health data across an entire community's homeless services population, with HUD funding consequences and harm to people already in crisis.
    ASK
    What client data do you enter into your community's homeless management information system?
  3. 03

    Residents have no alternative if the shelter cannot operate.

    WHY
    Shelters provide the only accommodation available to residents that night, with bed availability, intake, and referral coordinated across a community. Capacity is limited and demand exceeds it, particularly in cold weather.
    LOSS
    Residents turned away or intake halted because bed availability and eligibility cannot be verified, leaving people without shelter in conditions where exposure is a genuine risk to life.
    ASK
    How is bed availability tracked and communicated across your community's shelters?
