Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 339994

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 339994, Broom, Brush, and Mop Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Retail supply runs on vendor systems with enforced compliance.

    WHY
    Cleaning products ship to mass retailers, grocery, and distributors under EDI with labelling, packing, routing, and delivery window requirements enforced by chargeback.
    LOSS
    Order and shipping systems unavailable, with missed delivery windows, chargebacks applied per shipment, and shelf space at risk with concentrated retail customers.
    ASK
    What share of your volume ships through your three largest customers?
  2. 02

    Industrial and food service brushes carry compliance requirements.

    WHY
    Brushes used in food processing must meet food contact material requirements and, in many plants, be detectable or colour coded so that a broken bristle can be found before it reaches product.
    LOSS
    Bristles or components entering food product at a customer plant, with a foreign material recall covering the production run and the brush identified as the source.
    ASK
    Do your products go into food processing environments with foreign material controls?
  3. 03

    Production runs at volume with automated equipment.

    WHY
    Brush and broom production runs automated tufting, moulding, and assembly at high rate with machine settings and specifications held in production systems.
    LOSS
    Production systems unavailable or settings lost, with lines stopped and customer delivery windows missed while specifications are reconstructed.
    ASK
    Where are your machine settings and product specifications stored?
