Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111140

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111140, Wheat Farming, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Custom harvest is coordinated through cloud software across multiple states.

    WHY
    Wheat harvest moves north from Texas to Montana. Field locations, acres, moisture readings, machine telematics, and per-acre billing are coordinated between grower and custom cutter through cloud platforms and mobile devices. When that coordination fails, machines and crews stop.
    LOSS
    Idle custom harvest equipment and crew costs $8,000 to $15,000 per day. Standing wheat left through a weather event suffers shattering, sprout damage, and falling number discounts across the field.
    ASK
    Do you use a custom harvest crew, and how is field and acre information shared with them?
  2. 02

    Premiums and discounts are calculated from grade data held by the buyer.

    WHY
    Wheat settles on protein, test weight, falling number, and dockage. These are measured at the elevator and recorded in the elevator's system. The grower does not hold an independent copy. Disputes are resolved from the buyer's records.
    LOSS
    A 40 cent protein premium on 60,000 bushels is $24,000. If the buyer's records are encrypted or unavailable, there is no basis to support a claim for that amount.
    ASK
    Do you keep your own copy of scale tickets and grade sheets from your buyer?
  3. 03

    Crop insurance claims have deadlines that a systems outage does not extend.

    WHY
    Federal crop insurance requires acreage reports by the acreage reporting date, notice of loss generally within 72 hours of discovery, and production records to support an APH claim. These records are held in farm management software and FSA and RMA portals.
    LOSS
    A denied or reduced indemnity in a claim year. Wheat coverage commonly runs $80,000 to $250,000 on a mid-size operation.
    ASK
    Do you carry federal crop insurance, and how are your production and acreage records stored?
