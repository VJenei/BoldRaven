Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 424350

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 424350, Clothing and Clothing Accessories Merchant Wholesalers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Retail floor set dates decide the season.

    • Product must arrive for a specific retail floor set.
    • Delivery windows are fixed and enforced by chargeback.
    • Late product sells at markdown and the season is gone.
    ASK
    How are your deliveries scheduled against customer floor set dates?
  2. 02

    Labelling and origin requirements are federally enforced.

    • Fiber content, care, and country of origin labelling is required.
    • Errors cause retailer rejection and enforcement action.
    • Mislabelled goods must be relabelled or destroyed.
    ASK
    How do you verify labelling compliance on the goods you distribute?
  3. 03

    Import payments settle by transfer to overseas factories.

    • Imported apparel is paid for by transfer against supplier instructions.
    • Banking instructions arrive and are changed by email.
    • A diverted payment is a container of goods lost.
    ASK
    How are changes to supplier banking details verified before payment?
