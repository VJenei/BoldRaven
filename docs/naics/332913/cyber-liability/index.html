Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 332913

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 332913, Plumbing Fixture Fitting and Trim Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Drinking water fittings are regulated for lead content.

    WHY
    Fittings and trim contacting drinking water must meet the lead-free requirements of the Safe Drinking Water Act and be certified to NSF/ANSI 61 and 372. Certification is verified by third parties and required by plumbing codes.
    LOSS
    Non-compliant fittings installed in drinking water systems, with lead exposure to occupants, recall across distribution, and regulatory action for supplying products that do not meet federal lead limits.
    ASK
    How do you document lead-free certification for products in drinking water contact?
  2. 02

    Fitting failure causes water damage in occupied buildings.

    WHY
    Supply fittings, connectors, and valves are under constant pressure in occupied buildings. Failure floods the building continuously until someone finds it, which in an unoccupied period means an entire structure.
    LOSS
    Fitting failures across a production run flooding homes and commercial buildings, with property damage claims from every installation and recall through distribution and retail.
    ASK
    Are your products under constant water pressure once installed?
  3. 03

    Retail and wholesale supply runs on vendor systems.

    WHY
    Plumbing products ship to home centres and wholesale distributors under EDI with labelling, packaging, and delivery requirements enforced by chargeback.
    LOSS
    Order and shipping systems unavailable, with missed delivery windows, vendor compliance chargebacks, and shelf space at risk with a small number of very large customers.
    ASK
    What share of your volume goes to your three largest customers?
