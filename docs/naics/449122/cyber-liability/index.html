Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 449122

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 449122, Window Treatment Retailers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Every product is fabricated to measurements that cannot be guessed.

    WHY
    Blinds, shades, and drapery are cut and assembled to exact window dimensions captured in the home, with mount type, control side, and fabric all specified per opening. Products are made to order and are not returnable or resaleable.
    LOSS
    Lost measurement and specification records mean product fabricated to the wrong size, which is a total loss on custom goods plus a remeasure and refabrication at the retailer's cost.
    ASK
    How are in-home measurements recorded and transmitted to your fabricator?
  2. 02

    Motorised treatments are connected devices installed in customers' homes.

    WHY
    Motorised shades integrate with home automation platforms and are commissioned to a customer's network and account. Installers configure hubs and retain access for support. That access reaches inside the customer's home network.
    LOSS
    A compromise through installer access reaches customer home networks and, in homes with integrated security and cameras, creates a physical privacy exposure rather than a data one.
    ASK
    Do you commission motorised products onto customer home networks?
  3. 03

    Cord safety obligations carry recall and liability exposure.

    WHY
    Corded window coverings are subject to child strangulation safety standards, with requirements on stock products sold at retail. Order and specification records establish which products were supplied to which homes when a standard changes or a recall occurs.
    LOSS
    A child injury claim where the retailer cannot demonstrate which product was installed, or an obstructed recall where customer records are unavailable.
    ASK
    Could you identify which customers received a specific product model?
