Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 531311

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 531311, Residential Property Managers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold owners' money in trust accounts and disburse it monthly.

    WHY
    Property managers collect rent into trust accounts and disburse to owners and vendors on a monthly cycle, under state regulations requiring segregation, reconciliation, and audit. Owner banking details are held on file and change requests arrive by email.
    LOSS
    Diverted owner distributions or vendor payments, with the manager liable to restore trust funds and facing licence action for trust account shortfalls regardless of cause.
    ASK
    How are owner banking detail changes verified before a distribution is sent?
  2. 02

    Tenant files and payment data cover thousands of residents.

    WHY
    Managers hold applications with Social Security numbers, screening reports, lease documents, and stored payment methods for residents across many properties and owners. Screening reports are consumer reports subject to Fair Credit Reporting Act handling and disposal requirements.
    LOSS
    Breach notification across residents at every managed property simultaneously, plus FCRA exposure for consumer report data, with owners holding the manager responsible under the management agreement.
    ASK
    How many residents' files across how many properties are held in your system?
  3. 03

    Maintenance access means keys and codes to occupied homes.

    WHY
    Managers hold keys, access codes, and alarm information for units, coordinate vendor entry, and increasingly administer smart locks. Work order systems record when residents will not be home.
    LOSS
    Unauthorised entry to residents' homes using access information from your systems, which is a physical safety exposure and a direct claim against the manager and the owner.
    ASK
    Does your system hold access codes or smart lock control for occupied units?
