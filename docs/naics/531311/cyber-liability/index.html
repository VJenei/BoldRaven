Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 531311

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 531311, Residential Property Managers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold tenant funds and owner money.

    • Security deposits, rent, and owner distributions pass through your accounts.
    • Those funds are held in trust under state law.
    • A shortfall must be restored and affects your licence.
    ASK
    How are trust account disbursements authorised and reconciled?
  2. 02

    Tenant files hold everything needed for identity theft.

    • Applications carry social security numbers, income, and bank details.
    • Background and credit checks add more.
    • Every affected tenant and applicant must be notified.
    ASK
    How many tenant applications and files do you hold?
  3. 03

    Maintenance dispatch is a habitability obligation.

    • Repair requests are received and dispatched to vendors electronically.
    • Habitability issues carry legal deadlines in most states.
    • Tenants without heat or water have legal remedies immediately.
    ASK
    How are maintenance requests received and dispatched?
