Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 112310

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 112310, Chicken Egg Production, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    House environment controls keep the flock alive.

    • Ventilation, temperature, feed, and water are controlled automatically in every house.
    • A ventilation failure in a full house kills birds within hours.
    • House capacity means losses are counted in tens of thousands of birds.
    ASK
    Are your house ventilation and alarm systems automated?
  2. 02

    Egg safety rules require records that regulators inspect.

    • Salmonella testing, refrigeration, and rodent control records are required and inspected.
    • Eggs must be held and transported at a controlled temperature.
    • An outbreak without records recalls every egg you shipped.
    ASK
    What testing and refrigeration records do you keep for your egg operation?
  3. 03

    Grading, packing, and shipping run continuously.

    • Eggs are collected, graded, and packed by automated equipment every day.
    • Retail customers receive shipments on fixed daily or weekly schedules.
    • Hens keep laying whether or not the packing line runs.
    ASK
    How many days of egg production could you hold if your packing line stopped?
