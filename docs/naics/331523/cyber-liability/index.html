Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 331523

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 331523, Nonferrous Metal Die-Casting Foundries, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Die casting machines operate at high pressure with molten metal.

    WHY
    Die casting injects molten aluminium, zinc, or magnesium at very high pressure into steel dies, with machine control governing shot profile, pressure, and cycle. Molten metal ejection from a die failure is a severe burn hazard.
    LOSS
    Molten metal ejection injuring operators, plus die damage requiring repair or replacement of tooling that is expensive and slow to produce.
    ASK
    How are die casting machine parameters and safety interlocks controlled?
  2. 02

    Process parameters are qualified per part and per die.

    WHY
    Each part has a qualified shot profile, metal temperature, die temperature, and cycle producing acceptable porosity and dimensions. Automotive customers approve those parameters through production part approval.
    LOSS
    Loss of qualified parameters requiring requalification with customers, with automotive approval processes taking weeks and production halted throughout.
    ASK
    How many qualified process parameter sets are stored in your machine controllers?
  3. 03

    Automotive customers charge for line stoppages.

    WHY
    Die castings are components in automotive assemblies delivered just in time with dedicated tooling. Assembly lines run to schedule and stop without components.
    LOSS
    Automotive line-down charges that are contractual, substantial, and applied per hour regardless of the component value, plus expedited freight to recover.
    ASK
    What do your automotive customers charge per hour for a line stoppage?
