Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 238330

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 238330, Flooring Contractors, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Material is ordered by lot and cannot be mixed.

    • Flooring is ordered from matched dye or production lots for consistency.
    • Lot records reserve material for a specific job.
    • Material from another lot is visibly different across a floor.
    ASK
    How is material lot matching recorded and reserved for your jobs?
  2. 02

    Field measurements drive every cut and order.

    • Rooms are measured and material ordered to those dimensions.
    • Wrong measurements mean wrong quantities or wrong cuts.
    • Reordering a matched lot may not be possible at all.
    ASK
    How are field measurements captured and used for ordering?
  3. 03

    Installation is scheduled near the end of a project.

    • Flooring goes in late in the sequence with little schedule float.
    • Crews, material, and site readiness are coordinated together.
    • A delay at your point pushes occupancy directly.
    ASK
    How much schedule float is left at your installation point?
