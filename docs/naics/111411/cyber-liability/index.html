Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111411

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111411, Mushroom Production, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Growing rooms and compost tunnels are under continuous computer control.

    WHY
    Mushroom production uses phase II and III composting tunnels held at set temperatures for pasteurization and conditioning, then growing rooms controlled for air temperature, compost temperature, humidity, CO2, and air exchange. Flushes are triggered by controlled environmental changes. The building supplies the entire growing environment.
    LOSS
    Hours of lost environmental control aborts a flush and can contaminate the substrate. This costs the crop, the compost fill, and the room turn, commonly $50,000 to $200,000 per room. Rooms cycle every few weeks.
    ASK
    Are your growing room and tunnel controls accessible remotely?
  2. 02

    Mushrooms have a documented Listeria recall history.

    WHY
    Enoki and other mushrooms have been subject to repeated multistate Listeria monocytogenes recalls and import alerts. Traceback uses lot codes, grow room records, and distribution data. Listeria is an environmental pathogen, so investigators review facility records in addition to product records.
    LOSS
    Recall expense, destruction of distributed product, crisis management, and third-party bodily injury claims. Recall and crisis response costs are covered under cyber and recall policies, not farm property policies.
    ASK
    Can you identify which rooms and dates a specific shipment came from?
  3. 03

    Hand harvest runs 52 weeks a year with continuous payroll.

    WHY
    Mushrooms are picked by hand indoors year round using large permanent and seasonal crews on piece rate. Unlike field crops, the employee file is active every week of the year and includes I-9 forms, Social Security numbers, and direct deposit details.
    LOSS
    Breach notification across a large standing workforce, plus disruption to a weekly payroll with no seasonal pause to absorb the interruption.
    ASK
    How many employees are on your payroll year round?
