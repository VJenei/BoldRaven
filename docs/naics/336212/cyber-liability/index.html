Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 336212

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 336212, Truck Trailer Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Trailers carry loads on public roads and their failures cause crashes.

    WHY
    Trailers must meet Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards for brakes, lighting, underride protection, and structure. Brake, coupling, or structural failures on the highway involve a loaded trailer at speed.
    LOSS
    A brake or structural defect causing highway crashes, with an NHTSA recall across every trailer of the affected production and liability for the collisions themselves.
    ASK
    How is conformance to safety standards documented for each trailer built?
  2. 02

    Refrigerated trailers protect food in transit.

    WHY
    Refrigerated trailers maintain temperature for food and pharmaceutical loads, with FSMA sanitary transportation requirements obliging carriers to control and, where agreed, record temperature during transport.
    LOSS
    Refrigeration or monitoring defects allowing temperature excursions, with food loads condemned and carriers unable to demonstrate temperature control under their FSMA obligations.
    ASK
    Do your trailers include refrigeration and temperature monitoring systems?
  3. 03

    Trailers are built to order with long backlogs.

    WHY
    Trailers are configured to fleet specifications with orders placed months ahead and production scheduled against delivery dates fleets plan capacity around.
    LOSS
    Production halted with delivery dates missed across a backlog, and fleet customers unable to add the capacity they contracted freight against.
    ASK
    How far ahead are your production slots committed?
