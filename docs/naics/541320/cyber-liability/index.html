Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541320

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541320, Landscape Architectural Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Site design records support permits and long-term obligations.

    WHY
    Landscape architects produce grading, drainage, planting, and irrigation designs that support permits, stormwater approvals, and maintenance obligations running for years after construction. Regulatory approvals reference the approved drawings.
    LOSS
    Inability to produce the approved design in a permit compliance dispute or a drainage failure claim, where the record determines whether the design or the construction was at fault.
    ASK
    How long are your approved site plans and permit documentation retained?
  2. 02

    Drainage and grading design failures cause property damage.

    WHY
    Site grading and stormwater design determine where water goes. Errors cause flooding of adjacent property, structural damage, and regulatory violations, and claims arise years after completion when a large storm reveals the deficiency.
    LOSS
    Flood damage claims from the owner and neighbouring properties, defended only with the design calculations and approved drawings that either exist or do not.
    ASK
    Where are your drainage calculations and approved grading plans stored?
  3. 03

    Project deliverables are tied to construction and permitting schedules.

    WHY
    Design deliverables feed permit submissions and construction sequencing, with review cycles at agencies that have their own calendars. Missing a submission date can delay a project by a full review cycle.
    LOSS
    Delayed permitting pushing construction into an unfavourable season, with the owner claiming the resulting cost against the design team.
    ASK
    What agency submission deadlines depend on your deliverables?
