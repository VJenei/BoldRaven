Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311911

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311911, Roasted Nuts and Peanut Butter Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    This industry produced a salmonella outbreak that led to criminal convictions.

    WHY
    A peanut processing company knowingly shipped salmonella-contaminated product, causing an outbreak with deaths and resulting in criminal convictions and a 28-year prison sentence for its chief executive. Testing records and shipping decisions were the evidence.
    LOSS
    A salmonella outbreak from a low moisture product with long shelf life, distributed as an ingredient into hundreds of downstream products, with criminal exposure where testing records show what was known.
    ASK
    How are pathogen testing results recorded against lots, and who decides to release product?
  2. 02

    Peanut and tree nut allergens are the most severe.

    WHY
    Peanut and tree nut allergies cause the majority of fatal food anaphylaxis, and facilities processing both must control cross-contact rigorously. Undeclared peanut or tree nut in a product is among the most dangerous labelling errors possible.
    LOSS
    An anaphylaxis death from undeclared peanut or tree nut, with recall, litigation, and regulatory response at the highest level of severity.
    ASK
    How is cross-contact controlled between peanut and tree nut production?
  3. 03

    Aflatoxin testing is required and traceable to farm lots.

    WHY
    Peanuts and tree nuts carry aflatoxin risk with FDA action levels and mandatory testing at processing. Positive results trace back through buying points to specific farmer stock lots.
    LOSS
    Aflatoxin above action levels in distributed product, with recall and inability to trace back to source lots preventing any recovery from the supplier and expanding the recall scope.
    ASK
    How do you document aflatoxin testing against incoming lots?
