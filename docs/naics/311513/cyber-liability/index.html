Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311513

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311513, Cheese Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Cheese making runs on timed biological steps.

    • Culture, coagulation, cutting, and cooking run to precise time and temperature schedules.
    • A vat that misses its schedule produces cheese that cannot be sold as intended.
    • The vat cannot be paused once culture is added.
    ASK
    Is your cheesemaking run by automated time and temperature controls?
  2. 02

    Listeria and pathogen control determine whether cheese ships.

    • Environmental monitoring, sanitation, and pasteurisation records are inspected.
    • Cheese recalls for listeria have caused deaths, particularly in soft cheeses.
    • An outbreak without traceability recalls your whole production history.
    ASK
    What environmental monitoring records do you keep for listeria?
  3. 03

    Aged cheese represents months of held inventory.

    • Cheese ages in controlled rooms for weeks to years before sale.
    • Room temperature and humidity are controlled continuously.
    • A control failure ruins inventory representing months of production.
    ASK
    Are your aging rooms run by automated temperature and humidity controls?
