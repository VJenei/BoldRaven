Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 212390

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 212390, Other Nonmetallic Mineral Mining and Quarrying, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Specialty mineral customers qualify a source and hold it to specification.

    WHY
    Phosphate, potash, borate, gypsum, salt, and similar operations sell to industrial customers who qualify the source and test every delivery. Certificates of analysis, lot traceability, and specification records are held in laboratory and quality systems and are a condition of purchase.
    LOSS
    Rejected shipments and possible disqualification of the source, which for an industrial mineral customer is a lengthy requalification process.
    ASK
    Do your customers require a certificate of analysis with each shipment?
  2. 02

    Extraction and processing depend on continuous automated plants.

    WHY
    Solution mining, evaporation ponds, flotation, and thermal drying all run under automated control with pond level, brine chemistry, and dryer temperature managed continuously. Evaporation and solution circuits in particular respond over weeks, not hours.
    LOSS
    Loss of pond or brine control causes off-specification product and, if ponds overtop, a reportable environmental release with remediation cost.
    ASK
    Do you operate ponds or solution circuits with automated level and chemistry control?
  3. 03

    Mine safety and environmental compliance are documented obligations.

    WHY
    MSHA governs training records, workplace examinations, and equipment inspections at every mine site regardless of size. Environmental permits carry monitoring and reporting duties. All of it is maintained electronically and produced on demand during inspection.
    LOSS
    Citations and penalties for records that cannot be produced, including training records where the default assumption is that training did not occur.
    ASK
    How do you store MSHA training records and workplace examination logs?
