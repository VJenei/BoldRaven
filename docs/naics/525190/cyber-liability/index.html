Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 525190

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 525190, Other Insurance Funds, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Self-insured and captive arrangements hold claim data directly.

    WHY
    Funds in this category retain risk and administer claims for their members or participants, holding claimant medical records, wage information, and settlement details without a carrier standing between them and the data.
    LOSS
    Breach exposing claimant medical and financial records held directly by the fund, with notification obligations and, for health-related claims, potential HIPAA exposure depending on the arrangement.
    ASK
    Does the fund hold claimant medical records directly rather than through a carrier?
  2. 02

    Reserve adequacy depends on data that must be reliable.

    WHY
    Funds set reserves and contributions based on claim history, exposure data, and actuarial analysis. Regulators and members rely on those calculations, and errors accumulate quietly until the fund is underfunded.
    LOSS
    Reserve calculations that cannot be produced or verified, triggering regulatory examination and, where inadequacy is found, member assessments to restore solvency.
    ASK
    How is your claim and exposure data maintained for actuarial analysis?
  3. 03

    Member assessments and distributions move real money.

    WHY
    Funds collect contributions from and distribute to members based on formulas applied to participation and loss experience. Payment instructions and banking details are held for each member organisation.
    LOSS
    Diverted contributions or distributions, with the fund liable to members for amounts that were paid to a fraudulent account, and governance scrutiny from the member board.
    ASK
    How are member banking details verified before a distribution is made?
