Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 312130

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 312130, Wineries, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Harvest and fermentation happen once a year and cannot be repeated.

    WHY
    Crush and fermentation occur during a compressed harvest window, with fruit arriving at optimal ripeness and fermentation proceeding under temperature control over days to weeks. The vintage happens once and the fruit does not wait.
    LOSS
    Fermentation lost or spoiled during harvest, representing an entire vintage of specific vineyard lots that cannot be replaced until the following year, with the wine that would have been made simply absent.
    ASK
    How much of your annual production is committed during the harvest window?
  2. 02

    Direct to consumer sales hold stored payment credentials.

    WHY
    Wine clubs bill members quarterly to stored cards, alongside tasting room point of sale and e-commerce with shipping compliance data including dates of birth and signatures. Club membership is the most valuable asset in a DTC winery.
    LOSS
    Stored card compromise across a club membership built over a decade, with notification, card brand liability, and cancellation of the recurring revenue the winery depends on.
    ASK
    How many club members have payment methods stored for recurring shipments?
  3. 03

    Label claims must be substantiated for TTB.

    WHY
    Vintage, varietal, appellation, and vineyard designate claims must be supported by records of fruit source, tonnage, and blending, available for TTB audit. Those claims determine the price the bottle commands.
    LOSS
    Inability to substantiate label claims in an audit requiring relabelling or destruction of bottled inventory, on wine whose value depends entirely on the claims made.
    ASK
    Could you substantiate the appellation and vintage claims on your current release?
