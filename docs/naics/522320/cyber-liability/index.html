Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 522320

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 522320, Financial Transactions Processing, Reserve, and Clearinghouse Activities, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    When a clearinghouse goes down, an entire industry stops transacting.

    WHY
    The February 2024 ransomware attack on Change Healthcare, which processes a very large share of US healthcare transactions, left providers and pharmacies unable to verify coverage or submit claims for weeks, and the associated breach affected approximately 190 million people. Processors and clearinghouses are single points through which enormous transaction volumes flow.
    LOSS
    Every client is down simultaneously with no alternative path. Claims from clients for their own business interruption vastly exceed processing revenue, and the breach notification population is measured in the tens of millions.
    ASK
    How many client organisations would be unable to transact if your platform stopped?
  2. 02

    You hold transaction data for parties who never chose you.

    WHY
    Processors handle data belonging to their clients' customers, including payment credentials, account details, and in healthcare, protected health information. Those individuals have no relationship with the processor and often do not know it exists.
    LOSS
    Notification obligations flowing through every client relationship simultaneously, with the processor bearing the cost under contractual indemnities and regulators treating it as the responsible party.
    ASK
    Whose customers' data passes through your systems, and under what agreements?
  3. 03

    Settlement finality depends on records that must reconcile exactly.

    WHY
    Clearing and settlement functions net obligations between participants and move funds on a settlement cycle. Positions must reconcile precisely, and settlement finality means transfers cannot simply be reversed once complete.
    LOSS
    Unreconcilable settlement positions across participants, where the disputes cannot be resolved from any other source and the financial exposure sits with the processor until they are.
    ASK
    How are settlement positions independently verifiable if your primary records are unavailable?
