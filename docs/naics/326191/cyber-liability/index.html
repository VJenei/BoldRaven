Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 326191

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 326191, Plastics Plumbing Fixture Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Fixtures hold water in occupied buildings.

    • Tubs, tanks, and fixtures contain water under use and pressure.
    • Cracking or failure floods the building continuously.
    • Water damage claims come from every installation affected.
    ASK
    How are your material and process records maintained per production lot?
  2. 02

    Drinking water contact carries lead and material requirements.

    • Components contacting drinking water are subject to lead and material certification.
    • Plumbing codes require that certification for installation.
    • Non-compliant product cannot lawfully be installed.
    ASK
    What drinking water contact certifications do your products carry?
  3. 03

    Products are consumer goods with recall obligations.

    • Plumbing fixtures sold to consumers fall under consumer product safety jurisdiction.
    • Reporting obligations apply once a hazard is known.
    • Recall reach depends on distribution records.
    ASK
    Could you identify which distributors received a specific production run?
