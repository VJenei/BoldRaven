Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 115210

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 115210, Support Activities for Animal Production, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    The business connects to many client farms' systems.

    WHY
    Veterinary services, hoof trimmers, AI technicians, shearers, breeders, and boarding operations connect to client herd management systems, exchange data files, and are often granted portal access. A compromise originating with the service provider can reach multiple client operations.
    LOSS
    Contractual and third-party liability when a compromise propagates to client systems, plus loss of the client relationships the business depends on.
    ASK
    How many client farm systems or portals do you have access to?
  2. 02

    The business maintains client treatment and withdrawal records.

    WHY
    Service providers maintain individual animal treatment records, drug administration and withdrawal times, breeding and genetic data, and client billing information including stored cards. Withdrawal records carry direct FDA and FSIS residue enforcement consequences for the client.
    LOSS
    A client ships an animal under withdrawal because treatment records were unavailable, resulting in a residue violation and a claim against the service provider.
    ASK
    Do you maintain drug treatment and withdrawal records on behalf of your clients?
  3. 03

    Boarding and training operations hold client payment and personal information.

    WHY
    Operations providing boarding, training, and animal services maintain client contact information, emergency contacts, stored payment cards for recurring billing, signed waivers, and information on minor participants in youth programs.
    LOSS
    Card and personal information breach with notification obligations and card brand assessments. This liability is excluded or sublimited on a standard package policy.
    ASK
    Do you store client payment cards or records for minor participants?
