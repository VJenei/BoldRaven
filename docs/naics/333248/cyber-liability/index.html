Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333248

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333248, All Other Industrial Machinery Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Machines are engineered to order for one customer process.

    WHY
    Industrial machinery in this category is designed for a specific customer application, with engineering, control code, and documentation produced once. Nothing is stock and the design file is the machine.
    LOSS
    Loss of engineering and control code for machines in build and in the field, halting production and leaving installed machines unsupportable when they need a change or repair.
    ASK
    How many machine designs and control programmes exist only on your systems?
  2. 02

    Machines ship with control systems you wrote and support.

    WHY
    Industrial machines include PLCs, drives, and HMIs programmed by the manufacturer, frequently with remote access for commissioning and support that reaches into customer plant networks.
    LOSS
    Remote support access used to reach customer networks, with production stopped at multiple plants and the manufacturer identified as the common entry point.
    ASK
    How many customer sites do you retain remote access to?
  3. 03

    Machine safety functions prevent operator injury.

    WHY
    Guarding, interlocks, light curtains, and safety-rated control functions stop motion when a person is exposed. Those functions are designed, validated, and documented by the machine builder.
    LOSS
    A safety function defect permitting motion with a person exposed, causing serious injury, with the machine builder's validation records examined in the investigation and every machine of the design affected.
    ASK
    How are safety-rated control functions validated and documented per machine?
