CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 326212, Tire Retreading, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Retread failures cause crashes and highway debris.

    WHY
    Retreaded tires are used extensively on commercial vehicles, with casing inspection, buffing, and curing determining whether the retread holds. Tread separation on a highway causes loss of control and debris that damages other vehicles.
    LOSS
    Retread separations causing crashes and injuries, with the retreader's inspection and process records determining whether the casing should have been retreaded and whether the process was properly controlled.
    ASK
    How are casing inspection results and cure parameters recorded per tire?
  2. 02

    Casing history determines whether a tire can be retreaded.

    WHY
    Casings are inspected for damage, previous repairs, and age before retreading, with rejection criteria determining suitability. Fleet customers track their casings and expect them returned or accounted for.
    LOSS
    Unsuitable casings retreaded and returned to service, with failures in fleet operation, plus disputes with fleet customers over casings that cannot be accounted for.
    ASK
    How do you track individual customer casings through your process?
  3. 03

    Fleet customers depend on turnaround and casing accounting.

    WHY
    Commercial fleets send casings for retreading and expect return within a turnaround window, tracking their casing inventory as an asset. Trucks wait on tires and fleets manage casing pools closely.
    LOSS
    Casing tracking lost with fleets unable to reconcile their asset pool, and turnaround failures leaving trucks without tires, which for a fleet is equipment out of revenue service.
    ASK
    How many customer casings are in your plant at any time?
