Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 621330

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 621330, Offices of Mental Health Practitioners (except Physicians), needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Therapy records contain what patients tell no one else.

    WHY
    Counsellors, psychologists, and therapists hold session notes covering trauma, relationships, substance use, sexuality, and suicidality for identified individuals. The therapeutic relationship depends entirely on confidentiality, and patients disclose on that basis.
    LOSS
    Patient harm from disclosure affecting employment, custody, immigration, and personal relationships, with claims reflecting those consequences and a practice whose patients cannot continue treatment after a confidentiality failure.
    ASK
    What would happen to your patients if their session notes became public?
  2. 02

    Substance use disorder records carry stricter federal protection.

    WHY
    Records from federally assisted substance use disorder programmes are protected under 42 CFR Part 2, which restricts disclosure more tightly than HIPAA and requires specific patient consent for most disclosures, with its own penalties.
    LOSS
    Part 2 violations in addition to HIPAA exposure, with the regime specifically designed to prevent disclosures that deter people from seeking treatment.
    ASK
    Do you provide substance use disorder treatment subject to 42 CFR Part 2?
  3. 03

    Solo and small practices have no security function at all.

    WHY
    Mental health practice is frequently solo or small group, with records on a laptop or a cloud EHR selected without security review, no IT support, and telehealth conducted over consumer platforms.
    LOSS
    A single device compromise exposing an entire caseload, with the practitioner personally responsible for notification, OCR response, and licensing board consequences.
    ASK
    How many patients' records are accessible from the device you are using right now?
