Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 512199

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 512199, Other Motion Picture and Video Industries, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold client content before it is released.

    • Material arrives from producers and rights holders ahead of release.
    • Release plans are built around that timing.
    • A leak destroys the release and breaches your agreements.
    ASK
    What unreleased client content is stored on your systems?
  2. 02

    Rights and licensing records govern what you may do.

    • Territory, term, and media rights determine permitted use.
    • Payments and royalties follow those records.
    • Wrong records mean acting without rights.
    ASK
    Where are your rights and licensing records held?
  3. 03

    Delivery dates are contractual and fixed.

    • Clients schedule releases and campaigns around your delivery.
    • Marketing and distribution are committed to those dates.
    • A missed date has consequences beyond your fee.
    ASK
    What delivery commitments do your client agreements carry?
