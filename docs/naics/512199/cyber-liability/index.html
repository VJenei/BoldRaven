Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 512199

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 512199, Other Motion Picture and Video Industries, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Archives and libraries hold content whose value is exclusivity.

    WHY
    Businesses in this category hold film libraries, stock footage, and archival masters licensed repeatedly over decades. The value depends on controlling access. Once material circulates freely the licensing revenue is permanently reduced.
    LOSS
    Exfiltration of library content permanently undermines a revenue stream that would have continued for decades, and unlike ransomware the loss is not reversed by restoring a backup.
    ASK
    What is the annual licensing revenue from content you hold exclusively?
  2. 02

    Rights and clearance records determine what can lawfully be licensed.

    WHY
    Licensing depends on documented chain of title, talent and music clearances, and territory restrictions accumulated over the life of the material. Without clearance records, content cannot be licensed because the licensee will not accept the risk.
    LOSS
    Library content that becomes unlicensable because clearances cannot be evidenced, which removes its commercial value entirely even though the material still exists.
    ASK
    Where are the chain of title and clearance records for your library held?
  3. 03

    Client and licensee data supports the commercial relationships.

    WHY
    Licensing operations hold licensee agreements, negotiated rates, usage reports, and royalty calculations across many counterparties. Rate structures are commercially sensitive across a client base that talks to each other.
    LOSS
    Exposure of differential pricing across licensees damages every negotiation simultaneously, and lost usage records mean royalties that cannot be calculated or collected.
    ASK
    How are licensee usage reports and royalty calculations maintained?
