Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 238140

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 238140, Masonry Contractors, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Material is ordered to a specific colour, blend, and lot.

    WHY
    Brick and stone are ordered by colour range and blend, with sample panels approved by the architect and material reserved from a specific run. Late additions from a different lot do not match. Approval records and order details tie the delivered material to the approved sample.
    LOSS
    Mismatched masonry on a visible elevation is rejected by the architect and must be taken down and rebuilt, which is entirely rework at the mason's cost.
    ASK
    How do you record approved sample panels and material lot information?
  2. 02

    Scaffold and mast climber engineering is documented and inspected.

    WHY
    Masonry work depends on scaffold, mast climbing work platforms, and hoists erected to engineered drawings with load limits, inspected before each shift and documented. Scaffold collapse is a mass casualty event.
    LOSS
    A collapse with multiple fatalities, investigated against the engineered drawings and inspection records that either exist or do not.
    ASK
    How are your scaffold engineering drawings and daily inspection records maintained?
  3. 03

    Payment applications and lien deadlines are unforgiving.

    WHY
    Masonry subcontractors bill monthly with lien waivers, and mechanics lien rights expire on statutory deadlines measured from last furnishing of labour or material. Missing a preliminary notice or lien deadline forfeits the security entirely.
    LOSS
    Loss of lien rights on an unpaid balance because the dates and notice records were unavailable. On a commercial masonry package that can be several hundred thousand dollars with no recourse.
    ASK
    How do you track lien notice and filing deadlines across your active projects?
