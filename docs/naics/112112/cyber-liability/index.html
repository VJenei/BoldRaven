Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 112112

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 112112, Cattle Feedlots, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Custom yards hold cattle owned by others and maintain their records.

    WHY
    Custom feedlots feed cattle owned by investors, ranchers, and packers. Ownership records, feed billing, health treatment records, and closeout accounting are tracked by lot. The yard is a bailee responsible for the animals and for the records that establish ownership and performance.
    LOSS
    Third-party liability to cattle owners for lost performance data, disputed closeouts, and inability to document ownership, plus liability for exposing owners' financial information. Farm policies do not cover third-party data liability.
    ASK
    What percentage of the cattle in your yard are owned by customers rather than the yard?
  2. 02

    Feed is formulated and delivered by computerized batching systems.

    WHY
    Feedlots use computerized batching and delivery systems such as Micro Technologies and Performance Beef to control ration formulation, mixing accuracy, and bunk calls twice daily. Cattle on high-concentrate finishing rations develop acidosis and bloat if feedings are missed or batches are wrong.
    LOSS
    A batching failure across a 20,000 head yard causes death loss and performance setback. Finished cattle are worth $2,200 to $2,800 per head, so a 1 percent death loss is approximately $500,000.
    ASK
    What software do you use for ration formulation and feed delivery?
  3. 03

    Yards move large amounts of money weekly by wire and ACH.

    WHY
    Feedlots purchase feeder cattle, sell finished cattle to packers, buy commodity feed, and maintain futures and options positions with brokers. Payment traffic is high in both frequency and value and is arranged by email.
    LOSS
    Diverted purchase or settlement wires of $250,000 to $2M, or compromise of hedging account credentials resulting in unauthorized positions.
    ASK
    How many wire transfers does your office send in a typical week?
