Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 332618

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 332618, Other Fabricated Wire Product Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Wire products carry load ratings in lifting and safety applications.

    WHY
    Wire rope slings, chain, mesh, and fabricated wire products are used in lifting, fall protection, and guarding with rated capacities. Failure in lifting drops loads onto people.
    LOSS
    A sling or rope failure dropping a load with fatal injury, and the manufacturer's proof testing and material records determining whether the product ever met its rating.
    ASK
    Are your products used in lifting or fall protection with rated capacities?
  2. 02

    Proof testing and certification support the rating.

    WHY
    Lifting products are proof tested with certificates issued, and users rely on those ratings when selecting equipment for a load. Certificates accompany the product and are checked during inspections.
    LOSS
    Products rated without proper testing, or certificates that cannot be reproduced, with every unit from the affected production requiring withdrawal from service.
    ASK
    How are proof test results recorded and linked to individual products?
  3. 03

    Fabrication is to customer specification with dedicated setups.

    WHY
    Wire products are fabricated to customer dimensions, mesh, and configuration with tooling and setups specific to each product. Customers design equipment and guarding around the specification.
    LOSS
    Wrong specification production unusable by the customer, with their equipment or installation delayed and material scrapped.
    ASK
    What proportion of your production is made to individual customer specification?
