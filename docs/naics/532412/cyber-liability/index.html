Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 532412

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 532412, Construction, Mining, and Forestry Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Machines are connected to telematics platforms.

    • Fleet telematics reports location, hours, and condition continuously.
    • Some platforms support remote disabling.
    • That access reaches machines on active job sites.
    ASK
    What can be done to a machine through your telematics platform?
  2. 02

    Equipment is booked for a specific job and day.

    • Machines are reserved and delivered against project schedules.
    • Crews and other equipment are scheduled around them.
    • A missed delivery idles a crew for the day.
    ASK
    How are your reservations and deliveries scheduled?
  3. 03

    Inspection records matter after an accident.

    • Inspection and maintenance records are kept per machine.
    • Those records are examined when equipment injures someone.
    • Records you cannot produce are a defence you do not have.
    ASK
    How are your equipment inspection and maintenance records maintained?
