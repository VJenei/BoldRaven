Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 445131

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 445131, Convenience Retailers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Fuel dispensers and tank gauges are the most attacked equipment in retail.

    WHY
    Convenience sites run automatic tank gauges, dispenser payment terminals, and site controllers. Researchers have repeatedly found internet-exposed tank gauges across US fuel retail with little or no authentication, and payment card skimming at the pump remains one of the most persistent fraud problems in retail.
    LOSS
    Skimmed card data at the pump generates card brand assessments and fraud liability, while manipulated tank gauge data can mask a leak, which is a reportable environmental release with remediation cost.
    ASK
    Are your tank gauges or site controllers reachable from outside the site?
  2. 02

    Age-restricted sales are enforced by compliance checks.

    WHY
    Tobacco, alcohol, and nicotine sales require age verification at the point of sale, with electronic ID scanning and register prompts creating the record that a check occurred. States and FDA conduct undercover compliance checks and act on failures.
    LOSS
    Licence suspension for failed compliance checks removes the categories that drive both traffic and margin, and repeated failures put the fuel and lottery licences at risk too.
    ASK
    How does your point of sale enforce and record age verification?
  3. 03

    Small format stores hold cash, lottery, and card data in one system.

    WHY
    Convenience sites concentrate cash handling, lottery terminals, money transfer services, and card processing in a small operation with limited staff and often no dedicated IT support. Money services activity brings its own recordkeeping obligations.
    LOSS
    A compromise reaching payment and money services data at a business with no capacity to fund forensic investigation or notification, and the merchant agreement makes it the retailer's cost.
    ASK
    Do you offer money transfer or check cashing services alongside retail?
