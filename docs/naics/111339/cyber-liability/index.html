Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111339

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111339, Other Noncitrus Fruit Farming, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Tropical tree fruits are on the FDA Food Traceability List.

    WHY
    Papaya, mango, and other tropical tree fruits are covered by FSMA Section 204, with a compliance date of July 20, 2028. The rule requires traceability lot codes, Key Data Elements at Critical Tracking Events, and delivery of an electronic sortable spreadsheet to FDA within 24 hours of request. Papaya has a documented outbreak history.
    LOSS
    Records that cannot be produced within 24 hours widen the scope of a traceback and place the operation at the center of the advisory. Costs include recall expense, crisis management, and lost market access.
    ASK
    Do you grow papaya, mango, or another crop on the FDA Food Traceability List?
  2. 02

    Stone fruit requires rapid cooling and continuous cold chain monitoring.

    WHY
    Peaches, nectarines, plums, cherries, and apricots require hydrocooling or forced-air cooling within hours of picking and continuous cold chain to retail. Cooling and transport are monitored electronically. Cherry harvest windows are measured in days.
    LOSS
    A cooling or monitoring failure writes off a full day of harvest. On cherries, one lost week can represent the majority of the crop's marketable value.
    ASK
    How soon after picking does your fruit enter cooling, and is that process monitored electronically?
  3. 03

    Fruit clubs and direct shipping involve stored card data and recurring billing.

    WHY
    Noncitrus fruit growers sell through u-pick, farm stands, fruit-of-the-month shipping clubs, and online ordering. Shipping clubs require stored cards and recurring billing. Farm stands process high transaction volume in a short season. PCI DSS 4.0 became mandatory on March 31, 2025.
    LOSS
    Card compromise with brand assessments, forensics, and reissuance costs, plus loss of customers in a business built on direct relationships.
    ASK
    Do you operate a shipping club or subscription that stores customer cards?
