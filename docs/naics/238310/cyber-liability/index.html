Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 238310

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 238310, Drywall and Insulation Contractors, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Fire rated assemblies must be built exactly as designed.

    • Rated wall and ceiling assemblies specify materials, spacing, and detailing.
    • Inspectors verify assemblies against the listed design before cover.
    • A wrong assembly must be opened and rebuilt after inspection.
    ASK
    How are rated assembly details issued and verified before cover?
  2. 02

    Crews and material are scheduled across many jobs.

    • Crew assignments, board deliveries, and inspections run on one schedule.
    • Your work gates finishing trades behind it.
    • A scheduling failure idles crews and delays completion.
    ASK
    What software holds your crew schedules and job assignments?
  3. 03

    Weekly payroll runs for a large field workforce.

    • Hours and piece counts are captured in the field and paid weekly.
    • Crews do not return to a job that did not pay.
    • A missed payroll costs the workforce, not just the week.
    ASK
    How are field hours and piece counts captured and paid?
