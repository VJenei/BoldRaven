Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 238110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 238110, Poured Concrete Foundation and Structure Contractors, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    A pour cannot be paused once it starts.

    • Concrete is ordered, batched, and delivered to a scheduled pour time.
    • Trucks, pumps, and crews are all committed to that window.
    • Concrete that arrives with nowhere to go sets in the truck.
    ASK
    How are your concrete orders and pour schedules placed and confirmed?
  2. 02

    Placement drawings and layouts drive the pour.

    • Rebar layouts, embed locations, and dimensions come from issued drawings.
    • Concrete placed to a superseded drawing has to be demolished.
    • Demolition of structural concrete is measured in weeks.
    ASK
    How are drawing revisions controlled before a pour?
  3. 03

    Test results certify the structure years later.

    • Cylinder breaks, mix designs, and placement records certify strength.
    • Those records are examined if the structure is ever questioned.
    • Without records the concrete cannot be shown to have met specification.
    ASK
    How are your concrete test results and placement records retained?
