Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 524130

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 524130, Reinsurance Carriers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Cedent data covers entire books of business.

    • Bordereaux and claims data cover thousands of underlying policyholders.
    • That data belongs to the ceding company and their policyholders.
    • A breach exposes people who never dealt with you.
    ASK
    What cedent and underlying policyholder data do you hold?
  2. 02

    Treaty and pricing information is highly confidential.

    • Treaty terms, pricing, and exposure data determine competitive position.
    • Cedents and brokers share that data in confidence.
    • Disclosure damages relationships built over decades.
    ASK
    Where are your treaty and pricing records held?
  3. 03

    Settlements move very large sums by transfer.

    • Premium and claim settlements move at treaty value.
    • Instructions are exchanged with cedents and brokers electronically.
    • A diverted settlement is a very large single loss.
    ASK
    How are settlement instructions verified before funds move?
