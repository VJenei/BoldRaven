Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 811192

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 811192, Car Washes, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Unlimited wash memberships are recurring billing at scale.

    WHY
    Car washes operate monthly membership programmes billed automatically to stored payment methods, with vehicle recognition at the gate linking the plate to the account. Membership bases run into thousands per site.
    LOSS
    Compromise of stored payment credentials across the membership base, with card brand assessments and a cancellation wave that removes the recurring revenue the site was financed against.
    ASK
    How many members have payment methods stored for monthly billing?
  2. 02

    Tunnel controls and equipment are automated and can damage vehicles.

    WHY
    Conveyor tunnels run automated equipment sequenced against vehicle position and profile, with sensors preventing contact with mirrors, spoilers, and open windows. Control errors cause vehicle damage at speed.
    LOSS
    Vehicle damage across multiple customers before the fault is identified, with claims for repair and, in a tunnel, potential injury exposure if a vehicle is occupied.
    ASK
    How is your tunnel control system monitored and who can change its settings?
  3. 03

    Licence plate recognition creates a vehicle movement record.

    WHY
    Membership systems read licence plates at entry to identify accounts, building a record of which vehicles visited which sites and when. That data links plates to identified account holders and their locations over time.
    LOSS
    Exposure of plate recognition data creates a vehicle movement history for identified individuals, which is a surveillance-grade dataset customers did not expect a car wash to hold.
    ASK
    How long do you retain licence plate recognition records?
