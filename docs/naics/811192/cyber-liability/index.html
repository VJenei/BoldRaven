Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 811192

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 811192, Car Washes, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Equipment controls run the tunnel automatically.

    • Conveyors, brushes, and chemical dosing run on automated controls.
    • Vehicles are in the tunnel with people and equipment moving.
    • A control failure damages vehicles or injures someone.
    ASK
    How are your tunnel and equipment controls operated?
  2. 02

    Unlimited wash plans hold recurring payment data.

    • Membership plans carry customer contact and payment card details.
    • Recurring billing runs automatically month after month.
    • Every affected member must be notified.
    ASK
    How many membership accounts with payment details do you hold?
  3. 03

    Card payments run continuously at unattended terminals.

    • Payment terminals process cards, often with no staff present.
    • Card security requirements apply to every terminal.
    • Unattended terminals are targeted with skimming devices.
    ASK
    How are your payment terminals secured and inspected?
