Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561422

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561422, Telemarketing Bureaus and Other Contact Centers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Agents handle payment card and personal data on live calls.

    WHY
    Contact centres take orders, payments, and account changes on behalf of clients, with agents entering card data and personal information into client systems. PCI DSS applies to any environment where cardholder data is handled, and agent workstations and call recordings are in scope.
    LOSS
    Card data captured from agent screens or recordings, with brand assessments and forensic costs flowing to the client and contractually back to the centre, plus notification across every affected consumer.
    ASK
    Do your agents handle payment card data, and are calls recorded while they do?
  2. 02

    Telemarketing is heavily regulated with per-call statutory damages.

    WHY
    Outbound calling is governed by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act and the Telemarketing Sales Rule, with do-not-call obligations, consent requirements, and statutory damages per violating call. Consent records are the entire defence.
    LOSS
    Statutory damages multiplied across a calling campaign where consent and do-not-call records cannot be produced, which in TCPA class litigation has produced settlements in the tens and hundreds of millions.
    ASK
    Where are your consent and do-not-call suppression records maintained?
  3. 03

    Client customer databases are loaded into your dialler.

    WHY
    Campaigns require client customer and prospect lists loaded into dialling and CRM platforms, containing names, numbers, account details, and frequently purchase or account history. Those individuals have no relationship with the contact centre.
    LOSS
    Breach exposing client customer databases across multiple campaigns, with notification obligations flowing through each client and state privacy law exposure.
    ASK
    How many client customer records are loaded in your dialling platform right now?
