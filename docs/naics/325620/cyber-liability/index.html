Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 325620

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 325620, Toilet Preparation Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    MoCRA brought federal registration and safety substantiation to cosmetics.

    WHY
    The Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act requires facility registration, product listing, safety substantiation records, adverse event reporting, and good manufacturing practice requirements for cosmetics, with records retained and available to FDA.
    LOSS
    FDA enforcement where safety substantiation and adverse event records cannot be produced, with mandatory recall authority now available to FDA for cosmetics and products removed from market.
    ASK
    Where are your product safety substantiation records held?
  2. 02

    Contamination in personal care products injures consumers.

    WHY
    Cosmetics and personal care products contact skin, eyes, and mucous membranes, with microbiological contamination and chemical impurities causing infection and injury. Preservative systems and manufacturing hygiene are the controls.
    LOSS
    Contaminated product causing eye infections or skin injury, with recall, adverse event reporting, and litigation in a category where consumers apply products daily and to children.
    ASK
    How is microbiological testing recorded against each production batch?
  3. 03

    Contract manufacturing means holding brands' formulations.

    WHY
    Much of the industry produces for brand owners under contract, holding proprietary formulations, fragrance compositions, and unreleased product developments under confidentiality.
    LOSS
    Formulation disclosure to competitors, with contractual liability to brand owners whose products are defined by those formulations and immediate loss of the manufacturing relationships.
    ASK
    How many brand owners' formulations are held on your systems?
