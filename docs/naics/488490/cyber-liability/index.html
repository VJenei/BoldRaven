Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 488490

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 488490, Other Support Activities for Road Transportation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Truck stop and fuel operations combine payment, fuel, and driver data.

    WHY
    Facilities in this category handle high volume fuel card transactions, loyalty accounts, and driver identity data alongside tank gauges and dispenser controls. Fuel cards convert to cash quickly and unattended dispensers are a persistent skimming target.
    LOSS
    Card compromise across a high volume fuel operation, with brand assessments and fraud liability, plus environmental exposure if tank monitoring is manipulated to mask a release.
    ASK
    How many fuel card transactions do you process, and how are dispensers inspected?
  2. 02

    Toll and permitting services hold account and vehicle data.

    WHY
    Operators providing toll processing, permitting, and compliance services hold carrier account details, vehicle registrations, payment methods, and travel records across many client fleets. That data reveals fleet movements and account balances.
    LOSS
    Exposure of client fleet data and payment credentials, with third-party liability to every carrier whose account information was held.
    ASK
    How many carrier accounts and vehicle records do you administer?
  3. 03

    Roadside and inspection services support compliance obligations.

    WHY
    Providers of inspection, weighing, and compliance services produce records that carriers rely on to demonstrate regulatory compliance. Those records are examined during audits and after crashes.
    LOSS
    Client carriers unable to demonstrate compliance because the service provider's records are unavailable, with the resulting enforcement consequences claimed against the provider.
    ASK
    Do your clients rely on your records to demonstrate their own compliance?
