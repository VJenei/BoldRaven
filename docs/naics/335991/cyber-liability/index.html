Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 335991

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 335991, Carbon and Graphite Product Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Graphite electrodes are consumed continuously by steel mills.

    WHY
    Electric arc furnaces consume graphite electrodes continuously and cannot operate without them. Supply is concentrated among few producers and mills hold limited stock.
    LOSS
    Production or shipping stopped with mills unable to obtain electrodes, and steel production halted across customers at a daily value far exceeding the electrode price.
    ASK
    How many days of electrode stock do your steel customers hold?
  2. 02

    Graphitisation is a long, energy-intensive, controlled process.

    WHY
    Electrode production runs baking and graphitisation cycles taking weeks at very high temperature under process control. An interrupted or misrun cycle ruins product representing weeks of throughput and enormous energy input.
    LOSS
    Furnace control loss during graphitisation, with product ruined, weeks of capacity lost, and delivery commitments to mills missed with no way to compress the cycle.
    ASK
    How long is a graphitisation cycle, and what happens if control is lost partway?
  3. 03

    Specialty carbon products serve regulated and strategic applications.

    WHY
    Carbon and graphite products go into nuclear, aerospace, semiconductor, and defence applications with qualification, traceability, and in some cases export control requirements.
    LOSS
    Loss of qualification records or exposure of controlled technical data, removing eligibility for the highest value applications and creating export violation exposure.
    ASK
    Which of your products are supplied against nuclear, aerospace, or defence qualifications?
