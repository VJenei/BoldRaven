Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541410

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541410, Interior Design Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Drawings and specifications are the deliverable.

    • Design drawings, finish schedules, and specifications represent the project work.
    • Contractors and vendors order and build from those documents.
    • Recreating them means redesigning the project.
    ASK
    Where are your project drawings and specifications stored?
  2. 02

    Procurement moves client money to vendors.

    • Furniture and finish orders are placed and paid on client funds.
    • Vendor banking instructions arrive and change by email.
    • A diverted payment is client money you must replace.
    ASK
    How are vendor payment instructions verified before client funds move?
  3. 03

    Installation is scheduled against occupancy dates.

    • Furniture and finishes install against a move-in or opening date.
    • Long lead items are ordered months ahead.
    • A missed date leaves a space unusable.
    ASK
    What occupancy dates depend on your project delivery?
