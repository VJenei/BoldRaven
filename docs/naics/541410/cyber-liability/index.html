Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541410

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541410, Interior Design Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold floor plans and access information for clients' homes and offices.

    WHY
    Designers hold measured drawings, photographs, security and technology layouts, and access arrangements for residential and commercial clients. Residential work frequently involves affluent clients whose homes and possessions are documented in detail.
    LOSS
    Exposure of floor plans, valuable furnishings, and access details for identified addresses is a burglary targeting resource, and high net worth clients treat the confidentiality failure as unforgivable.
    ASK
    Do your project files include floor plans and photographs of clients' homes?
  2. 02

    Procurement means you are handling clients' money.

    WHY
    Designers specify and purchase furniture, fixtures, and finishes on behalf of clients, collecting deposits and paying vendors on long lead time orders. Payment instructions to vendors and deposit requests to clients move by email.
    LOSS
    Diverted client deposits or vendor payments on orders that were never placed, with lead times meaning the loss is discovered months later and the client still expects the goods.
    ASK
    How much client money passes through your accounts for procurement each year?
  3. 03

    Specifications and orders are the only record of what was selected.

    WHY
    Projects involve hundreds of specified items with finishes, dimensions, and lead times, ordered against a schedule tied to construction completion. Custom items are made to the specification and are not returnable.
    LOSS
    Lost specification and order records mean items ordered wrong or not at all, on custom goods with long lead times and a client move-in date that has already been set.
    ASK
    How many specified items are on order for your active projects right now?
