Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 485119

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 485119, Other Urban Transit Systems, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Riders depend on the service with no substitute.

    • Transit carries people to work, medical care, and school.
    • Many riders have no alternative.
    • A stoppage strands them entirely.
    ASK
    How many daily riders depend on your system with no alternative?
  2. 02

    Vehicle and system control run electronically.

    • Movement, signalling, and vehicle control run on connected systems.
    • Loss of control is a safety event, not a service issue.
    • Federal oversight follows any control related incident.
    ASK
    What systems control vehicle movement on your network?
  3. 03

    Fare and rider data run through your systems.

    • Fare collection holds payment cards and rider account data.
    • Trip history reveals where individuals travel and when.
    • Every affected rider must be notified.
    ASK
    What rider account and payment data does your fare system hold?
