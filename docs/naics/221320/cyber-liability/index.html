Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 221320

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 221320, Sewage Treatment Facilities, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    A treatment plant taken over remotely spills sewage, and it has happened.

    WHY
    Wastewater collection and treatment run on SCADA controlling lift station pumps, valves, aeration, and chemical dosing. The Maroochy Shire case in Australia remains the reference example, where a former contractor used radio access to release hundreds of thousands of litres of sewage into parks and waterways. Collection systems are geographically dispersed and controlled remotely by necessity.
    LOSS
    A sanitary sewer overflow is a reportable NPDES violation with cleanup, third-party property claims, and consent decree exposure. Overflows into surface water attract citizen suits as well as regulators.
    ASK
    How are your lift stations monitored and controlled, and over what communication path?
  2. 02

    Discharge permits are enforced against monitoring data you generate.

    WHY
    NPDES permits set effluent limits monitored continuously and reported in discharge monitoring reports. Missing data is not neutral, and exceedances are self-reported under penalty. Laboratory information and SCADA historian records are the evidence.
    LOSS
    Permit violations that cannot be characterised or defended without data, plus penalties assessed per day per parameter, which accumulate quickly across a monitoring gap.
    ASK
    How is your discharge monitoring data collected and stored?
  3. 03

    Biological treatment takes weeks to recover from an upset.

    WHY
    Activated sludge and other biological processes depend on maintaining a living population under controlled aeration, return rates, and loading. An upset from mis-set aeration or a slug load kills the biology. Reseeding and restabilising a plant takes weeks during which the effluent is out of compliance.
    LOSS
    Weeks of continuous permit violation while the plant recovers, with penalties accruing daily and no ability to simply stop accepting flow.
    ASK
    How long would it take your biological process to recover from a serious upset?
