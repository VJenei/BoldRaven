Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541211

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541211, Offices of Certified Public Accountants, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    The IRS requires tax professionals to have a written security plan.

    WHY
    Tax preparers are subject to the FTC Safeguards Rule as financial institutions, and the IRS requires a written information security plan as a condition of maintaining a preparer tax identification number. Guidance is explicit that this is mandatory, not advisory.
    LOSS
    IRS and FTC exposure for the absence of a documented plan, examined separately from the breach, plus the practical consequence that compromised preparer credentials are used to file fraudulent returns.
    ASK
    Do you have a written information security plan as required for your PTIN?
  2. 02

    Client files hold everything needed to impersonate a taxpayer.

    WHY
    Accountants hold Social Security numbers for clients, spouses, and dependants, income documentation, bank account details, and prior year returns. Preparer software credentials allow filing on behalf of every client in the practice.
    LOSS
    Fraudulent returns filed for clients using your credentials, which delays legitimate refunds and creates months of remediation per client, plus notification obligations across the whole practice.
    ASK
    How many client records with Social Security numbers for entire families do you hold?
  3. 03

    Filing deadlines are statutory and concentrated.

    WHY
    Tax practice is driven by filing deadlines with the majority of work compressed into short periods. Extensions exist but penalties and client consequences attach to missed deadlines, and audit and attest engagements carry their own reporting dates.
    LOSS
    Missed filing deadlines across a client base during filing season, with penalties and interest that clients will look to the accountant to cover, plus reputational damage in a referral-driven profession.
    ASK
    How many client returns are due in your busiest filing week?
