Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 713910

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 713910, Golf Courses and Country Clubs, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Member data covers affluent households in detail.

    WHY
    Clubs hold member and family details, home addresses, stored payment methods for dues and charging privileges, financial statements provided during membership application, and guest records. Membership is frequently a matter of local status and privacy.
    LOSS
    Exposure of an affluent membership roster with addresses and financial information, which supports targeted fraud and burglary and is a serious privacy failure in a community where members know each other.
    ASK
    Do your membership applications include financial statements or net worth information?
  2. 02

    Irrigation and course systems are networked and control the asset.

    WHY
    Course irrigation runs on central control systems managing hundreds of zones, with pump stations and weather stations connected. Turf on greens is a high value asset that dies quickly without correct irrigation, particularly in summer.
    LOSS
    Greens and fairways damaged by irrigation failure or over-watering, with putting surfaces taking months to recover and the course unplayable or degraded through the peak season.
    ASK
    How long would your greens survive an irrigation control failure in midsummer?
  3. 03

    Club operations run entirely on the membership and POS system.

    WHY
    Tee times, dining, retail, member charging, and dues billing all run through connected systems, with members charging to account rather than paying at the point of sale. Monthly billing depends on the accumulated charge record.
    LOSS
    Inability to record member charges or bill dues, with revenue unrecoverable where charges were not captured and member disputes over amounts that cannot be substantiated.
    ASK
    How are member charges captured across dining, retail, and the pro shop?
