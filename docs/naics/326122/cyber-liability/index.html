Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 326122

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 326122, Plastics Pipe and Pipe Fitting Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Pipe carries pressure ratings that failure makes catastrophic.

    WHY
    Plastic pipe for water, gas, and pressure applications is manufactured to standards with pressure ratings, material designation, and third-party certification marked on the pipe. Gas distribution pipe in particular is a life safety product.
    LOSS
    Pipe below rating installed in gas or water distribution, with failures causing explosions, flooding, or service interruption, and every length from the affected production requiring identification in systems already buried.
    ASK
    Is your pipe used in gas distribution or pressure water service?
  2. 02

    Drinking water contact requires certification.

    WHY
    Pipe and fittings for potable water must be certified for drinking water contact, with material and additive compliance verified by third-party certification bodies and required by codes and water utilities.
    LOSS
    Uncertified material installed in drinking water systems, requiring removal from systems that may already be in service, with public health concern and utility claims.
    ASK
    Which drinking water certifications do your products carry?
  3. 03

    Extrusion parameters determine wall thickness and material properties.

    WHY
    Pipe extrusion controls diameter, wall thickness, and cooling to produce the required pressure rating. Wall thickness below specification reduces pressure capacity in ways not visible externally.
    LOSS
    Under-thickness pipe carrying the correct printed rating installed in pressure service, which is a latent defect discovered only when the pipe fails under pressure.
    ASK
    How is wall thickness monitored and recorded during extrusion?
