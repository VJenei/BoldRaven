Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 491110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 491110, Postal Service, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Mail and package data covers essentially the whole population.

    WHY
    Postal operations hold addresses, change of address records, package tracking, delivery confirmations, and imaging of mailpieces, covering nearly every household and business. Change of address data in particular is a tool for identity theft and mail redirection fraud.
    LOSS
    A breach of address and forwarding data enables mail redirection fraud at scale, which is a precursor to account takeover and identity theft affecting people who never chose to transact with anyone.
    ASK
    What controls prevent fraudulent change of address submissions?
  2. 02

    Processing and distribution centres run on automated sortation.

    WHY
    Mail and parcels are sorted by automated equipment reading addresses and barcodes at very high speed, with routing decided by the sortation system. Manual sorting at national volume is not achievable.
    LOSS
    Mail and parcels accumulating unsorted across the network within hours, with recovery taking far longer than the outage as backlogs compound through every downstream facility.
    ASK
    What volume of mail passes through your sortation equipment each day?
  3. 03

    Retail and money services operate at every window.

    WHY
    Postal retail sells postage, processes money orders, handles passport applications, and accepts payment cards. Money order issuance carries anti-money laundering recordkeeping obligations, and passport processing involves identity documents.
    LOSS
    Exposure of identity document data from passport processing and money services records, which combines financial and identity information in a single incident.
    ASK
    Do your retail operations handle passport applications or money orders?
