Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 332311

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 332311, Prefabricated Metal Building and Component Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Buildings are engineered for a specific site, code, and loading.

    WHY
    Metal buildings are engineered for the wind, snow, and seismic loads of a specific location, with sealed drawings and fabrication data for each project. The design accounts for local code and site conditions.
    LOSS
    Buildings fabricated to wrong designs or loads erected at sites where they are under-designed, which is a structural deficiency discovered in a wind or snow event with collapse potential.
    ASK
    How are project-specific engineered designs released into fabrication?
  2. 02

    Erection sequences depend on components arriving complete and in order.

    WHY
    Metal building components are fabricated as a package for a specific building, with erection crews and cranes scheduled. Missing or incorrect components stop erection entirely.
    LOSS
    Erection crews and cranes standing idle for missing or wrong components, with costs of thousands per day and a construction schedule that has other trades sequenced behind enclosure.
    ASK
    What does an erection crew and crane cost per day on your projects?
  3. 03

    Fabrication runs from digital data to automated equipment.

    WHY
    Frame fabrication runs from engineering data through automated cutting, drilling, and welding, with hole patterns and dimensions determined by the design file. Errors are built into steel that cannot be uncut.
    LOSS
    Members fabricated to wrong dimensions or hole patterns, scrapped at material and fabrication cost, with the project delayed while replacements are made.
    ASK
    How does engineering data reach your fabrication equipment?
