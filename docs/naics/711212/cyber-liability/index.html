Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 711212

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 711212, Racetracks, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Pari-mutuel wagering systems have been manipulated from the inside.

    WHY
    Wagering runs through totalisator systems that pool bets, calculate odds, and determine payouts. A 2002 fraud in which a totalisator company employee altered wagers after races were run led to criminal convictions and reshaped industry security practice. Wagering integrity is the entire basis of the business.
    LOSS
    Manipulated wagers or payouts, with criminal investigation, racing commission action, and the loss of public confidence that pari-mutuel wagering depends on entirely.
    ASK
    How is your wagering system access controlled and independently audited?
  2. 02

    Racing regulation requires extensive records and testing.

    WHY
    Racetracks operate under state racing commission licences with requirements covering licensing of participants, medication and drug testing records, race results, and wagering reporting. Integrity records are examined continuously.
    LOSS
    Racing commission action where required records cannot be produced, with licence suspension stopping racing entirely and every associated business at the track with it.
    ASK
    What records does your racing commission require you to produce on demand?
  3. 03

    Account wagering holds customer funds and identity.

    WHY
    Advance deposit wagering accounts hold customer funds, identity verification data, and wagering histories, subject to anti-money laundering obligations and state regulation. Account takeover results in direct theft of customer balances.
    LOSS
    Customer account balances drained through takeover, with the track liable to restore funds and regulators examining identity verification and account security controls.
    ASK
    How are customer wagering accounts authenticated before funds are withdrawn?
