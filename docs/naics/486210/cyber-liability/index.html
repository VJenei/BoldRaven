Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 486210

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 486210, Pipeline Transportation of Natural Gas, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Federal pipeline security directives apply directly to you.

    • Covered operators must report cyber incidents and maintain approved security plans.
    • Regulators require tested response and documented controls.
    • Findings can restrict pipeline operations.
    ASK
    What federal pipeline security requirements apply to your system?
  2. 02

    Pressure control failure causes explosions.

    • Compressors, valves, and pressure are controlled remotely.
    • Over pressure events have destroyed neighbourhoods.
    • The consequence is measured in homes, not service minutes.
    ASK
    What can be operated on your pipeline from the control centre?
  3. 03

    Gas supply failures happen in winter.

    • Heating and power generation depend on continuous gas delivery.
    • Supply failures occur during extreme cold.
    • People lose heat when they cannot survive without it.
    ASK
    What utilities and generators depend on your pipeline?
