Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 326140

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 326140, Polystyrene Foam Product Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Foam manufacture involves flammable blowing agents.

    WHY
    Expandable polystyrene contains pentane, a flammable hydrocarbon released during expansion and storage. Pentane accumulation in silos, moulding areas, and storage has caused explosions in this industry.
    LOSS
    A pentane explosion destroying production areas with worker fatalities, and the finished foam itself burning rapidly once ignited, producing a fire that consumes the facility.
    ASK
    How is pentane concentration monitored in your expansion and storage areas?
  2. 02

    Insulation products carry thermal and fire performance ratings.

    WHY
    Foam insulation is certified for thermal resistance and flame spread, with those values relied on by building codes and energy compliance. Foam plastic insulation requires thermal barriers and has specific code requirements.
    LOSS
    Product below rated thermal performance installed in buildings failing energy code compliance, and flame spread failures creating fire exposure in occupancies where the product was approved on its rating.
    ASK
    What thermal and flame spread ratings do your products carry?
  3. 03

    Food service and packaging products face regulatory restrictions.

    WHY
    Polystyrene food service products are banned or restricted in a growing number of states and municipalities, with compliance depending on knowing where products ship and what local rules apply.
    LOSS
    Product shipped into jurisdictions where it is prohibited, with enforcement against customers who used it and the manufacturer's market access reduced without warning.
    ASK
    How do you control which products ship into jurisdictions with polystyrene restrictions?
