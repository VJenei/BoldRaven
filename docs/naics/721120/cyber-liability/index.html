Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 721120

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 721120, Casino Hotels, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Two major casino resort operators were compromised in the same month by phone calls.

    WHY
    In September 2023 attackers social engineered IT help desks at two of the largest casino resort operators. One disclosed approximately $100 million of impact with slot machines, hotel reservations, and room key systems unavailable for around ten days; the other was reported to have paid roughly $15 million. The entry point was identity verification at the help desk.
    LOSS
    Gaming floor, hotel operations, restaurants, and entertainment all stop simultaneously, with guests unable to check in, use keys, or gamble, and remediation costs in the tens of millions on top of lost revenue.
    ASK
    How does your help desk verify identity before resetting an employee credential?
  2. 02

    You are both a casino and a hotel, with both regulatory regimes.

    WHY
    Casino hotels are financial institutions under the Bank Secrecy Act with AML programme, currency transaction reporting, and record retention obligations, while simultaneously holding hotel guest identity, passport, and payment data at scale. Gaming regulators require notification and can restrict operations.
    LOSS
    FinCEN enforcement, gaming regulator action, and guest data breach obligations arising from a single incident, each with separate authorities, deadlines, and penalties.
    ASK
    Which regulators would you need to notify after a cyber incident, and how quickly?
  3. 03

    Player tracking links identity, spending, and behaviour.

    WHY
    Loyalty and player tracking systems record identity, play history, theoretical loss, comp entitlement, and credit lines, building a detailed behavioural profile of each patron. Gaming patrons frequently regard their play as private.
    LOSS
    Exposure of player tracking data reveals gambling behaviour and losses for identified individuals, which causes personal and relationship harm beyond financial exposure and is uniquely damaging in this industry.
    ASK
    What does your player tracking data reveal about an individual patron?
