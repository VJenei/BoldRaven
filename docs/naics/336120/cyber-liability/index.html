Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 336120

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 336120, Heavy Duty Truck Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Trucks are connected and receive software over the air.

    WHY
    Commercial vehicles run electronic control units, telematics, and remote diagnostics with over the air software delivery. Braking, steering, and driver assistance functions are software controlled on vehicles weighing 80,000 pounds.
    LOSS
    A vulnerability affecting control functions on heavy trucks in service, with accident potential proportional to vehicle mass, an NHTSA recall, and a software campaign across a fleet operating on public roads.
    ASK
    What vehicle functions can be affected by software delivered over the air?
  2. 02

    Emissions certification is enforced through engine software.

    WHY
    Heavy duty engines are certified to EPA and CARB standards with aftertreatment governed by engine calibration. Enforcement against defeat devices and uncertified calibrations has produced settlements in the billions.
    LOSS
    Vehicles built or serviced with wrong or altered calibrations, which is an emissions violation with per-vehicle penalties, recall, and potentially a consent decree with ongoing federal oversight.
    ASK
    How are certified engine calibrations controlled at build and in service?
  3. 03

    Fleet customers and dealers depend on your systems continuously.

    WHY
    Trucks are sold and serviced through dealers using manufacturer systems for parts, warranty, and diagnostics. A truck out of service costs a fleet operator revenue every day it does not run.
    LOSS
    Parts and diagnostic systems unavailable with trucks unable to be repaired across the dealer network, and fleet customers pursuing the manufacturer for the revenue lost on idle vehicles.
    ASK
    How do dealers access diagnostics and order parts from you?
