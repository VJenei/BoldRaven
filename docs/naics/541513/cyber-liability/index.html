Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541513

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541513, Computer Facilities Management Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You operate the client's information systems entirely.

    WHY
    Facilities management arrangements place the provider in operational control of a client's IT function, including infrastructure, applications, and data. The client has no independent capability to operate or recover those systems.
    LOSS
    The client's entire operation stops and they have no internal function able to respond. Contractual service levels are the smallest part of the exposure compared with the client's business interruption claim.
    ASK
    Does your client retain any internal capability to operate their systems without you?
  2. 02

    You hold and process client data under their regulatory obligations.

    WHY
    Operating a client's systems means holding whatever data those systems contain, including protected health information, cardholder data, and personal data subject to state privacy laws. Contracts pass those obligations to the provider.
    LOSS
    A single breach triggering the client's obligations under multiple regimes at once, with the provider contractually responsible for notification and remediation costs across all of them.
    ASK
    What regulated data categories sit inside the systems you operate?
  3. 03

    Concentration means one incident affects every client you serve.

    WHY
    Providers operate shared infrastructure, tooling, and staff across multiple client engagements. An incident in the shared layer reaches every client at once rather than being contained to one relationship.
    LOSS
    Simultaneous outage and breach across the entire client base, with aggregated liability that can exceed the provider's own revenue by a wide margin.
    ASK
    What infrastructure or tooling is shared across all of your client engagements?
