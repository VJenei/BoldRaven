Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 711310

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 711310, Promoters of Performing Arts, Sports, and Similar Events with Facilities, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Ticketing platforms concentrate enormous volumes of customer data.

    WHY
    Venues sell through ticketing systems holding customer identity, payment methods, and event attendance histories. A 2024 breach affecting a major ticketing provider was reported to involve data associated with hundreds of millions of customers, accessed through a cloud account without multi-factor authentication.
    LOSS
    Notification across a customer base measured in millions, with the venue carrying the audience relationship and regulatory attention regardless of whether the platform or the venue was breached.
    ASK
    Which ticketing platform holds your customer data, and is multi-factor authentication enforced on it?
  2. 02

    Events happen on a fixed date with thousands of people arriving.

    WHY
    Venue operations depend on ticketing, access control, point of sale, and building systems all functioning on event night. Doors open at a fixed time and the crowd arrives whether the systems work or not.
    LOSS
    A sold-out event where patrons cannot be admitted, which becomes a crowd safety situation, plus refunds, artist guarantees payable regardless, and lost concession and parking revenue.
    ASK
    What is your plan if access control fails 30 minutes before doors on a sold-out night?
  3. 03

    Settlement with artists and promoters happens after every show.

    WHY
    Venues settle with artists and promoters against box office receipts, expenses, and contractual guarantees immediately after each event, with substantial sums moving by wire on the night or shortly after.
    LOSS
    Diverted settlement payments and unresolvable box office reconciliation, with artists and promoters unpaid and future bookings at risk in a relationship-driven industry.
    ASK
    How are post-show settlement payments calculated and transmitted?
