Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561990

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561990, All Other Support Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Support providers hold client data and client obligations.

    WHY
    Firms in this category perform functions clients have chosen not to keep in house, holding whatever data the function requires and frequently carrying contractual obligations that flow down the client's own regulatory duties.
    LOSS
    Breach exposing client and end-customer data, with notification obligations flowing through each client contract and separate claims from every affected client.
    ASK
    Whose data do you hold on behalf of clients, and under what contractual terms?
  2. 02

    Clients cannot perform the function themselves.

    WHY
    Outsourced functions are outsourced because the capability was removed internally. When the provider stops, the client has no fallback and the function stops with it.
    LOSS
    Client operations interrupted with consequential claims reflecting the client's business rather than the service fee, and clients switching to competitors who were available.
    ASK
    Could your clients perform your function internally if you were unavailable?
  3. 03

    Small providers carry aggregated risk with limited defences.

    WHY
    Support businesses are frequently small operations serving many clients from shared systems, with no dedicated IT function, no incident response plan, and often no tested backup.
    LOSS
    A single incident affecting every client simultaneously, with aggregated liability exceeding the provider's revenue and no capacity to fund forensic investigation or notification.
    ASK
    How many clients would be affected simultaneously by a single incident at your business?
