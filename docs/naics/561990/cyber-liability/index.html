Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561990

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561990, All Other Support Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Client data passes through your systems.

    • Business, customer, and employee data is handled on the client's behalf.
    • Their regulatory obligations follow the data to you.
    • Every affected individual must be notified.
    ASK
    What client data do you handle?
  2. 02

    Clients depend on functions you perform.

    • Support functions are outsourced because clients lack the capability.
    • Their operations depend on your continuity.
    • Their work stops when yours does.
    ASK
    What client functions depend entirely on your operation?
  3. 03

    Scheduling and dispatch run your delivery.

    • Assignments, routing, and coverage run on connected systems.
    • Clients expect service on defined schedules.
    • A scheduling failure misses commitments across the client base.
    ASK
    How are your assignments and schedules managed?
