Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 331410

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 331410, Nonferrous Metal (except Aluminum) Smelting and Refining, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Smelting runs continuously and handles molten metal.

    • Furnaces and refining run as continuous high temperature processes.
    • Molten metal contacting moisture causes violent explosions.
    • An uncontrolled stop freezes vessels and takes weeks to recover.
    ASK
    What does a day of unplanned downtime cost at your plant?
  2. 02

    Refined metal is valuable and tracked by record.

    • Cathode, ingot, and refined metal are weighed, assayed, and recorded.
    • Inventory records are the only check on what leaves.
    • Altered records hide theft until a physical count.
    ASK
    How is refined metal weighed, recorded, and reconciled against shipments?
  3. 03

    Emissions and discharge monitoring is continuous.

    • Smelting emissions and discharges are monitored and reported to regulators.
    • Reported data determines permit compliance.
    • Gaps or corrupted data are treated as exceedances.
    ASK
    How is your emissions and discharge monitoring recorded and reported?
