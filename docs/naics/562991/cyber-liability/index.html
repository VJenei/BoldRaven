Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 562991

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 562991, Septic Tank and Related Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Pumping and disposal are regulated with manifest requirements.

    WHY
    Septage haulers are permitted and must document quantities pumped, sources, and disposal locations, with receiving treatment plants requiring manifests. Illegal disposal is a serious environmental violation with criminal exposure.
    LOSS
    Regulatory enforcement where disposal records cannot be produced, and inability to demonstrate lawful disposal is treated as evidence of illegal dumping regardless of what actually happened.
    ASK
    How do you document where each load was disposed and in what quantity?
  2. 02

    Customer service records support system history and inspections.

    WHY
    Service records document tank pumping frequency, system condition, and repairs, relied on during property transactions, county inspections, and permit renewals. Homeowners frequently have no other record of their system's history.
    LOSS
    Property transactions delayed and permits refused where service history cannot be produced, with the customer attributing the consequence to the service provider who held the records.
    ASK
    Do your customers rely on your records to prove service history at a property sale?
  3. 03

    Emergency calls involve failing systems and health hazards.

    WHY
    System failures cause sewage backup into homes and surfacing effluent, which is a health hazard requiring prompt response. Dispatch and routing determine how quickly a crew reaches a failing system.
    LOSS
    Delayed response to a sewage backup causing property damage and health exposure inside a customer's home, with claims that vastly exceed the service charge.
    ASK
    How are emergency service calls received and dispatched outside business hours?
