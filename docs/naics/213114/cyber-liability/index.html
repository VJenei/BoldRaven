Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 213114

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 213114, Support Activities for Metal Mining, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Drilling and assay results are the client's most valuable data.

    • Exploration drilling produces core, logs, and assay results for the client.
    • Those results move valuations and are confidential until disclosed.
    • A leak affects the client's share price and your contract.
    ASK
    What client drilling and assay data is stored on your systems?
  2. 02

    Your crews work under the mine's safety and training system.

    • Contractor training, certification, and inspection records are inspected.
    • Records support every person you put on site.
    • Missing records are citable against you and the operator.
    ASK
    What training and certification records do you keep for your mine crews?
  3. 03

    Work is scheduled into mine production windows.

    • Contract work is booked against production schedules and planned outages.
    • Mines lose production for every hour work overruns.
    • A scheduling failure is charged back to you.
    ASK
    How is your contract work scheduled against mine production?
