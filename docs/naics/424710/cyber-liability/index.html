Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 424710

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 424710, Petroleum Bulk Stations and Terminals, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Terminal automation controls what leaves the rack.

    • Loading racks, tank gauging, and inventory run on automated control systems.
    • Product moves in thousands of gallons per load.
    • Loss of control means overfills, releases, and fires.
    ASK
    Are your loading racks and tank gauging run by automated control systems?
  2. 02

    Fuel supply stops when the rack stops.

    • Retailers and fleets load continuously from your terminal.
    • They hold days of fuel at most.
    • A terminal outage empties stations across the region.
    ASK
    How many stations and fleets load from your terminal?
  3. 03

    Measurement and inventory records carry tax and financial obligations.

    • Loaded volumes determine excise tax, sales, and inventory reconciliation.
    • Those records are filed with regulators.
    • Discrepancies are treated as tax and environmental compliance failures.
    ASK
    How is your loading and inventory measurement data recorded and reported?
