Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 115113

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 115113, Crop Harvesting, Primarily by Machine, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Custom harvest runs a fixed route across a short season.

    • Crews move north through the season on committed dates.
    • Machines, trucks, labour, and lodging are all scheduled to that route.
    • A break in the route loses every field behind it.
    ASK
    How are your harvest route and customer commitments scheduled?
  2. 02

    Machines are connected to dealers and to customer data.

    • Combines carry telematics, guidance, and yield monitoring linked to outside systems.
    • Customer field boundaries and yield data pass through your machines.
    • A machine that cannot get its data runs blind or not at all.
    ASK
    Does your harvest equipment connect to dealer or customer data systems?
  3. 03

    You are paid on acres and bushels you record.

    • Job records, acres, and yields are captured electronically and invoiced from them.
    • Customers reconcile against their own scale tickets.
    • Lost job records mean work done that cannot be billed.
    ASK
    How are your acres harvested and job records recorded for billing?
