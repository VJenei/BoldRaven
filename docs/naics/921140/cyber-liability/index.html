Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 921140

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 921140, Executive and Legislative Offices, Combined, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Combined offices concentrate every function on one network.

    WHY
    Smaller governmental units run executive, legislative, finance, permitting, and often public safety support functions from shared infrastructure administered by a small IT team or an outsourced provider.
    LOSS
    Every governmental function stopping simultaneously, including revenue collection, permitting, and public safety support, with no unaffected department able to assist and no funding mechanism for recovery.
    ASK
    How many separate government functions depend on your single network?
  2. 02

    Small governments have minimal security capability.

    WHY
    Municipal and county governments of modest size frequently have one or two IT staff, limited budget, and legacy systems, while holding the same categories of sensitive data as much larger entities and being subject to the same laws.
    LOSS
    A compromise the entity has no capability to detect, scope, or remediate, requiring external assistance funded from a budget that has no provision for it.
    ASK
    How many people are responsible for cybersecurity in your organisation?
  3. 03

    Records span public disclosure and strict confidentiality.

    WHY
    Combined offices hold public records alongside personnel files, litigation materials, law enforcement information, and resident data, each with different legal treatment and all in the same environment.
    LOSS
    Simultaneous failure to meet public records obligations and inadvertent exposure of confidential material, with legal consequences arising from both directions at once.
    ASK
    How are confidential and publicly disclosable records separated in your systems?
