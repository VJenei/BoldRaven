Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 611513

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 611513, Apprenticeship Training, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Apprentice records support certification and registration.

    • Hours, competencies, and progression records support registered apprenticeship status.
    • Regulators and sponsors verify those records.
    • Apprentices cannot complete without them.
    ASK
    How are your apprentice hour and competency records maintained?
  2. 02

    Apprentice records hold personal and employment data.

    • Records carry identity, employer, and wage progression information.
    • Sponsors and employers rely on that data.
    • Every affected apprentice must be notified.
    ASK
    What apprentice personal and employment data do you hold?
  3. 03

    Training is scheduled around employer work.

    • Classroom and on-the-job training are scheduled with employers.
    • Employers plan crews around apprentice availability.
    • A scheduling failure disrupts employers as well as apprentices.
    ASK
    How are your training schedules coordinated with employers?
