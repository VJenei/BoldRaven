Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 332994

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 332994, Small Arms, Ordnance, and Ordnance Accessories Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Firearm manufacturing is federally licensed with mandatory records.

    WHY
    Manufacturers hold a federal firearms licence with acquisition and disposition records for every firearm by serial number, retained and produced on ATF inspection. Records must be maintained permanently and surrendered if the licence ends.
    LOSS
    Loss of acquisition and disposition records, which is a licence violation and defeats firearm tracing, with revocation exposure that ends the ability to manufacture.
    ASK
    How are your acquisition and disposition records maintained and backed up?
  2. 02

    Technical data on weapons is export controlled.

    WHY
    Firearm and ordnance designs are defence articles with technical data controlled under ITAR, requiring registration and controls on access. Disclosure to a foreign person, including remotely, is an export.
    LOSS
    Exposure of controlled technical data, which is a reportable ITAR violation with civil and criminal penalties and potential debarment from defence work.
    ASK
    How is access to weapon technical data restricted and logged?
  3. 03

    Firearm defects cause unintentional discharge and injury.

    WHY
    Firearms are not subject to CPSC jurisdiction, but manufacturers conduct safety recalls for defects causing unintentional discharge or failure. Locating affected firearms requires serial number records through distribution.
    LOSS
    A defect causing unintentional discharges with injuries, and a recall that cannot reach affected firearms because serial number distribution records are unavailable, leaving defective weapons in circulation.
    ASK
    Could you identify which distributors received specific serial number ranges?
