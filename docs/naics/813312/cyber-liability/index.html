Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 813312

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 813312, Environment, Conservation and Wildlife Organizations, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Location data can direct poachers and developers.

    • Species locations, nest sites, and habitat data are held in your systems.
    • That data tells someone exactly where to find protected species.
    • Disclosure has led to poaching and habitat destruction.
    ASK
    What species and habitat location data do you hold?
  2. 02

    Advocacy work makes you a target.

    • Campaigns against commercial and state interests attract sophisticated opposition.
    • Internal strategy and communications are the target.
    • Disclosure damages campaigns and exposes staff.
    ASK
    What campaign strategy and communications data do you hold?
  3. 03

    Donor and member records fund the work.

    • Supporter records carry contact details, giving history, and payment data.
    • Recurring donations run against those records.
    • Every affected supporter must be notified.
    ASK
    What donor and member records do you hold?
