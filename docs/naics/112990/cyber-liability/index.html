Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 112990

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 112990, All Other Animal Production, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Housing and watering systems run without a person present.

    • Ventilation, feeding, and watering are automated and alarmed.
    • Animals depend on those systems continuously.
    • A failure overnight is discovered when the losses are already taken.
    ASK
    Are your housing and watering systems automated with alarms?
  2. 02

    Movement and health records control what you can sell.

    • Official identification, health certificates, and movement records are required.
    • Disease tracing runs through those records.
    • Animals without documentation cannot be sold or moved.
    ASK
    What identification, health, and movement records do you maintain?
  3. 03

    Sales are made to a small number of buyers by transfer.

    • Animals are sold in lots and paid by electronic transfer.
    • A faked banking change diverts an entire settlement.
    • Animals leave the operation before payment arrives.
    ASK
    Do you receive payment or banking instructions from buyers by email?
