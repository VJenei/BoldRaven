Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 611699

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 611699, All Other Miscellaneous Schools and Instruction, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Instruction businesses hold participant and payment data.

    WHY
    Schools in this category enrol participants including minors, holding contact details, emergency information, medical disclosures where relevant, signed waivers, and recurring payment methods.
    LOSS
    Breach notification across participants and, where minors are involved, a reaction from parents and any partner organisations that is disproportionate to the size of the business.
    ASK
    Do you enrol minors, and what information do you hold about them?
  2. 02

    Scheduling and enrolment systems run the operation.

    WHY
    Class scheduling, instructor assignment, enrolment, and billing run through a single platform, with participants booked into dated sessions and courses. Instructors and facilities are committed against that schedule.
    LOSS
    Sessions that cannot be run or attended with instructors and facilities already committed, and enrolment records lost so that participants cannot be contacted.
    ASK
    How would you contact enrolled participants if your system were unavailable?
  3. 03

    Certification and completion records support participants' credentials.

    WHY
    Many programmes issue certificates of completion relied on by employers, licensing bodies, or insurers. Verification requests arrive years after attendance.
    LOSS
    Inability to verify a former participant's completion, with the participant unable to demonstrate a credential they earned and paid for.
    ASK
    How long do you retain completion records for verification requests?
