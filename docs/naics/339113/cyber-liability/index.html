Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 339113

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 339113, Surgical Appliance and Supplies Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Sterilisation and lot records support every sterile product claim.

    WHY
    Sterile single use devices are released on the basis of validated sterilisation cycles with load records and biological indicator results. A product labelled sterile that was not properly processed causes surgical site infections.
    LOSS
    Product released without valid sterilisation records reaching operating rooms, with infections in patients, an FDA recall, and every lot from the affected period withdrawn from hospitals.
    ASK
    How are sterilisation load records tied to released product lots?
  2. 02

    Quality system records are the FDA inspection record.

    WHY
    Manufacturers must maintain device master records, complaint files, and CAPA records under the Quality System Regulation, produced on inspection. Medical Device Reports are required within specified timeframes.
    LOSS
    Inability to produce quality records during inspection, with findings, a warning letter, and potential consent decree restricting the ability to manufacture and distribute.
    ASK
    How are complaint and CAPA records maintained and backed up?
  3. 03

    Hospitals hold limited stock of supplies they use daily.

    WHY
    Surgical supplies are consumed continuously with hospitals holding days of stock, ordered through distributors and group purchasing arrangements. Supply interruptions become procedure cancellations.
    LOSS
    Order and shipping systems unavailable, with hospitals unable to obtain supplies and elective procedures cancelled across the customer base within days.
    ASK
    How many days of stock do your hospital customers typically hold?
