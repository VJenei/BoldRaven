Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 444110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 444110, Home Centers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Pro contractor accounts carry credit and lien exposure.

    WHY
    Home centres extend credit to contractors buying material for specific projects, with delivery to job sites. Mechanics lien rights against the project secure those balances and depend on job-tracked delivery records and statutory notice deadlines.
    LOSS
    Lost lien rights on delivered material converts secured project exposure into unsecured trade debt, on account balances that for active contractors run well into six figures.
    ASK
    How do you track job-site deliveries for lien notice purposes?
  2. 02

    High transaction volume across registers and online makes PCI compliance mandatory.

    WHY
    Home centres process very high card volume across many lanes plus buy-online-pickup-in-store fulfilment. PCI DSS 4.0 became fully mandatory on March 31, 2025, adding requirements for monitoring scripts on e-commerce payment pages because that is where skimming now occurs.
    LOSS
    Card brand assessments and forensic costs scale with transaction count, and a home centre's volume puts it in the highest merchant tiers with the strictest obligations.
    ASK
    Do you offer buy online, pickup in store, and who manages your payment page code?
  3. 03

    Inventory and pricing systems drive every register and every shelf.

    WHY
    Pricing, promotions, inventory, and replenishment across tens of thousands of SKUs are system-controlled. Registers cannot price items without the system, and replenishment stops when consumption data stops flowing.
    LOSS
    Stores that cannot transact at all, with perishable seasonal inventory and promotional periods lost, and replenishment gaps persisting for weeks after recovery.
    ASK
    Could your registers price and sell anything if the central system were unavailable?
