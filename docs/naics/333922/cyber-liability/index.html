Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333922

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333922, Conveyor and Conveying Equipment Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Conveyors cause entanglement and crush injuries.

    WHY
    Conveyors have nip points, moving belts, and automated starts, with guarding, pull cords, and control interlocks preventing injury. Unexpected starting during clearing or maintenance is a recognised fatality mechanism.
    LOSS
    A control defect permitting unexpected conveyor start during a clearing task, causing an entanglement fatality, with an OSHA investigation examining the control design and every system of the type affected.
    ASK
    How do your control systems prevent unexpected starting during maintenance?
  2. 02

    Systems are engineered to order with control code you write.

    WHY
    Conveying systems are laid out for a specific facility with PLC code, routing logic, and integration to warehouse and production systems written for that installation.
    LOSS
    Loss of layout and control code for systems in build and in service, halting projects and leaving installed systems unsupportable when they need changes or fault resolution.
    ASK
    How many installed systems run on control code held only by you?
  3. 03

    Conveying systems carry the customer's entire throughput.

    WHY
    Distribution centres and production plants move all their material through conveying systems. When the system stops, nothing moves and there is no manual alternative at volume.
    LOSS
    Systems stopped by a control problem traced to supplied software, with customer distribution centres unable to ship and claims driven by their throughput value.
    ASK
    What throughput depends on a single conveying system at your customer sites?
