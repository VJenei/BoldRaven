Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541430

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541430, Graphic Design Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Client brand assets and unreleased campaigns sit on your systems.

    WHY
    Designers hold logos, brand guidelines, packaging artwork, and campaign creative before launch, along with the working files that clients frequently do not hold themselves. Campaigns are timed to announcements and product releases.
    LOSS
    A leaked campaign or unreleased packaging design forfeits the launch and hands competitors advance notice, with client claims measured against the campaign spend rather than the design fee.
    ASK
    Do you hold unreleased campaign or packaging artwork for clients right now?
  2. 02

    Production deadlines are set by print and media bookings.

    WHY
    Artwork must reach printers, publications, and media platforms by booking deadlines that were paid for in advance. Print slots and media placements do not move for a missed file.
    LOSS
    Missed print and media deadlines with the placement already bought, so the client loses the media value and claims it against the designer.
    ASK
    What media or print bookings depend on your file delivery dates?
  3. 03

    Working files are frequently the only copy in existence.

    WHY
    Clients typically receive finished output rather than layered working files, which remain with the designer. Future revisions, format adaptations, and reprints all depend on those files.
    LOSS
    Loss of working files means brand assets must be recreated from finished output, degrading quality and costing the client the value of design work already paid for.
    ASK
    Do your clients hold copies of the layered working files, or only the final output?
