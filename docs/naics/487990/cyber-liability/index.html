Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 487990

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 487990, Scenic and Sightseeing Transportation, Other, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Air tour and adventure operations carry concentrated life safety risk.

    WHY
    Helicopter tours, balloon rides, and similar operations carry passengers in activities where an equipment or judgement failure is usually fatal. Maintenance status, pilot currency, weather decision records, and passenger weight data are all required before dispatch.
    LOSS
    An accident where maintenance and currency records cannot be produced, in NTSB investigation and litigation involving multiple fatalities.
    ASK
    How is maintenance status and pilot currency verified before each flight?
  2. 02

    Weight and balance depends on accurate passenger data.

    WHY
    Air and balloon operations require actual passenger weights and distribution for weight and balance calculation. Booking systems capture the data and dispatch relies on it. Errors put the aircraft outside its certificated envelope.
    LOSS
    An aircraft dispatched outside weight and balance limits, which is a direct loss of control exposure and a certificate action against the operator.
    ASK
    How are passenger weights captured and used in your weight and balance calculation?
  3. 03

    Bookings, waivers, and payment run through one platform.

    WHY
    Adventure operators sell online with stored payment methods, signed electronic liability waivers, and medical declarations from participants. The waiver is the operator's primary legal protection and exists only electronically.
    LOSS
    A liability claim where the signed waiver and medical declaration cannot be produced, removing the defence the entire risk model depends on.
    ASK
    Where are your signed electronic waivers stored, and could you produce one from three years ago?
