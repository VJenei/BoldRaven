Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 488410

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 488410, Motor Vehicle Towing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold vehicles and everything inside them.

    WHY
    Towing and impound operations take custody of vehicles and their contents, including personal property, documents, and sometimes firearms or cash. Intake records, photographs, and property inventories are the only evidence of what was in the vehicle when it arrived.
    LOSS
    Property claims from vehicle owners that cannot be rebutted without intake inventories and photographs, plus regulatory complaints in a sector where consumer protection agencies are already attentive.
    ASK
    How do you record vehicle contents and condition at intake?
  2. 02

    Police rotation and motor club dispatch are the source of most work.

    WHY
    Towing companies receive dispatch from police rotations, motor clubs, and roadside assistance platforms, with response time performance measured and rotation position at stake. Digital dispatch platforms assign calls in seconds to whoever accepts first.
    LOSS
    Removal from a police rotation or motor club network for missed response times, which removes the majority of call volume and is difficult to regain.
    ASK
    What share of your calls come from police rotation or motor club platforms?
  3. 03

    Lien and notification processes are legally prescribed and time-bound.

    WHY
    Disposing of unclaimed vehicles requires statutory notice to registered owners and lienholders, obtained through state records, with prescribed waiting periods and documentation before sale. Getting the process wrong exposes the operator to conversion claims.
    LOSS
    Conversion liability for vehicles sold without proper notice, plus storage fees that cannot be collected because the lien process was not properly documented.
    ASK
    How do you track statutory notice deadlines for unclaimed vehicles?
