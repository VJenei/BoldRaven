Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541921

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541921, Photography Studios, Portrait, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold photographs of clients, including children.

    WHY
    Portrait studios hold images of families, school pupils, and individuals, frequently linked to names, schools, and contact details. School photography in particular holds images and identifying information for large numbers of children.
    LOSS
    Exposure of children's photographs with names and school affiliation is a child safety exposure that parents, schools, and authorities treat entirely differently from ordinary data loss, and school contracts do not survive it.
    ASK
    Do you hold photographs of children linked to their names and schools?
  2. 02

    Images cannot be recreated once the moment has passed.

    WHY
    Weddings, newborn sessions, and events are unrepeatable. The images exist only as files held by the photographer between the session and delivery, and clients frequently order reprints years afterward from the studio's archive.
    LOSS
    Loss of images from an unrepeatable event, where no amount of refund addresses the harm and the resulting claims and public reaction are severe in a referral-driven business.
    ASK
    How are images backed up between the session and delivery to the client?
  3. 03

    Sessions are dated commitments with deposits taken.

    WHY
    Portrait and event photography is booked months ahead for weddings, school dates, and family occasions, with deposits collected and dates committed. Studios coordinate multiple photographers and locations against a calendar.
    LOSS
    Missed bookings on dates that cannot be rescheduled, with clients arriving for sessions that were never recorded and events proceeding without a photographer.
    ASK
    How many dated bookings with deposits are you currently holding?
