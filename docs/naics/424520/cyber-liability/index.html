Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 424520

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 424520, Livestock Merchant Wholesalers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Federal law requires livestock sellers to be paid within a day.

    WHY
    The Packers and Stockyards Act requires prompt payment for livestock, generally before the close of the next business day, and dealers must be bonded and registered. Payment failures trigger trust claims and USDA enforcement. There is no grace period for a systems outage.
    LOSS
    Missed prompt payment obligations across a sale week bring USDA enforcement, bond claims, and potential suspension of registration, which ends the ability to deal in livestock.
    ASK
    How quickly are you legally required to pay for livestock you purchase?
  2. 02

    Cattle purchases and sales settle by wire in large amounts.

    WHY
    Livestock dealers and order buyers move large sums daily between producers, feedyards, and packers, arranged by phone and email and settled by wire. Counterparties often transact a few times a year, and payment instructions are exchanged in the moment.
    LOSS
    A diverted settlement of $100,000 to $1M, where the seller remains legally entitled to prompt payment and the dealer pays twice or defaults on a statutory obligation.
    ASK
    What is the value of livestock payments leaving your office in a typical week?
  3. 03

    Animal identification and movement records are now electronic and mandatory.

    WHY
    Since November 5, 2024, APHIS requires official eartags on covered cattle and bison moving interstate to be both visually and electronically readable. Dealers handle animals from many origins and must maintain identification and movement records for disease traceback.
    LOSS
    Inability to produce movement records in a disease investigation puts the dealer at the centre of a traceback, and blocks interstate movement in the meantime.
    ASK
    How do you record animal identification and movement through your operation?
