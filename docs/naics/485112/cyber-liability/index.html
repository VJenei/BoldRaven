Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 485112

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 485112, Commuter Rail Systems, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Positive train control is federally mandated and safety critical.

    WHY
    Commuter rail operates under a positive train control mandate requiring systems that prevent train-to-train collisions, overspeed derailments, and incursions into work zones. PTC is a networked system integrating onboard, wayside, and back office components.
    LOSS
    Trains cannot operate on PTC-required lines without a functioning system, which suspends service for a commuting population with no alternative capacity. A compromise of train control is a mass casualty exposure.
    ASK
    How is your positive train control back office network protected and segmented?
  2. 02

    TSA security directives impose cybersecurity duties on passenger rail.

    WHY
    TSA has issued cybersecurity directives to higher-risk passenger railroad carriers requiring a designated cybersecurity coordinator, reporting of incidents to CISA within 24 hours, an incident response plan, and vulnerability assessments, alongside network segmentation and access control requirements.
    LOSS
    TSA enforcement and civil penalties, plus mandated remediation on timelines that consume agency resources while service continues.
    ASK
    Have you designated a cybersecurity coordinator under the TSA rail directives?
  3. 03

    Ticketing and rider data serve a captive commuting population.

    WHY
    Commuter rail sells monthly passes and tickets through apps and machines, holding payment methods, commuter benefit enrolments, and travel patterns that reveal home and work locations for hundreds of thousands of riders.
    LOSS
    Breach exposing travel patterns that map home to workplace for a large population, which is a personal safety concern rather than a purely financial one, plus loss of fare revenue during the outage.
    ASK
    Does your ticketing data link individual riders to their regular origin and destination?
