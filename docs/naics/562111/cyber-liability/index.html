Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 562111

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 562111, Solid Waste Collection, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Route and dispatch systems run collection for entire municipalities.

    WHY
    Collection operations dispatch hundreds of trucks against routes, service verification, and onboard computers, with municipal contracts specifying service levels and missed pickup penalties. Uncollected waste accumulates visibly within days.
    LOSS
    Missed collections across a service area with contractual penalties, municipal escalation, and public health complaints. Waste does not stop being generated because the routing system is down.
    ASK
    How many households or businesses would notice within 48 hours if your routes could not run?
  2. 02

    Billing depends on service verification across thousands of accounts.

    WHY
    Commercial and residential accounts are billed from service records, container inventories, and weight data, with onboard systems verifying that a container was serviced. Disputes are resolved from that record.
    LOSS
    Unbillable service across the customer base and disputes resolved against you where service verification cannot be produced, on a business with thin margins and high fixed costs.
    ASK
    How is service to each container verified and turned into a bill?
  3. 03

    Municipal contracts carry reporting and diversion obligations.

    WHY
    Public sector contracts require tonnage reporting, diversion rate documentation, and service level reporting, sometimes with recycling mandates tied to state law. Reporting is a payment condition and a compliance obligation.
    LOSS
    Contract payment withheld and compliance findings where tonnage and diversion reporting cannot be produced, with municipal contracts subject to termination and rebid.
    ASK
    What tonnage and diversion reporting do your municipal contracts require?
