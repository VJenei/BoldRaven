Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 325193

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 325193, Ethyl Alcohol Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Fermentation and distillation run continuously with fire risk.

    WHY
    Ethanol plants run continuous fermentation, distillation, and dehydration producing a flammable liquid at large scale, with distillation columns, storage tanks, and loading racks all handling flammable material under control.
    LOSS
    Fire or explosion in distillation or storage destroying plant sections, with a rebuild measured in many months and the plant's contracted ethanol supply unmet throughout.
    ASK
    How are your distillation and storage systems monitored and interlocked?
  2. 02

    Renewable fuel credits depend on documented production.

    WHY
    Ethanol production generates renewable identification numbers under the Renewable Fuel Standard, with generation tied to documented feedstock, production volume, and pathway compliance. Those credits are a substantial component of plant revenue.
    LOSS
    Credits that cannot be substantiated are invalidated with EPA enforcement, and RIN fraud has produced criminal prosecutions in this industry, so documentation failures attract serious attention.
    ASK
    How is RIN generation documented against production and feedstock records?
  3. 03

    Grain procurement and co-product sales run against markets.

    WHY
    Plants buy corn and sell ethanol and distillers grains, with margin determined by the spread across three markets and managed with futures positions. Grain receiving, storage, and co-product loading run continuously.
    LOSS
    Unmanaged crush margin exposure through a market move, plus grain receiving and co-product loading stopped, which backs up both grower deliveries and feed customers.
    ASK
    How is your crush margin position tracked and hedged?
