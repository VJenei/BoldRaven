Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 922160

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 922160, Fire Protection, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Dispatch failure means nobody comes.

    • Emergency calls are received and dispatched through connected systems.
    • Fires and medical emergencies are in progress when the call arrives.
    • Response time determines survival.
    ASK
    How are emergency calls received and dispatched to your units?
  2. 02

    Preplans and hazard data guide response.

    • Building preplans, hazardous material data, and hydrant locations guide crews.
    • Crews arrive and act on that information.
    • Wrong or missing data sends crews into a building blind.
    ASK
    What building preplan and hazard data do your crews depend on?
  3. 03

    Patient care records carry health information.

    • Emergency medical responses generate patient care records.
    • Health privacy obligations apply to those records.
    • Every affected patient must be notified.
    ASK
    How many patient care records do you hold?
