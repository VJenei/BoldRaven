Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333912

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333912, Air and Gas Compressor Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Compressors handle gas at pressure and fail violently.

    WHY
    Compressors raise gas to high pressure, with relief protection, temperature control, and materials determining safe operation. Failure of a pressurised component or handling of flammable gas produces explosion and fire.
    LOSS
    A compressor failure releasing flammable gas or rupturing at pressure, with fire, injury, and destruction of the plant area, investigated against the manufacturer's design and test records.
    ASK
    What gases and pressures do your compressors handle?
  2. 02

    Plant air and process gas supply stops production when it fails.

    WHY
    Compressed air and process gas are utilities that entire plants depend on. Compressor failure stops instrumentation, pneumatics, and process equipment across the site simultaneously.
    LOSS
    Compressor failures across an installed base stopping production at customer plants, with claims driven by their production losses rather than the compressor value.
    ASK
    What stops at a customer plant when your compressor stops?
  3. 03

    Compressors ship with connected controls and monitoring.

    WHY
    Industrial compressors include controllers with network connectivity for monitoring, sequencing, and remote diagnostics that link to manufacturer platforms and sit inside plant networks.
    LOSS
    Compromise of compressor controls affecting operation or providing a path into customer plant networks, requiring firmware remediation at every installed site.
    ASK
    How do your compressor controllers connect back to your monitoring systems?
