Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311514

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311514, Dry, Condensed, and Evaporated Dairy Product Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Powdered dairy carries salmonella risk that survives for years.

    • Salmonella survives in dry powder and dry plant environments indefinitely.
    • Environmental monitoring in the dryer environment is closely inspected.
    • Infant formula and powder recalls have been national events.
    ASK
    What environmental monitoring records do you keep for your dryer environment?
  2. 02

    Spray drying runs continuously under process control.

    • Evaporation and spray drying run to controlled temperature and moisture targets.
    • Product above target moisture supports microbial growth.
    • A control failure produces unstable product across the whole run.
    ASK
    How are your drying parameters and finished moisture recorded per lot?
  3. 03

    Powder is an ingredient shipped worldwide.

    • Dairy powders go into food manufacturing and export markets.
    • One lot reaches many customers across many countries.
    • A contamination event follows the powder into every product.
    ASK
    How many customers could receive product from a single production lot?
