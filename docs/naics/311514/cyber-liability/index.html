Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311514

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311514, Dry, Condensed, and Evaporated Dairy Product Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Powdered dairy has caused large salmonella outbreaks including in infant formula.

    WHY
    Dry dairy powders including milk powder and infant formula bases are low moisture products where salmonella and Cronobacter survive for long periods. Infant formula contamination has produced recalls, illness, and death in infants, with congressional and FDA scrutiny.
    LOSS
    A recall of powdered product with long shelf life distributed globally, with infant illness or death where formula is involved, and regulatory and criminal exposure at the highest level of severity.
    ASK
    Do you produce powders used in infant or medical nutrition?
  2. 02

    Spray drying is continuous, energy intensive, and control dependent.

    WHY
    Evaporation and spray drying run continuously with feed concentration, inlet and outlet temperature, and powder moisture controlled throughout. Dryer fires and product outside moisture specification are both consequences of losing control.
    LOSS
    A dryer fire destroying the tower with months of rebuild time, or off-specification powder produced continuously until detected, on a product where moisture determines shelf stability.
    ASK
    How is dryer outlet temperature and powder moisture controlled?
  3. 03

    Powder is an ingredient in a very wide range of products.

    WHY
    Dairy powders are supplied to bakery, confectionery, beverage, and nutrition manufacturers who incorporate them into finished products distributed further. Contamination propagates through every downstream product.
    LOSS
    A recall cascading through customers' finished goods, with claims from every manufacturer whose products contained the affected lots and a recall scope determined by your traceability records.
    ASK
    How many downstream manufacturers could be affected by one contaminated powder lot?
