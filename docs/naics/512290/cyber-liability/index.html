Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 512290

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 512290, Other Sound Recording Industries, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Audio production for clients holds work that must be delivered on air dates.

    WHY
    Businesses in this category produce audiobooks, podcasts, commercials, and audio content against publication and broadcast dates. Session material, edits, and finished masters sit with the producer until delivery, and the client has committed a schedule.
    LOSS
    Missed delivery on advertising or broadcast dates, where the client's media buy is already placed and the value of the missed placement far exceeds the production fee.
    ASK
    Are your deliverables tied to client broadcast or publication dates?
  2. 02

    Talent and rights records determine what can be used and for how long.

    WHY
    Voice talent agreements, music licences, and usage terms limit where and how long audio may be used, with residual and renewal obligations. Using audio beyond its licensed term is an infringement.
    LOSS
    Continued use of audio past its licence term generates infringement and residual claims from talent unions and rights holders, with liability accruing per use.
    ASK
    How do you track licence and usage terms for talent and music in your productions?
  3. 03

    Client audio archives accumulate over years of relationships.

    WHY
    Producers retain client project archives to support revisions, versioning, and reuse, building up years of material representing the client's brand assets. Clients expect that material to be available on request.
    LOSS
    Loss of client archives means brand audio, campaign assets, and series continuity cannot be reproduced, and the client rebuilds from scratch at your expense.
    ASK
    How many years of client project archives do you hold?
