Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 212321

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 212321, Construction Sand and Gravel Mining, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Ready-mix and paving customers order same day through integrated dispatch.

    WHY
    Sand and gravel producers are tied directly into ready-mix and asphalt customer ordering, often through shared dispatch and ticketing platforms. Concrete is placed to a schedule that cannot slip, because crews, pumps, and finishers are already booked.
    LOSS
    A dispatch or ticketing failure stops pours across multiple customer job sites, generating delay claims from contractors and permanent loss of accounts to competitors who could deliver.
    ASK
    Do your customers order material through a shared dispatch or ticketing platform?
  2. 02

    Wash plants and dredges run under automated control.

    WHY
    Sand and gravel processing uses screens, classifiers, wash plants, and in many pits a floating dredge, all under PLC control with water recycling and settling pond management. Product specification depends on classifier control.
    LOSS
    Off-specification sand rejected by concrete producers, plus a settling pond or process water incident if level controls fail, which is a reportable discharge.
    ASK
    Do you operate a dredge or wash plant under automated control?
  3. 03

    Reserves, permits, and reclamation obligations are documented electronically.

    WHY
    Pit permits, mining plans, reclamation bonds, groundwater monitoring, and annual production reporting to the state are all documentary. Aggregate operations are permitted decades in advance and permit conditions are enforced against the record.
    LOSS
    Inability to demonstrate compliance with permit conditions and reclamation progress, risking bond forfeiture and permit suspension.
    ASK
    How do you maintain your permit compliance and reclamation records?
