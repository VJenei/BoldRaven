Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 212321

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 212321, Construction Sand and Gravel Mining, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Loadout and ticketing are how you get paid.

    • Trucks are weighed, ticketed, and invoiced through the scale system.
    • Hundreds of loads a day pass through that one system.
    • A scale outage means selling with no record or not selling.
    ASK
    How are your truck scales and load tickets recorded?
  2. 02

    Concrete and asphalt plants run out of material in hours.

    • Ready mix and paving customers hold almost no stockpile.
    • Deliveries are scheduled load by load against pours.
    • A supply stop halts pours and paving across your customers.
    ASK
    How much stockpile do your ready mix and paving customers hold?
  3. 03

    Material must meet tested specifications for public work.

    • Gradation and quality results certify sand and gravel for specified uses.
    • Agencies and contractors verify those results.
    • Uncertified material is rejected at the job site.
    ASK
    How are your material test results recorded and certified?
