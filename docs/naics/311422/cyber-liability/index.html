Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311422

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311422, Specialty Canning, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Low acid and acidified food processing is federally regulated.

    WHY
    Specialty canned products including soups, sauces, and prepared foods fall under low acid or acidified food regulations requiring registered processes, trained operators, and continuous processing records. Acidified products depend on pH control to prevent botulism.
    LOSS
    Product with unverifiable process or pH records must be destroyed, and product that shipped creates botulism exposure with fatality potential and criminal investigation.
    ASK
    How are pH and thermal process records captured for each production lot?
  2. 02

    Complex formulations concentrate allergen risk.

    WHY
    Specialty canned foods combine dairy, wheat, soy, seafood, and other allergens in formulations produced on shared equipment, with label accuracy depending on formulation control and packaging management.
    LOSS
    An allergen recall from a formulation or labelling error, with injury exposure and a recall scope determined by whether affected lots can be identified from production records.
    ASK
    How is the label verified against the formulation actually produced?
  3. 03

    Co-packing means producing other companies' products.

    WHY
    Specialty canners frequently co-pack for brand owners, holding customer formulations, process authority documentation, and specifications. The co-packer executes processes the brand owner is responsible for.
    LOSS
    Loss of customer formulations and scheduled process documentation, which cannot be recreated without a process authority and stops production of every affected customer product.
    ASK
    Where are your scheduled process filings and customer formulations held?
