Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111992

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111992, Peanut Farming, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Buying points test every load and set the payment.

    • Loads are graded for moisture, foreign material, and damage on arrival.
    • Aflatoxin testing determines whether a load is edible or crushing grade.
    • A downgraded load is worth a fraction of contract price.
    ASK
    How are your load grading and test results recorded?
  2. 02

    Digging and combining run on a narrow maturity window.

    • Peanuts are dug at maturity and combined a few days later.
    • Equipment and drying capacity are scheduled around that window.
    • Digging late or early costs yield and grade at the same time.
    ASK
    How are your harvest and drying schedules managed?
  3. 03

    Drying must be controlled or the crop is downgraded.

    • Peanuts are dried in trailers under controlled heat and airflow.
    • Overheating causes off flavour and rejects the load.
    • A dryer fault ruins the peanuts in that trailer.
    ASK
    Are your drying trailers run by automated temperature controls?
