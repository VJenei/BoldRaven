Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111421

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111421, Nursery and Tree Production, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Shipping plants across state lines requires inspection and certificates.

    • States require phytosanitary certificates and nursery inspection before plants move.
    • Shipment, origin, and treatment records support those certificates.
    • A load without paperwork is turned back or destroyed at the border.
    ASK
    Do you ship nursery stock across state lines?
  2. 02

    Irrigation failure kills container stock in a single day.

    • Container plants have no soil reserve and are watered daily.
    • Irrigation is run by automated timers, valves, and controllers.
    • One missed cycle in heat can kill an entire block.
    ASK
    Are your irrigation systems run by automated timers or controllers?
  3. 03

    Sales are compressed into a spring window.

    • Most of the year's shipping happens over a few weeks in spring.
    • Orders, availability, and shipping schedules run on connected systems.
    • Volume missed in spring cannot be sold later in the year.
    ASK
    What share of your annual sales ships between March and June?
