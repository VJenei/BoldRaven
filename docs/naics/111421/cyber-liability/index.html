Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111421

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111421, Nursery and Tree Production, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Mail-order plant sales run through an e-commerce platform with a short selling season.

    WHY
    Nurseries sell direct to consumers online and ship live plants nationwide. Most annual revenue is earned in a six to ten week spring window. The operation runs a shopping cart, stored card data, and a customer database. PCI DSS 4.0 became mandatory on March 31, 2025 and added requirements for monitoring e-commerce payment page scripts.
    LOSS
    A cart compromise or ransomware event during the spring window affects the majority of annual revenue. Additional costs include card brand assessments, forensics, and customer notification across all states.
    ASK
    What percentage of your annual sales occurs in your peak eight weeks, and do you sell online?
  2. 02

    Interstate shipping requires state phytosanitary certification and quarantine compliance.

    WHY
    Shipping nursery stock across state lines requires state phytosanitary certification, nursery licenses, and compliance with quarantine orders for pests including sudden oak death, boxwood blight, emerald ash borer, and spotted lanternfly. Certificates and shipping records are electronic. Quarantine compliance requires documented origin and inspection history per shipment.
    LOSS
    A stop-sale order or shipping ban during the spring window while compliance cannot be documented, plus regulatory action if traceback fails during a pest investigation.
    ASK
    Do you ship nursery stock across state lines, and how are inspection and shipping records stored?
  3. 03

    Container stock is watered by automated irrigation and dies quickly without it.

    WHY
    Container nursery stock holds very little water reserve and is irrigated by automated boom, overhead, and drip systems on timed and sensor-driven controllers. Many are remotely managed. In summer, container stock requires water daily or more often.
    LOSS
    Two days without irrigation in summer kills container inventory representing one to five years of growing.
    ASK
    Is your irrigation controlled by an automated system, and is it monitored remotely?
