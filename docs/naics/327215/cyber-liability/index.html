Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 327215

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 327215, Glass Product Manufacturing Made of Purchased Glass, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Safety glazing must meet impact standards or people are cut.

    WHY
    Tempered and laminated safety glazing is required in doors, sidelights, shower enclosures, and other hazardous locations, certified to impact standards and permanently marked. Untempered glass in those locations breaks into sharp shards.
    LOSS
    Non-safety glass installed in hazardous locations causing lacerating injuries, with the fabricator identified from the absence or falsity of the safety marking and liability for injuries that the standard exists to prevent.
    ASK
    How is safety glazing certification verified and marked on your product?
  2. 02

    Insulating glass units are fabricated to project measurements.

    WHY
    Insulating units and architectural glass are cut, tempered, and assembled to dimensions taken from field measurement for a specific building. Nothing is stock and tempered glass cannot be cut after processing.
    LOSS
    Units fabricated to wrong dimensions that cannot be modified, requiring complete remake with tempering and assembly lead times while the building remains open.
    ASK
    How are field dimensions transferred into your cutting and fabrication systems?
  3. 03

    Tempering and lamination parameters determine performance.

    WHY
    Tempering furnace parameters determine surface compression and therefore whether the glass qualifies as safety glazing. Lamination requires autoclave cycles producing the bond that holds fragments in place.
    LOSS
    Glass processed outside parameters that does not meet safety standards despite being marked as such, which is a latent defect discovered only when the glass breaks and injures someone.
    ASK
    How are tempering and autoclave parameters recorded per production batch?
