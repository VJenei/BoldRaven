Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 221113

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 221113, Nuclear Electric Power Generation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Federal regulation requires a documented cyber security programme for the plant.

    WHY
    NRC regulation 10 CFR 73.54 requires licensees to protect digital computer and communication systems associated with safety, security, and emergency preparedness functions from cyber attack, under an NRC-approved cyber security plan. Compliance is inspected. This is a licence condition, not a best practice.
    LOSS
    Findings against the cyber security plan escalate through the NRC action matrix, drawing increased inspection and, in serious cases, restrictions on operation. Regulatory response is disproportionate to the technical severity by design.
    ASK
    When was your cyber security plan under 10 CFR 73.54 last inspected?
  2. 02

    Business and support systems are increasingly digital even where safety systems are isolated.

    WHY
    Safety systems are deliberately analogue or isolated, but work management, procedures, radiation dose tracking, chemistry, outage planning, and the emergency plan implementing procedures are all digital. A refuelling outage is scheduled to the hour across thousands of contractor workers using those systems.
    LOSS
    An outage extended by loss of work management and scheduling costs the replacement power for every extra day, commonly over $1 million per day for a large unit.
    ASK
    How is your outage work management system protected and recovered?
  3. 03

    Security, personnel, and dose records are highly sensitive and highly regulated.

    WHY
    Licensees hold unescorted access authorisation records, behavioural observation programme data, fitness for duty testing, and occupational radiation dose histories for every worker. Safeguards information about the security plan is separately controlled and its disclosure is a federal offence.
    LOSS
    A breach that exposes safeguards information or personnel security data is both a serious regulatory event and a personal harm to workers, with notification and enforcement running in parallel.
    ASK
    Where is safeguards information and access authorisation data stored?
