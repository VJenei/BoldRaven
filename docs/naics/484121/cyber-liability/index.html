Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 484121

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 484121, General Freight Trucking, Long-Distance, Truckload, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Fictitious pickup and identity cloning have grown into the dominant theft method.

    WHY
    Strategic cargo theft grew dramatically between 2022 and 2024, with fictitious pickups rising from an average of around 66 a year to several hundred. Criminals clone motor carrier numbers, create fraudulent carrier identities, compromise load board credentials, and use double brokering to take possession of loads legitimately in appearance.
    LOSS
    A full truckload of $150,000 to $500,000 diverted, with cargo liability to the shipper attaching to the contracted carrier regardless of who physically took it. Voluntary release generally defeats a theft claim.
    ASK
    How do you authenticate a carrier or driver before tendering a load?
  2. 02

    The transportation management system is the whole operation.

    WHY
    Load assignment, routing, fuel purchasing, driver settlement, customer billing, and safety compliance all run through a TMS integrated with telematics and ELDs. Long-haul operations coordinate hundreds of trucks across the country in real time.
    LOSS
    Trucks running without dispatch and drivers without instructions, with unbillable trips and no visibility of where equipment or freight is. Fixed costs on tractors and trailers continue regardless.
    ASK
    Where are your trucks right now, and how do you know?
  3. 03

    Safety and driver qualification records determine whether you can operate.

    WHY
    Carriers must maintain driver qualification files, drug and alcohol testing records including Clearinghouse queries, hours of service records, and vehicle maintenance files. FMCSA audits examine these, and after a crash the plaintiff's first request is the driver file.
    LOSS
    An unsatisfactory safety rating removes operating authority, and after a serious crash the absence of a driver qualification file is close to indefensible in litigation.
    ASK
    How many driver qualification files do you maintain, and where?
