Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 711510

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 711510, Independent Artists, Writers, and Performers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Unpublished work is the asset and it exists in one place.

    WHY
    Independent creators hold manuscripts, compositions, recordings, and artwork in progress, frequently on a single computer with no systematic backup. A sustained phishing campaign against publishing specifically targeted unpublished manuscripts for years, resulting in a federal indictment.
    LOSS
    Loss of unpublished work that cannot be recreated, or its disclosure before publication which undermines the release and, for some rights, can affect protection.
    ASK
    Where is the only copy of your current work in progress?
  2. 02

    Income comes from many small payers through platforms.

    WHY
    Independent creators receive royalties, platform payments, licensing fees, and direct sales through numerous accounts, each with banking details and tax information. Platform account takeover redirects income silently.
    LOSS
    Redirected royalty and platform payments discovered months later, with recovery from platforms difficult and the creator having no finance function to notice the absence quickly.
    ASK
    How many platforms hold your banking details for payment?
  3. 03

    Fan and customer data comes with direct sales.

    WHY
    Creators selling directly hold customer contact and payment information through their own storefronts, mailing lists, and membership platforms, frequently without any security review of the tools chosen.
    LOSS
    Customer payment and contact data exposed through a personal storefront, with notification obligations falling on an individual creator with no capacity to fund them.
    ASK
    Do you sell directly to fans and hold their payment or contact details?
