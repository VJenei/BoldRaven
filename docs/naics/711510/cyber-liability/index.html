Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 711510

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 711510, Independent Artists, Writers, and Performers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Your work exists as files before it is published.

    • Manuscripts, recordings, and artwork exist before release.
    • Publishers and platforms build releases around that timing.
    • A leak destroys the release and the value.
    ASK
    Where is your unpublished work stored?
  2. 02

    Rights and royalty records determine your income.

    • Ownership, licensing, and royalty records determine what you are owed.
    • Payments come from many sources over years.
    • Wrong or lost records mean money that never arrives.
    ASK
    Where are your rights and royalty records held?
  3. 03

    Bookings and commitments run your calendar.

    • Performances, deliveries, and appearances are committed to dates.
    • Venues and clients plan around those dates.
    • A missed commitment costs the fee and the relationship.
    ASK
    How are your bookings and delivery commitments recorded?
