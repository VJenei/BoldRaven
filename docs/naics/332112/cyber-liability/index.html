Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 332112

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 332112, Nonferrous Forging, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Aerospace forgings are qualified and traceable to melt.

    WHY
    Titanium and aluminium forgings for aerospace are produced under approved processes with full traceability to melt, process records, and testing. Aerospace quality accreditation is a condition of supply.
    LOSS
    Non-conforming forgings in aerospace structures requiring identification across aircraft in service, with grounding and replacement of structural components.
    ASK
    Do your forgings go into aerospace primary or secondary structure?
  2. 02

    Process parameters determine metallurgical properties.

    WHY
    Nonferrous forging controls temperature, strain rate, and subsequent heat treatment to develop properties, with titanium in particular requiring tight control and contamination avoidance. Properties are verified by destructive testing on samples.
    LOSS
    Parts with correct geometry but wrong microstructure, which is invisible to inspection and appears as premature failure in the demanding applications these forgings serve.
    ASK
    How are forging and heat treatment parameters recorded per lot?
  3. 03

    Accreditation and customer approval control market access.

    WHY
    Aerospace and defence customers require accreditation under industry programmes with process approvals audited regularly. Records demonstrate that approved processes were followed for every part.
    LOSS
    Loss of accreditation where process records cannot be produced, removing eligibility to supply the aerospace customers that constitute the business.
    ASK
    Which process accreditations does your operation hold, and what records support them?
