Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 484110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 484110, General Freight Trucking, Local, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Cargo theft by deception now targets carriers through their own systems.

    WHY
    Estimated cargo theft losses across the United States and Canada reached nearly $725 million in 2025, with strategic theft accounting for close to a third of incidents. Criminals use phishing, spoofed domains, compromised credentials, and cloned motor carrier numbers to arrange fictitious pickups and divert loads. The average value per theft rose to roughly $274,000.
    LOSS
    A load released to a fraudulent party is generally not covered as a theft because it was handed over voluntarily. Cargo liability to the shipper attaches regardless, and repeated incidents remove you from broker load boards.
    ASK
    How do you verify that the party collecting a load is who they claim to be?
  2. 02

    Electronic logging devices are federally mandated and connect to the truck.

    WHY
    ELDs are required to record hours of service and connect to the vehicle's engine control module, transmitting data to fleet systems. Records must be produced to enforcement on demand and retained for six months. Some devices have documented security weaknesses because compliance certification is self-certified.
    LOSS
    Out of service orders where hours cannot be produced during a roadside inspection, and a compromised telematics path reaching vehicle systems is a safety exposure rather than a data one.
    ASK
    Which ELD provider do you use, and how is that data transmitted and stored?
  3. 03

    Dispatch and driver settlement stop without the system.

    WHY
    Local trucking dispatches loads, captures proof of delivery, and settles driver pay from a transportation management system. Drivers are paid weekly on captured trips, and customers are billed from the same records.
    LOSS
    Unbillable deliveries and unpaid drivers in the same week, and drivers who do not get paid do not stay, which in a capacity-constrained market is a lasting loss.
    ASK
    How is proof of delivery captured and turned into an invoice?
