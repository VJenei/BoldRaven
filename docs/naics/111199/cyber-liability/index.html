Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111199

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111199, All Other Grain Farming, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Specialty grain premiums are conditional on contract documentation.

    WHY
    Barley, oats, sorghum, rye, and specialty grains are grown on contract to maltsters, millers, and feed buyers. Contracts specify protein, plumpness, germination, test weight, and DON limits. The premium is paid only when the specification documentation is produced.
    LOSS
    A malting barley contract paying $2 to $4 over feed barley reverts to feed value across the contracted volume.
    ASK
    Do you sell any grain under a specification contract that pays a premium over commodity price?
  2. 02

    Specialty grains often have only one buyer within hauling distance.

    WHY
    Unlike corn and soybeans, small grains and specialty grains frequently have a single realistic buyer in the area. The FBI documented multiple grain cooperatives encrypted within a three week window during the 2021 harvest.
    LOSS
    Grain with no delivery point during harvest and limited on-farm storage, forcing a distressed sale or ground storage losses.
    ASK
    How many buyers will take this crop within your normal hauling distance?
  3. 03

    Breach notification law applies to farms of any size.

    WHY
    Any farm with employees holds W-2 data, direct deposit routing numbers, and I-9 forms. Many also hold H-2A documentation. All 50 states require notification when personal information is breached. There is no exemption based on revenue or number of employees.
    LOSS
    Forensics, required notification, credit monitoring, and state attorney general response cost $25,000 to $100,000 for a small employer before any lawsuit.
    ASK
    How many employees do you hold Social Security numbers and direct deposit information for?
