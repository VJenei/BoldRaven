Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 722330

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 722330, Mobile Food Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    The point of sale on the truck is the entire business.

    WHY
    Mobile food operations run card payment through mobile point of sale devices over cellular, with no cash-only fallback for most customers. Service is at a fixed location for a limited window and the queue does not wait.
    LOSS
    Inability to take payment during a service window, which for a truck at an event or a lunch rush is the entire day's revenue, and food already prepared that cannot be sold.
    ASK
    What proportion of your sales are card rather than cash?
  2. 02

    Event bookings and permits are dated and location-specific.

    WHY
    Mobile operators book festivals, corporate events, and commissary and permit slots for specific dates and locations, frequently with deposits paid. Health permits are location and jurisdiction specific.
    LOSS
    Missed event bookings with deposits paid and food purchased, on dates that represent a disproportionate share of monthly revenue for a single-unit operator.
    ASK
    How many event bookings are you holding with deposits already paid?
  3. 03

    Food safety records are required despite the small scale.

    WHY
    Mobile units are inspected by health departments with requirements for temperature logs, commissary agreements, water and waste handling, and food handler certification. Records are examined during inspection.
    LOSS
    Permit suspension where temperature logs and certification records cannot be produced, which stops operation entirely for a business with a single revenue-generating unit.
    ASK
    How are your temperature logs and food handler certifications recorded?
