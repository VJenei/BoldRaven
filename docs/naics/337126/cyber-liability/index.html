Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 337126

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 337126, Household Furniture (except Wood and Upholstered) Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Collapse and tip-over injure users.

    • Metal and plastic furniture carries load ratings and stability requirements.
    • Reporting obligations apply once a hazard is known.
    • Recall reach depends entirely on distribution records.
    ASK
    Could you identify which retailers received a specific production run?
  2. 02

    Children's furniture carries mandatory testing requirements.

    • Products for children require third party testing and a certificate.
    • Lead and phthalate limits apply as well.
    • Uncertified children's products cannot lawfully be sold.
    ASK
    Which of your products are children's products requiring third party testing?
  3. 03

    Volume ships through a few very large customers.

    • Product ships to large retailers and marketplaces under vendor requirements.
    • Delivery windows and packaging rules are enforced by chargeback.
    • Those customers can reallocate shelf and listing space.
    ASK
    What proportion of your sales goes through your largest customer?
