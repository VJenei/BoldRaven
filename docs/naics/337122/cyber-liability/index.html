Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 337122

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 337122, Nonupholstered Wood Household Furniture Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Clothing storage furniture is now subject to a mandatory stability standard.

    WHY
    The STURDY Act required CPSC to establish a mandatory stability standard for clothing storage units after tip-over deaths of children. Compliance requires testing and applies to units manufactured after the effective date.
    LOSS
    Units manufactured that do not meet the stability standard, which cannot lawfully be sold, with recall from retail and consumers and CPSC enforcement, plus litigation where a tip-over injured a child.
    ASK
    How do you document stability testing for clothing storage units?
  2. 02

    Formaldehyde emissions from composite panels are federally regulated.

    WHY
    Composite wood panels in furniture are subject to EPA TSCA Title VI emission standards with certification and record retention required through the supply chain.
    LOSS
    Compliance records unavailable on request, which is a TSCA violation with penalties, and finished goods that cannot lawfully be sold.
    ASK
    How are formaldehyde compliance certifications retained for panel purchases?
  3. 03

    Retail supply runs on vendor systems with delivery windows.

    WHY
    Furniture ships to retailers and directly to consumers under EDI with routing, labelling, and delivery requirements, with the category concentrated in a few large retail and online customers.
    LOSS
    Order and shipping systems unavailable, with missed delivery windows, vendor chargebacks, and direct to consumer orders unfulfilled at the peak of the selling season.
    ASK
    What share of your volume ships through your two largest customers?
