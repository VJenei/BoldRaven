Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 928120

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 928120, International Affairs, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Diplomatic and international communications are intelligence targets.

    WHY
    Organisations in international affairs hold diplomatic communications, negotiating positions, assessments of foreign parties, and information about local staff and contacts. Foreign intelligence services target this material continuously.
    LOSS
    Exposure of negotiating positions and assessments damaging relationships and ongoing negotiations, and disclosure of local contacts and staff creating personal risk for individuals in countries where the affiliation is dangerous.
    ASK
    Would exposure of your contacts put any individual at risk in their own country?
  2. 02

    Local staff and contacts are exposed personally.

    WHY
    International operations depend on locally engaged staff, partners, and contacts whose association with a foreign government or organisation is sensitive and in some countries dangerous. Employment and contact records identify them.
    LOSS
    Identification of local staff and contacts leading to harassment, arrest, or violence, which is a direct personal consequence for individuals who cannot be protected once the information is out.
    ASK
    Do your records identify local staff and contacts in sensitive countries?
  3. 03

    Programme and assistance data identifies beneficiaries.

    WHY
    International programmes hold beneficiary records, partner organisation details, and programme locations, frequently in contexts where receiving foreign assistance is politically sensitive or personally dangerous.
    LOSS
    Exposure of beneficiary and partner data in environments where association with foreign programmes is punished, with consequences for individuals and partner organisations that no remediation addresses.
    ASK
    Would beneficiaries or partners face consequences if their participation became known?
