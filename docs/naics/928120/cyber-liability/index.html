Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 928120

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 928120, International Affairs, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Diplomatic communications are a top intelligence target.

    • Cables, negotiations, and assessments are targeted by foreign intelligence services.
    • Disclosure damages negotiations and relationships permanently.
    • Sources and contacts abroad can be identified and endangered.
    ASK
    What controls protect your diplomatic communications and reporting?
  2. 02

    Personnel and contact data can endanger people.

    • Records identify local staff, contacts, and sources abroad.
    • In some countries that association is dangerous.
    • Disclosure leads to arrest, harassment, and worse.
    ASK
    What personnel and local contact data do you hold?
  3. 03

    Consular systems hold citizen and visa data.

    • Passport, visa, and consular records carry identity and travel data.
    • Those records cover citizens and foreign nationals alike.
    • Every affected individual must be notified.
    ASK
    How many passport, visa, and consular records do your systems hold?
