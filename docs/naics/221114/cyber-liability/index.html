Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 221114

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 221114, Solar Electric Power Generation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Inverters and trackers are managed remotely across the whole site.

    • Inverters, trackers, and site controllers are configured and updated over network connections.
    • One management platform can change settings on the entire fleet.
    • Output can be reduced or stopped everywhere at once.
    ASK
    Can your inverters and trackers be configured remotely from a central platform?
  2. 02

    Grid reliability and interconnection requirements apply to your controls.

    • Larger facilities fall within federal reliability security requirements.
    • Interconnection agreements impose control and response obligations.
    • Non-compliance carries penalties per day against the registered entity.
    ASK
    Which of your systems fall within grid reliability security requirements?
  3. 03

    Revenue depends on metered output being recorded correctly.

    • Production is metered and settled under long term power purchase agreements.
    • Renewable energy credits are issued from that same recorded output.
    • Lost or corrupted generation data means unbillable and uncreditable production.
    ASK
    How is your generation metering data recorded and reported for settlement?
