Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 811191

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 811191, Automotive Oil Change and Lubrication Shops, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Service history determines what the vehicle needs.

    WHY
    Quick lube operations depend on vehicle lookup by licence plate or VIN to determine specification, capacity, filter, and service interval, with customer history recorded across visits. Using the wrong specification oil damages modern engines.
    LOSS
    Wrong specification lubricant used across multiple vehicles, causing engine damage claims that far exceed the service price, with the shop unable to demonstrate what was specified and used.
    ASK
    How do your technicians determine the correct oil specification for a vehicle?
  2. 02

    High transaction volume concentrates card data.

    WHY
    Quick service formats process very high transaction counts at low average ticket across multiple bays and locations, with card payment at the bay. PCI DSS 4.0 is fully mandatory since March 31, 2025.
    LOSS
    Card compromise across a high volume operation, where assessments and reissuance costs are driven by transaction count rather than the size of the business.
    ASK
    How many card transactions do you process across your locations in a month?
  3. 03

    Customer records support marketing and warranty claims.

    WHY
    Shops retain customer contact details, vehicle records, and service histories used for reminder marketing and to support claims that manufacturer maintenance requirements were met. Customers rely on those records to preserve powertrain warranty.
    LOSS
    Customers unable to demonstrate maintenance history for a warranty claim, with the manufacturer declining coverage and the customer looking to the shop that held the records.
    ASK
    Do your customers rely on your records to prove maintenance for warranty purposes?
