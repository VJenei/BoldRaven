Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 324110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 324110, Petroleum Refineries, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Attackers have specifically targeted safety instrumented systems in this sector.

    WHY
    The Triton malware discovered in 2017 targeted safety instrumented systems at a petrochemical facility, with the apparent objective of disabling the safety layer that prevents a catastrophic release. Refineries operate distributed control systems alongside independent safety instrumented systems designed to bring units to a safe state.
    LOSS
    A compromised safety system permits an event the safety layer exists to prevent, which in a refinery means fire, explosion, and toxic release with multiple fatalities and destruction of process units. This is the highest consequence cyber scenario in industry.
    ASK
    How is your safety instrumented system separated and protected from the process control network?
  2. 02

    Process safety management is a federal requirement with enormous consequences.

    WHY
    Refineries are covered by OSHA process safety management and EPA risk management planning, requiring process safety information, mechanical integrity, management of change, and incident investigation with extensive documentation reviewed after any event.
    LOSS
    Regulatory findings following an incident where process safety documentation cannot be produced, with penalties, criminal referral in serious cases, and consent decrees imposing years of oversight.
    ASK
    Could you produce your process safety information and management of change records on demand?
  3. 03

    A refinery outage disrupts regional fuel supply.

    WHY
    Refineries supply pipelines, terminals, and markets with limited alternative capacity, and restarting units after an unplanned shutdown takes days to weeks. Product specifications change seasonally and cannot simply be sourced elsewhere.
    LOSS
    Regional fuel supply shortage with price effects, contractual supply failures, and lost margin measured in millions per day on a refinery whose fixed costs continue regardless.
    ASK
    What is your daily throughput, and what does a week of lost production represent?
