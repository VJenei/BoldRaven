Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 512240

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 512240, Sound Recording Studios, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Session recordings exist in one place.

    • Multitrack sessions and stems are the recorded performance.
    • Recreating them means rebooking musicians and studio time.
    • Some performances cannot be recreated at all.
    ASK
    Where are your session recordings and project files stored?
  2. 02

    You hold unreleased material for artists and labels.

    • Recordings exist before any release date.
    • Labels and artists build releases around that timing.
    • A leak destroys the release and ends the relationship.
    ASK
    What unreleased artist material do you hold?
  3. 03

    Studio time is booked and paid for in blocks.

    • Sessions are booked with artists, producers, and musicians scheduled.
    • Those bookings cannot easily be moved.
    • A lost day is paid for by someone and produces nothing.
    ASK
    What does a studio day cost your clients?
