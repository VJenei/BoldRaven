Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 512240

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 512240, Sound Recording Studios, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Unreleased masters and session files are irreplaceable and leak-sensitive.

    WHY
    Studios hold multitrack sessions, stems, and unreleased masters for artists and labels, frequently for high-profile projects. Leaked material ahead of release damages the campaign, and stolen stems circulate permanently.
    LOSS
    A leak destroys a release strategy and creates direct liability to the label under the studio's terms, plus reputational damage that removes the studio from consideration for future work.
    ASK
    How is unreleased client material stored and access controlled in your facility?
  2. 02

    Session data represents work that cannot be recreated.

    WHY
    Recording sessions capture performances that happened once, with session files, takes, and settings representing the accumulated work of a project. Musicians, engineers, and studio time were paid for once and are not repeatable in the same form.
    LOSS
    Loss of session material requires rerecording where possible at full cost, and where performers or arrangements cannot be reassembled, the work is simply gone.
    ASK
    How are session files backed up during and after a project?
  3. 03

    Client confidentiality is part of what studios sell.

    WHY
    Studios know who is recording what and with whom before it is public, including collaborations and projects that are commercially sensitive or deliberately secret. Booking records alone reveal this.
    LOSS
    Exposure of booking and project data reveals unannounced collaborations and release plans, which damages client relationships in an industry that runs on discretion and word of mouth.
    ASK
    Do your booking records reveal which artists are working together before it is announced?
