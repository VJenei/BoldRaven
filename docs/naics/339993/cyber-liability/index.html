Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 339993

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 339993, Fastener, Button, Needle, and Pin Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Small parts on children's products are a choking hazard.

    WHY
    Buttons, snaps, and fasteners on children's apparel are subject to small parts and attachment strength requirements, with children's products requiring third party testing and lead and phthalate compliance.
    LOSS
    Components detaching from children's apparel, with choking incidents, a CPSC recall of the finished garments across every brand that used the component, and testing records determining responsibility.
    ASK
    Which of your components go onto children's products requiring third party testing?
  2. 02

    Components are supplied into other manufacturers' products.

    WHY
    Fasteners and trim are supplied to apparel and product manufacturers who build them into finished goods. A component defect becomes a recall of every finished product containing it.
    LOSS
    A component defect requiring recall of finished goods across many customers, with recall cost driven by the garments and products rather than the components.
    ASK
    How many customer products could contain a single lot of your components?
  3. 03

    Production runs at very high volume with fast delivery expected.

    WHY
    Components are produced in millions and shipped against apparel and product production schedules that are already tight, with customers holding little inventory of trim.
    LOSS
    Order and production systems unavailable, with customer production lines stopped for want of trim components and their finished goods delivery dates missed.
    ASK
    How much trim inventory do your customers hold before production stops?
