Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 236115

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 236115, New Single-Family Housing Construction (except For-Sale Builders), needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Construction draws are paid by wire against emailed requests.

    WHY
    Custom home builders are paid in draws from a construction lender or directly by the owner, requested with lien waivers and inspection sign-offs sent by email. Subcontractors and suppliers are paid from those draws on the same cycle. Payment instructions change often enough that a change request does not look unusual.
    LOSS
    A diverted draw of $50,000 to $300,000. The builder still owes the subcontractors, so the loss is paid twice, and mechanics lien exposure follows if they go unpaid.
    ASK
    How do you verify a change of bank details from a subcontractor or supplier?
  2. 02

    You hold your clients' financial and personal information.

    WHY
    Custom builds require the owner's financing details, loan documents, allowance budgets, and often personal information gathered for the lender. Contracts, change orders, and selections are exchanged by email and stored in project folders for years after completion.
    LOSS
    Breach notification to clients whose financial documents you held, in a business where every future job comes from referral by those same clients.
    ASK
    Do you retain client financing or loan documents after a project closes?
  3. 03

    Plans, selections, and schedules are the only record of what was agreed.

    WHY
    Custom work runs on drawings, revisions, allowances, and a long chain of change orders and selection approvals. Disputes over what was specified are the most common source of litigation in residential construction, and the record is entirely digital.
    LOSS
    An unwinnable dispute over scope or allowance because the approved selections and change order chain cannot be produced, on a contract worth several hundred thousand dollars.
    ASK
    Where are your signed change orders and selection approvals stored?
