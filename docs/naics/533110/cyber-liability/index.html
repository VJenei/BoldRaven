Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 533110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 533110, Lessors of Nonfinancial Intangible Assets (except Copyrighted Works), needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    The asset exists only as documentation.

    WHY
    Patents, trademarks, trade secrets, and franchise rights are licensed for royalties, and their value depends entirely on registration records, licence agreements, assignment chains, and evidence of use. There is no physical asset to fall back on.
    LOSS
    An unprovable chain of title or licence terms makes the right unenforceable and unlicensable, which destroys the revenue stream even though the underlying registration still exists.
    ASK
    Where are your assignment chains and executed licence agreements held?
  2. 02

    Trade secrets stop being assets the moment they are disclosed.

    WHY
    Trade secret protection depends on the owner taking reasonable measures to keep the information secret. Disclosure ends the right permanently, and courts examine whether the owner's security measures were reasonable when deciding whether protection existed at all.
    LOSS
    A breach that discloses a licensed trade secret destroys the asset outright and may defeat the legal protection retroactively, since inadequate security undermines the claim that reasonable measures were taken.
    ASK
    Could you demonstrate that you took reasonable measures to protect your trade secrets?
  3. 03

    Royalty collection depends on licensee reporting and audit rights.

    WHY
    Licence income is calculated from licensee-reported usage, sales, or production, verified through audit rights exercised periodically. Reporting records and historical calculations are the basis for both collection and audit.
    LOSS
    Under-collection across licensees that cannot be identified or recovered, with audit rights unusable because the baseline records against which to compare are gone.
    ASK
    How do you verify that licensees are reporting their usage accurately?
