Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333243

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333243, Sawmill, Woodworking, and Paper Machinery Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Machines cut and press with force that kills.

    • Saws, presses, and paper machines cause amputation and crushing injuries.
    • Interlocks, guarding, and control software prevent motion during clearing.
    • The injury happens during a maintenance task, not during production.
    ASK
    How are machine safety functions validated before a machine ships?
  2. 02

    Mills run continuously and you support them remotely.

    • Controllers, optimisation software, and remote support connect into mill networks.
    • Mills operate around the clock and a stop halts the whole line.
    • You become the access path into their control network.
    ASK
    How do you connect to installed machinery for support and updates?
  3. 03

    Optimisation software determines recovery from every log.

    • Scanning and optimisation software drives cutting decisions directly.
    • Configurations are developed for each specific mill.
    • Running on defaults costs recovery on every log processed.
    ASK
    Do you hold the only copies of mill-specific optimisation configurations?
