Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 811412

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 811412, Appliance Repair and Maintenance, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Technicians are scheduled into customers' homes.

    WHY
    Appliance repair is performed in homes on scheduled appointments, with dispatch systems holding customer addresses, appointment windows, access arrangements, and payment methods.
    LOSS
    Exposure of customer addresses and appointment schedules indicates when homes will be occupied and by whom, which is a physical security concern distinct from payment data exposure.
    ASK
    Do your records include access instructions or notes about when customers are home?
  2. 02

    Parts identification depends on model and serial lookup.

    WHY
    Correct parts are identified from model and serial numbers against manufacturer catalogues, with many parts non-returnable once ordered. Wrong parts extend the customer's outage and are absorbed as cost.
    LOSS
    Wrong parts ordered against guessed models, with non-returnable stock written off and customers without functioning refrigeration or laundry for additional days.
    ASK
    How do your technicians identify the correct part for a specific appliance?
  3. 03

    Warranty and service contract work is authorised electronically.

    WHY
    Much of the work is authorised and paid through manufacturer warranty portals and third-party service contract administrators, with claim submission deadlines and documentation requirements.
    LOSS
    Unbillable work where claims cannot be submitted within the administrator's window, on jobs already completed with parts already fitted.
    ASK
    What proportion of your work is warranty or service contract rather than customer pay?
