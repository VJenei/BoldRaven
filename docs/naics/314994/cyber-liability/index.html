Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 314994

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 314994, Rope, Cordage, Twine, Tire Cord, and Tire Fabric Mills, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Rope and cordage failures kill people.

    WHY
    Rope and cordage are used in lifting, mooring, climbing, and safety applications with breaking strength specifications and testing. Failure under load causes severe injury and death, and products are certified to standards specifiers rely on.
    LOSS
    A rope failure in a lifting or life safety application, with testing and production records determining whether the product met its rating and the manufacturer's liability for the resulting injury or death.
    ASK
    Are your products used in lifting, mooring, or life safety applications?
  2. 02

    Tire cord is a structural component of a safety-critical product.

    WHY
    Tire cord and fabric provide the structural reinforcement of tires, manufactured to tension, adhesion, and dimensional specifications qualified by tire manufacturers. Defects in reinforcement cause tread separation and blowouts.
    LOSS
    Defective reinforcement incorporated into tires, with tire recalls affecting millions of units and traceability from cord lot to tire production determining the scope and the supplier's share of the liability.
    ASK
    How is your material traced into customer tire production lots?
  3. 03

    Continuous production runs at high speed with tight tension control.

    WHY
    Extrusion, twisting, and treating run continuously with tension, twist, and dimensional control determining product properties. Process deviations produce material that fails specification in ways not visible on inspection.
    LOSS
    Off-specification material shipped and incorporated into customer products before testing detects it, with recall reaching the finished products rather than stopping at your material.
    ASK
    How are tension and twist parameters monitored during production?
