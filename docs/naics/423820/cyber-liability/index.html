Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 423820

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 423820, Farm and Garden Machinery and Equipment Merchant Wholesalers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Dealers hold administrative access to farmers' precision agriculture accounts.

    WHY
    Equipment dealers set up and support customer accounts on manufacturer platforms such as John Deere Operations Center, transferring field boundaries, prescriptions, and yield data. Dealer staff hold access across many farm accounts. Researchers demonstrated in 2021 that flaws in Deere's platform allowed owner enumeration and administrative access.
    LOSS
    Exposure of many farm customers' agronomic and yield data through dealer access, which is third-party liability to every affected farm and destroys the trust the dealership relies on.
    ASK
    How many customer precision agriculture accounts can your dealership access?
  2. 02

    The selling and service season is short and immovable.

    WHY
    Planting and harvest windows are measured in days, and a dealer that cannot supply parts or dispatch service during them fails customers at the only moment that matters. Parts inventory across dealer locations is checked live during those weeks.
    LOSS
    A farmer losing planting or harvest days because parts could not be located, with the resulting yield loss claimed against the dealer and the account lost permanently.
    ASK
    How do you locate parts across dealer locations during planting and harvest?
  3. 03

    Equipment finance and floor plan run against unit records.

    WHY
    New and used inventory is floor planned, and customer purchases are financed through manufacturer credit with applications containing personal and farm financial information. Trade-in valuations and unit histories sit in the dealer management system.
    LOSS
    A breach exposing customer credit applications and farm financial statements, plus floor plan audit failures where unit records cannot be reconciled.
    ASK
    Does your dealership process customer finance applications containing personal financial data?
