Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 336414

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 336414, Guided Missile and Space Vehicle Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Everything you design is controlled defence technology.

    WHY
    Missile and space vehicle designs are defence articles with technical data controlled under ITAR at the highest sensitivity. Access by a foreign person, including remote access, is an export requiring authorisation.
    LOSS
    Exposure of controlled technical data to a foreign actor, which is a national security matter with criminal exposure, debarment, and programme consequences beyond any civil penalty.
    ASK
    How is access to controlled programme data restricted, logged, and reviewed?
  2. 02

    Programmes require cleared facilities and demonstrated compliance.

    WHY
    Defence programmes require facility clearances, compliance with NIST SP 800-171 and CMMC, and in classified work compliance with the National Industrial Security Program operating manual. Compliance is continuously assessed.
    LOSS
    Loss of facility clearance or contract eligibility after an incident or failed assessment, which ends participation in the programmes the business exists to perform.
    ASK
    What CMMC level and facility clearance do your programmes require?
  3. 03

    Mission failure has consequences measured in national capability.

    WHY
    Vehicles are built in small numbers with enormous per-unit value and mission timelines set by launch windows and national requirements. A defect discovered late affects capability, not just cost.
    LOSS
    A design, software, or manufacturing defect causing mission failure, with the vehicle lost, the payload lost, and programme and national capability consequences that dwarf the unit cost.
    ASK
    What is the value of a single vehicle and the payload it carries?
