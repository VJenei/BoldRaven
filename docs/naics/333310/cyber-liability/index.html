Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333310

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333310, Commercial and Service Industry Machinery Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Your machines are connected and take payments in public.

    WHY
    Vending machines, kiosks, car wash systems, and commercial equipment increasingly include payment terminals, network connections, and remote management. Payment card handling brings PCI DSS 4.0 requirements, mandatory since March 31 2025.
    LOSS
    Card data captured through machines in the field, with card brand assessments, forensic investigation, and remediation across an installed base spread over thousands of unattended locations.
    ASK
    Do your machines accept card payments, and how is that data handled?
  2. 02

    Installed machines are managed from your systems.

    WHY
    Commercial machinery is monitored and configured remotely for pricing, inventory, and diagnostics. That platform can change what machines do across the entire installed base at once.
    LOSS
    Compromise of the management platform allowing mass reconfiguration or disabling of machines, with revenue stopped for every operator running them and site visits required to restore each one.
    ASK
    How many machines can be reconfigured from a single management account?
  3. 03

    Commercial food equipment carries safety and sanitation requirements.

    WHY
    Commercial equipment handling food is certified to sanitation standards, and equipment with heating, refrigeration, or mechanical hazards carries safety listings that depend on the built configuration.
    LOSS
    Equipment built outside its listed or certified configuration causing a fire, injury, or contamination, with recall across commercial locations and listing agency involvement.
    ASK
    How are certified configurations controlled through your build process?
