Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 483114

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 483114, Coastal and Great Lakes Passenger Transportation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Ferries carry commuters and vehicles on schedules communities depend on.

    WHY
    Coastal and lake ferry services are frequently the only practical connection for island and peninsula communities, carrying commuters, vehicles, freight, and emergency services. Ticketing, reservations, and vehicle deck loading are managed electronically.
    LOSS
    Service suspension isolates communities, blocking commuters, deliveries, and medical access. Public and political consequences are immediate and disproportionate to the commercial loss.
    ASK
    Do any communities you serve have no alternative route?
  2. 02

    Coast Guard cybersecurity rules apply and passenger vessels are scrutinised.

    WHY
    US-flagged passenger vessels fall under the Coast Guard cybersecurity rule effective July 16, 2025, with immediate incident reporting to the National Response Center, training by January 12, 2026, and a Cybersecurity Officer, assessment, and approved plan by July 16, 2027.
    LOSS
    Captain of the Port restrictions removing vessels from service, with no substitute capacity available and public service obligations unmet.
    ASK
    Have you started your Cybersecurity Assessment under the Coast Guard rule?
  3. 03

    Passenger manifests are a life safety record.

    WHY
    Passenger and vehicle counts and manifests determine stability calculations and are the basis for search and rescue if the vessel is in distress. Ticketing systems produce those counts, and overloading is a direct capsizing risk.
    LOSS
    Loading without accurate passenger accounting risks exceeding stability limits, and in a distress situation responders do not know how many people to search for.
    ASK
    How is your passenger count verified before departure?
