Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 481111

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 481111, Scheduled Passenger Air Transportation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Airlines cannot dispatch aircraft without their operational systems.

    WHY
    Flight planning, weight and balance, crew scheduling, maintenance release, and dispatch release are all system-generated and legally required before a flight departs. An aircraft cannot lawfully dispatch without a signed dispatch release and current weight and balance. Airlines have grounded entire fleets over failures in these systems.
    LOSS
    A ground stop across a fleet cascades for days as aircraft and crews end up out of position. A major carrier loses tens of millions per day of disruption, plus rebooking, accommodation, and DOT consumer protection obligations.
    ASK
    Which systems must be working before you can legally dispatch a flight?
  2. 02

    TSA imposes mandatory cybersecurity requirements on aircraft operators.

    WHY
    TSA issued cybersecurity requirements for TSA-regulated aircraft operators requiring network segmentation between information and operational technology, access controls, continuous monitoring, and timely patching, together with a TSA-approved implementation plan and assessment programme. These are enforceable security directives, not guidance.
    LOSS
    TSA enforcement action and civil penalties, plus the operational consequence of being required to remediate under a mandated timeline while flying.
    ASK
    Do you hold a TSA-approved cybersecurity implementation plan?
  3. 03

    Passenger data includes identity documents and travel patterns.

    WHY
    Airlines hold passenger name records with names, dates of birth, passport and identity document numbers, payment cards, frequent flyer balances, and complete travel histories. Loyalty accounts hold transferable value that converts quickly.
    LOSS
    Breach notification across millions of passengers spanning multiple jurisdictions, plus loyalty account takeover draining miles that must be reinstated at the airline's cost.
    ASK
    How many passenger records with passport or identity document data do you hold?
