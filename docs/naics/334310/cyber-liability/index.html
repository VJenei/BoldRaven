Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 334310

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 334310, Audio and Video Equipment Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Your products have microphones and cameras in private homes.

    • Smart speakers, streaming devices, and displays sit in living spaces.
    • They include microphones, cameras, and network connections.
    • Compromise of those devices is surveillance of the customer's home.
    ASK
    Do your products include microphones or cameras with network connectivity?
  2. 02

    Viewing data is regulated personal information.

    • Devices collect what customers watch and listen to.
    • Video viewing data carries specific statutory protection and damages.
    • Sharing it with advertisers is a recognised enforcement target.
    ASK
    What viewing or listening data do your devices collect, and who receives it?
  3. 03

    Firmware updates are the only way to fix shipped products.

    • Devices stay in homes for years with security depending on updates.
    • A large proportion of devices never update.
    • Those units stay exploitable indefinitely.
    ASK
    What proportion of your deployed devices are running current firmware?
