Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 621112

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 621112, Offices of Physicians, Mental Health Specialists, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Psychiatric records are the most damaging health data to expose.

    WHY
    Psychiatric practice records contain diagnoses, medication histories, session content, and details of suicidality, trauma, and substance use. Patients disclose this to no one else, and exposure causes employment, custody, and relationship harm that cannot be remediated.
    LOSS
    Patient harm that credit monitoring does not address, with claims reflecting the personal consequences of disclosure, and a practice whose patients cannot be replaced because trust in confidentiality is the treatment relationship.
    ASK
    What would exposure of your session notes mean for your patients personally?
  2. 02

    Psychotherapy notes have distinct protection under HIPAA.

    WHY
    HIPAA gives psychotherapy notes maintained separately from the medical record heightened protection, generally requiring specific authorisation for disclosure. Where substance use disorder treatment is involved, 42 CFR Part 2 imposes restrictions stricter than HIPAA.
    LOSS
    Regulatory exposure under both HIPAA and, where applicable, Part 2, which carries its own penalties and consent requirements, alongside professional licensing consequences for confidentiality failures.
    ASK
    Are psychotherapy notes stored separately from the general medical record?
  3. 03

    Controlled substance prescribing carries DEA obligations.

    WHY
    Psychiatric practice involves controlled substances requiring DEA registration, electronic prescribing with identity proofing and two-factor authentication, and prescription monitoring programme checks in most states.
    LOSS
    Compromised prescriber credentials used to issue fraudulent controlled substance prescriptions, with DEA investigation of the registrant and patients unable to obtain necessary medication.
    ASK
    How is access to your electronic prescribing credential protected?
