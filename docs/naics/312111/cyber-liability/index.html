Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 312111

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 312111, Soft Drink Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Bottling lines run continuously and stop the whole plant when they fail.

    WHY
    Filling, capping, labelling, and packaging run as a continuous high-speed line under integrated control, with syrup batching and carbonation upstream. Lines run thousands of containers per minute and product in process is lost on any stop.
    LOSS
    Extended line downtime with retail delivery commitments unmet and product in process discarded, on capital equipment whose utilisation determines whether the plant is profitable.
    ASK
    How many containers per minute does your line fill, and what stops when it does?
  2. 02

    Syrup batching accuracy determines every unit produced.

    WHY
    Concentrate is diluted and batched to formulation with sweetener, water treatment, and carbonation controlled automatically. An error in batching is multiplied across every container filled from that batch.
    LOSS
    Off-specification product across an entire batch reaching retail, with recall and consumer complaints, and brand damage where taste deviation is noticed by consumers.
    ASK
    How many finished units are produced from a single syrup batch?
  3. 03

    Water treatment is a food safety and quality control.

    WHY
    Beverage water is treated to remove contaminants and standardise chemistry, monitored continuously. Water quality affects both product safety and taste consistency across production.
    LOSS
    Product produced with untreated or improperly treated water, which is a food safety issue requiring recall, and quality variation that customers detect immediately.
    ASK
    How is treated water quality monitored before it enters production?
