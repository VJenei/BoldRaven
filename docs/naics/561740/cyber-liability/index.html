Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561740

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561740, Carpet and Upholstery Cleaning Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You enter customer homes on a schedule.

    • Records carry addresses, access arrangements, and appointment times.
    • That data shows when homes are occupied and when they are not.
    • Every affected customer must be notified.
    ASK
    What customer address and access data do you store?
  2. 02

    Scheduling and dispatch run every job.

    • Appointments, crews, and routing run on connected systems.
    • Customers take time off to be present.
    • A lost schedule cancels appointments across many customers.
    ASK
    How are your appointments and crew routing scheduled?
  3. 03

    Card payments are taken in the customer's home.

    • Technicians take card payments on mobile terminals.
    • Card security requirements apply to that handling.
    • A breach affects customers who paid at their own front door.
    ASK
    How do your technicians take and process card payments?
