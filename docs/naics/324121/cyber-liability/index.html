Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 324121

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 324121, Asphalt Paving Mixture and Block Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Mix designs are approved by the DOT and control every load.

    WHY
    Asphalt mixtures are produced to job mix formulas approved by the state DOT, with aggregate gradation, binder content, and temperature controlled at the plant. Every load is produced to a specific approved design for a specific project.
    LOSS
    Mix produced outside the approved formula and placed on a highway project, which is rejected on testing and must be removed and replaced at the producer's cost, including the paving labour.
    ASK
    How is the job mix formula loaded into and verified at your plant?
  2. 02

    Paving happens in a season and to a temperature window.

    WHY
    Asphalt must be produced, transported, and placed within a temperature range, with the paving season limited by weather. Plants supply crews that are already mobilised on a job site with equipment running.
    LOSS
    Paving crews idle on site with no mix available, at costs of thousands per hour, and a paving season with fixed weather limits that cannot be extended to recover lost days.
    ASK
    What does it cost a paving crew to wait for mix?
  3. 03

    Ticketing and quality records support payment and acceptance.

    WHY
    Every load is ticketed with mix type, tonnage, temperature, and time, used for payment and for project acceptance documentation. DOT projects require that documentation for the material to be accepted.
    LOSS
    Unbillable material and, more seriously, placed material that cannot be documented and therefore cannot be accepted by the owner, with removal and replacement as the remedy.
    ASK
    How are load tickets and mix temperature records captured and retained?
