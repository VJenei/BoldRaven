Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 334512

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 334512, Automatic Environmental Control Manufacturing for Residential, Commercial, and Appliance Use, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Thermostats are connected devices that know when you are out.

    • Controls connect to home and building networks and to your cloud platform.
    • Occupancy patterns reveal exactly when a building is empty.
    • That data plus remote control is a burglary tool.
    ASK
    What data do your connected controls send to platforms you operate?
  2. 02

    Controls prevent equipment from operating unsafely.

    • Limit functions stop overheating and unsafe operation in the host equipment.
    • Safety listings depend on those functions performing.
    • A defeated limit means the equipment overheats and ignites.
    ASK
    Do your controls perform safety limit functions in the equipment they are fitted to?
  3. 03

    Your controls are embedded in other manufacturers' products.

    • Appliance and equipment makers build your controls into their products.
    • A control defect becomes a recall of every finished product containing it.
    • The recall cost is their appliance, not your control.
    ASK
    How many finished product models contain your controls?
