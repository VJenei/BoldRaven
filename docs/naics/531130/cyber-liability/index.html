Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 531130

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 531130, Lessors of Miniwarehouses and Self-Storage Units, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Gate and unit access is entirely electronic and unstaffed.

    WHY
    Self-storage operates on gate access codes, individual unit alarms, and increasingly app-based entry, with many facilities unstaffed for most or all of the day. The access system is the only thing separating the public from customers' property.
    LOSS
    Unauthorised access to units at an unstaffed site, with theft from multiple units before discovery and the operator unable to demonstrate who entered and when.
    ASK
    Is your facility unstaffed at any point, and what controls access during those hours?
  2. 02

    You do not know what is in the units you are protecting.

    WHY
    Customers store household goods, business records, inventory, and sometimes items of very high value without declaring contents. Claims after a loss assert whatever the customer says was inside, and the rental agreement and access logs are the only evidence available.
    LOSS
    Claims for high value contents you had no knowledge of, defended only with access logs and the terms of the rental agreement, which is why those records matter more than the property itself.
    ASK
    How do you evidence who accessed a specific unit and when?
  3. 03

    Lien sales require statutory notice on strict timelines.

    WHY
    Selling the contents of a delinquent unit requires notice to the tenant and lienholders following state-specific procedures, with documented delivery and waiting periods. Getting the process wrong exposes the operator to conversion claims for the value of everything sold.
    LOSS
    Conversion liability for units sold without proper notice, where the customer's claimed contents value determines damages and the operator cannot prove the notice was properly given.
    ASK
    How do you document lien notices and their delivery to delinquent tenants?
