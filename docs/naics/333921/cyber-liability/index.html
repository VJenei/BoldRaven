Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 333921

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333921, Elevator and Moving Stairway Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Elevators carry people and are code-regulated for that reason.

    WHY
    Elevators and escalators carry passengers with safety functions including door interlocks, overspeed governors, and brakes governed by ASME A17.1 and inspected by jurisdictions. Failures cause falls, entrapments, and crush injuries.
    LOSS
    A control or safety function defect causing a car movement with doors open or an uncontrolled descent, with fatal injury and an investigation examining the manufacturer's design and installation records.
    ASK
    How are elevator safety functions validated before a unit is placed in service?
  2. 02

    Units are monitored and serviced remotely by you.

    WHY
    Modern elevators connect to manufacturer monitoring platforms for fault reporting, diagnostics, and in some cases parameter changes, across large installed bases in occupied buildings.
    LOSS
    Compromise of the monitoring platform reaching elevator controllers in occupied buildings, with entrapments and safety function interference, requiring shutdown of units until each is verified.
    ASK
    What can be changed on an installed elevator through your remote platform?
  3. 03

    Service and maintenance contracts run the installed base.

    WHY
    Elevator manufacturers hold service contracts with dispatch, technician scheduling, parts, and compliance records for inspections and testing across thousands of units.
    LOSS
    Service dispatch and records systems unavailable, with entrapment calls unrouted, required periodic tests missed, and jurisdictions taking units out of service for lapsed inspection.
    ASK
    How are entrapment calls received and dispatched to technicians?
