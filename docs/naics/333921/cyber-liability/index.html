CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 333921, Elevator and Moving Stairway Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Elevators carry people and are code regulated for it.

    • Door interlocks, overspeed governors, and brakes are inspected safety functions.
    • Failures cause falls, entrapments, and crush injuries.
    • A car moving with doors open is fatal.
    ASK
    How are elevator safety functions validated before a unit is placed in service?
  2. 02

    You monitor and reach installed units remotely.

    • Elevators connect to your platforms for fault reporting and diagnostics.
    • Some platforms can change parameters on installed units.
    • Those units are in occupied buildings full of people.
    ASK
    What can be changed on an installed elevator through your remote platform?
  3. 03

    Service contracts run the entire installed base.

    • Dispatch, technician scheduling, parts, and inspection compliance run on your systems.
    • Entrapment calls arrive through that channel.
    • Lapsed inspections take units out of service by order.
    ASK
    How are entrapment calls received and dispatched to technicians?
