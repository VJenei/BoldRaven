Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 322299

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 322299, All Other Converted Paper Product Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Converted products are made to customer specification.

    WHY
    Converters produce to customer dimensions, materials, and printing for specific applications, with job specifications driving setup and run parameters. Products are components in customers' operations.
    LOSS
    Wrong specification production unsaleable elsewhere, with customers whose own production or packaging depends on the component halted.
    ASK
    What proportion of your output is made to individual customer specification?
  2. 02

    Food and medical contact applications carry compliance duties.

    WHY
    Converted papers used in food packaging and medical applications must meet material and cleanliness requirements with documentation supporting customer compliance obligations.
    LOSS
    Non-conforming material entering food or medical packaging, with recall at the customer level and the converter identified as the source of the compliance failure.
    ASK
    Do your products contact food or medical materials?
  3. 03

    Converting equipment is programme driven with tight tolerances.

    WHY
    Slitting, die cutting, laminating, and finishing run from job data controlling dimensions and registration. Tolerances are tight where products feed into customer automated equipment.
    LOSS
    Out of tolerance product jamming customer automated equipment, with their line stoppages claimed against the converter alongside the material cost.
    ASK
    Do your products feed customer automated equipment requiring tight dimensional tolerance?
