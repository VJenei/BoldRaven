Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 622210

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 622210, Psychiatric and Substance Abuse Hospitals, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    These records are the most damaging health data that exists.

    WHY
    Psychiatric and substance use treatment records document diagnoses, involuntary commitment, suicidality, trauma, and substance use for identified patients. Federally assisted substance use disorder programmes are additionally protected under 42 CFR Part 2 with stricter consent requirements than HIPAA.
    LOSS
    Patient harm affecting employment, custody, professional licensure, and personal safety, with Part 2 violations alongside HIPAA exposure and claims reflecting consequences that no remediation addresses.
    ASK
    What would exposure of your patient census mean for those individuals' lives?
  2. 02

    Inpatient psychiatric care cannot pause.

    WHY
    Patients are admitted involuntarily and voluntarily with acute risk requiring continuous observation, medication administration, and documentation of legal status, commitment expiry, and privileges. Legal hold status determines whether a patient may lawfully be detained.
    LOSS
    Inability to verify legal status and medication histories for acutely unwell patients, with unlawful detention or premature release exposure and medication errors in a population where errors are dangerous.
    ASK
    How would staff verify a patient's legal hold status if systems were unavailable?
  3. 03

    Patients are frequently unable to protect their own interests.

    WHY
    The patient population includes people who are acutely unwell, cognitively impaired, or under legal restriction, who cannot monitor their own credit, dispute misuse, or take protective action after a breach.
    LOSS
    A breach affecting a population that cannot self-protect, which regulators, courts, and patient advocates treat as an aggravating factor rather than a neutral one.
    ASK
    How many of your patients could take protective action if their data were exposed?
