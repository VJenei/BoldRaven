Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 115114

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 115114, Postharvest Crop Activities (except Cotton Ginning), needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold other people's crops in your facility.

    • Grain, produce, and other crops are stored for growers and buyers.
    • Inventory records establish whose crop is in which bin or room.
    • A lost inventory record means you cannot prove what you hold.
    ASK
    How is stored crop ownership and inventory recorded in your facility?
  2. 02

    Storage conditions are held by automated controls.

    • Temperature, humidity, aeration, and atmosphere are controlled continuously.
    • Stored crops deteriorate within days when controls fail.
    • A failed room is a total loss on someone else's crop.
    ASK
    Are your storage rooms or bins run by automated environmental controls?
  3. 03

    Food safety and traceability records follow every lot out.

    • Lot codes link incoming crop to storage, treatment, and outgoing shipment.
    • Buyers and regulators trace through those codes during an investigation.
    • Without traceability every lot you handled is implicated.
    ASK
    Could you trace an outgoing lot back to its incoming grower and date?
