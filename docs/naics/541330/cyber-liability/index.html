Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 541330

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 541330, Engineering Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Engineering calculations are the evidence that a design was sound.

    WHY
    Structural, mechanical, and civil engineering deliverables are supported by calculations, analysis models, and design assumptions that constitute the professional record. Statutes of repose extend liability many years past completion, and claims are defended from that record.
    LOSS
    A structural or system failure claim that cannot be defended without the calculations, where the engineer's inability to produce the record effectively concedes the allegation on a loss involving injury or collapse.
    ASK
    Could you produce the design calculations for a project completed eight years ago?
  2. 02

    You hold client facility and infrastructure information.

    WHY
    Engineers hold detailed information about industrial facilities, utilities, transportation infrastructure, and buildings, including process design, control systems, and structural details. Much of this is confidential and some of it is security sensitive.
    LOSS
    Third-party liability to owners for disclosed facility information, and for critical infrastructure clients a security exposure that draws regulatory attention to both parties.
    ASK
    Do you hold design information for critical infrastructure or industrial process facilities?
  3. 03

    Federal and defence work imposes cybersecurity requirements by contract.

    WHY
    Engineering firms working on federal projects must meet FAR safeguarding requirements, and those handling controlled unclassified information face DFARS clauses and CMMC obligations that flow down through subcontracts.
    LOSS
    Loss of eligibility for federal work, contract termination, and False Claims Act exposure where compliance was represented in a proposal but not achieved.
    ASK
    Do your contracts flow down DFARS or CMMC requirements?
