Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111991

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111991, Sugar Beet Farming, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Growers are cooperative shareholders and the cooperative controls delivery and payment.

    WHY
    Sugar beet production runs through grower-owned cooperatives that allocate delivery by shares, schedule piling stations, operate tare labs, and pay in installments over the following year. The FBI notice of April 2022 identified agricultural cooperatives as targets during critical seasons and documented six grain cooperatives encrypted within three weeks.
    LOSS
    Harvest delivery blocked during the fall window, plus disruption to a payment structure spread over 12 months or more. As a shareholder, the grower absorbs the cooperative's loss as well.
    ASK
    Are you a shareholder in the cooperative that receives and processes your crop?
  2. 02

    Beets are stored in outdoor piles and paid on tare lab results.

    WHY
    Harvested beets are piled at receiving stations, ventilated to control pile temperature, and paid on sugar percentage and tare determined by laboratory sampling. Piles must be processed on a managed schedule or sugar content declines and rot spreads.
    LOSS
    Delayed processing reduces recoverable sugar across the harvest. Inability to verify or dispute tare and sugar results affects payment on the full tonnage.
    ASK
    How long is your crop held in piles before processing?
  3. 03

    Sugar beets carry high per-acre input costs paid by wire.

    WHY
    Sugar beet production requires high spending per acre on seed, fungicide programs, irrigation, and specialized harvest equipment. Inputs are funded on operating credit and prepaid to suppliers by wire.
    LOSS
    A diverted input prepay wire of $150,000 to $500,000 against an operating line sized to the crop plan.
    ASK
    What is your input cost per acre, and how are supplier prepayments sent?
