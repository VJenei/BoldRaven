Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311411

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311411, Frozen Fruit, Juice, and Vegetable Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    The cold chain is the entire preservation system.

    • Freezing, storage, and shipping temperatures are monitored and recorded continuously.
    • Product that thaws and refreezes is unsafe and unsellable.
    • A refrigeration failure can lose an entire warehouse.
    ASK
    Is your freezer and storage temperature monitored and recorded automatically?
  2. 02

    Frozen produce recalls have caused listeria outbreaks.

    • Listeria survives freezing and grows in the plant environment.
    • Environmental monitoring and sanitation records are inspected.
    • An outbreak without traceability recalls everything you shipped.
    ASK
    What environmental monitoring records do you keep for listeria?
  3. 03

    Traceability determines the size of any recall.

    • Lot codes link incoming raw product to finished packages and customers.
    • Investigators trace back through those codes within days.
    • Without traceability the recall covers your whole production history.
    ASK
    Could you trace a packed lot back to a specific grower and date?
