Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 311411

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 311411, Frozen Fruit, Juice, and Vegetable Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Frozen produce recalls have involved Listeria with fatalities.

    WHY
    Frozen fruit and vegetable products have been the subject of multistate Listeria outbreaks with deaths, leading to very large recalls across many brands because the same processor supplied multiple private labels. Environmental monitoring and lot traceability determine the scope.
    LOSS
    A Listeria recall covering every brand and product produced in a period, with wrongful death claims, FDA enforcement, and in serious cases criminal exposure for executives.
    ASK
    How many customer brands are produced on the lines in your facility?
  2. 02

    Freezing and cold storage must hold continuously.

    WHY
    Individual quick freezing and frozen storage maintain product safety and quality, with temperature monitored continuously. Product that thaws and refreezes is a food safety and quality failure.
    LOSS
    A freezing or cold storage control failure condemning inventory measured in millions of pounds, with disposal cost on top of the product loss.
    ASK
    What is the value of frozen inventory in your storage right now?
  3. 03

    Several of your inputs are on the Food Traceability List.

    WHY
    Fresh produce entering the plant includes items on the FDA Food Traceability List, and manufacturers who transform them carry Key Data Element obligations under FSMA Section 204, with a compliance date of July 20, 2028 and 24-hour record production to FDA.
    LOSS
    A traceback that cannot link finished product to incoming produce lots, expanding an investigation across all production and preventing any claim against the grower whose product caused it.
    ASK
    Can you link a finished frozen lot back to the specific grower lots that went into it?
