Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561431

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561431, Private Mail Centers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    You hold mail and identity records for box holders.

    • Box holder records carry identity documents and addresses.
    • Federal requirements govern that identification.
    • Every affected customer must be notified.
    ASK
    What box holder identity records do you hold?
  2. 02

    Mail contains financial and personal documents.

    • Customer mail includes financial statements and identity documents.
    • Mail is held and forwarded on customer instruction.
    • Misdirected or intercepted mail is identity theft material.
    ASK
    How are mail forwarding instructions verified before they take effect?
  3. 03

    Shipping and payment run through your systems.

    • Shipping, postage, and payment processing run on connected systems.
    • Customers pay by card at the counter.
    • Card security requirements apply to every location.
    ASK
    How does your point of sale system handle card data?
