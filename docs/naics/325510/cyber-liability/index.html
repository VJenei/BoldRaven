Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 325510

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 325510, Paint and Coating Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Formulations are the business and colour matching is the service.

    WHY
    Paint manufacturers hold formulations and tint formulas developed over decades, with colour matching to customer standards being the technical product. Industrial and OEM coatings are formulated to customer specification and qualified for their application.
    LOSS
    Formulation loss means colours and performance that cannot be reproduced, with OEM customers whose products depend on a qualified coating unable to continue production.
    ASK
    How many formulations and tint formulas are held in your systems?
  2. 02

    Solvent handling creates fire and explosion exposure.

    WHY
    Solvent-based coating manufacture involves flammable liquids in mixing, thinning, and filling operations, with vapour control, grounding, and classified electrical areas managing ignition risk. Mixing vessels and solvent storage are the principal hazards.
    LOSS
    A solvent fire or explosion destroying production and storage, with worker fatality exposure and a facility that takes many months to rebuild while customers requalify elsewhere.
    ASK
    How are flammable vapour levels monitored in your mixing and filling areas?
  3. 03

    VOC and chemical regulation varies by jurisdiction.

    WHY
    Coatings are regulated for volatile organic compound content with limits varying by state and air district, and chemical content is subject to TSCA reporting and increasingly to state restrictions on specific substances.
    LOSS
    Product shipped into jurisdictions where its VOC content is not permitted, with enforcement, product recall from distribution, and reformulation required before sales can resume.
    ASK
    How do you control which formulations may ship into which jurisdictions?
