Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 325611

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 325611, Soap and Other Detergent Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Concentrated products cause injury when packaging or labelling fails.

    WHY
    Detergents and cleaning products are corrosive or irritant concentrates, with child-resistant packaging required for some products and warning labels required under federal hazardous substances rules. Liquid laundry packets in particular have caused serious child injuries.
    LOSS
    Packaging or labelling failures leading to child ingestion or chemical injury, with CPSC action, recall, and litigation in a product category that has already attracted regulatory attention for child safety.
    ASK
    Do your products require child-resistant packaging or hazard warnings?
  2. 02

    Batching accuracy determines both performance and safety.

    WHY
    Detergent manufacture batches surfactants, builders, enzymes, and fragrances to formulation, with concentration determining cleaning performance and safety. Over-concentration produces a more hazardous product than the label describes.
    LOSS
    Product batched outside formulation causing skin and eye injuries beyond what the label warns of, with recall and injury claims where the product was more hazardous than represented.
    ASK
    How is batch concentration verified before filling and labelling?
  3. 03

    High speed filling lines run continuously to retail demand.

    WHY
    Filling, capping, and packaging run at high rate supplying retail on continuous replenishment. Household products have consistent demand and retailers expect continuous supply.
    LOSS
    Retail out of stocks across a distribution network with shelf space and promotional commitments unmet, plus product in process lost on line stops.
    ASK
    How many units per hour do your filling lines produce?
