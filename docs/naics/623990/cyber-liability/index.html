Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 623990

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 623990, Other Residential Care Facilities, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Residents are dependent and frequently unable to protect themselves.

    WHY
    Facilities in this category house residents requiring supervision and support, holding personal, medical, financial, and in many cases guardianship or court-related documentation. Residents commonly cannot detect or report misuse of their information.
    LOSS
    Exploitation of a dependent resident population using data from your systems, with regulatory and advocacy attention treating resident vulnerability as central to the assessment.
    ASK
    How many residents could recognise and report misuse of their own information?
  2. 02

    Care plans direct daily support and safety.

    WHY
    Residents receive care according to individual plans covering medication, dietary needs, behavioural supports, and medical protocols. Staff rely on those plans to deliver care safely, particularly with turnover and agency staffing.
    LOSS
    Care delivered without plans, causing medication errors, dietary incidents, and behavioural crises, with licensing findings and liability for resulting harm.
    ASK
    How would agency staff know a resident's care requirements without the plan?
  3. 03

    Licensing and funding depend on maintained documentation.

    WHY
    Facilities operate under state licensing and frequently under Medicaid or other public funding, with assessment, service delivery, incident, and staffing documentation required and inspected.
    LOSS
    Licensing action and funding recoupment where required documentation cannot be produced, with public funding being the source of most revenue.
    ASK
    What documentation does your licensing authority require you to maintain?
