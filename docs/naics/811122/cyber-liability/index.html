Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 811122

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 811122, Automotive Glass Replacement Shops, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Windscreen replacement now requires ADAS recalibration.

    WHY
    Cameras mounted to the windscreen for lane keeping, automatic braking, and adaptive cruise require recalibration after replacement, following manufacturer procedures with documented completion. An uncalibrated system misjudges lane position and obstacle distance.
    LOSS
    A collision involving a vehicle whose camera calibration was not performed or cannot be documented, where the glass shop returned a vehicle with safety systems out of specification.
    ASK
    How do you document recalibration completion after a windscreen replacement?
  2. 02

    Insurance networks route the work and hold the billing.

    WHY
    Glass claims are administered through third-party administrators and insurer networks that dispatch jobs, authorise pricing, and process payment. Shops are dispatched work through those platforms and paid through them.
    LOSS
    Loss of network dispatch and billing access removes both the work and the payment mechanism simultaneously, and network scorecards affect future dispatch volume.
    ASK
    What share of your work is dispatched through insurance networks?
  3. 03

    Mobile service means scheduling customers at their homes and workplaces.

    WHY
    Much of the work is performed at customer locations scheduled through dispatch systems holding addresses, vehicle details, appointment times, and payment methods.
    LOSS
    Missed mobile appointments across a route, plus exposure of customer addresses and schedules, with vehicles left with damaged glass and customers waiting.
    ASK
    How many mobile appointments does your team run in a day?
