Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 112111

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 112111, Beef Cattle Ranching and Farming, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Cattle sales are settled by wire days after delivery.

    WHY
    Calves and feeders are sold through video auctions, order buyers, and private treaty. Settlement is by wire after delivery. Delivery instructions, weights, and payment details are exchanged by email between parties who transact once or twice a year.
    LOSS
    One load of calves is $80,000 to $150,000 and a large sale day can exceed $1M. Once the transfer is authorized, it is a deception loss rather than a covered theft, and banks rarely recover funds after 24 hours.
    ASK
    How many times a year do you receive payment by wire, and what is the largest amount?
  2. 02

    USDA requires electronically readable identification for interstate cattle movement.

    WHY
    Effective November 5, 2024, APHIS requires official eartags on sexually intact cattle and bison 18 months and older, all dairy cattle, and rodeo and exhibition animals to be both visually and electronically readable for interstate movement. EID numbers are tied to premises IDs, health certificates, and movement records.
    LOSS
    Inability to produce EID and movement records blocks interstate sale and shipment and prevents the operation from demonstrating compliance in a disease trace.
    ASK
    Do you move cattle across state lines, and how do you store your EID and movement records?
  3. 03

    Remote water and monitoring systems cover ground that is not checked daily.

    WHY
    Extensive operations use remote water level and pump monitoring on tanks and wells, cellular and satellite telemetry, GPS collars, and remote gates across large acreage. In dry conditions cattle die within one to two days of losing water. Pastures may not be visited for several days.
    LOSS
    Death loss from water failure in summer heat, discovered days late. Cattle values are commonly $2,000 or more per head. Farm policies do not cover losses caused by a control system failure.
    ASK
    Do you use remote monitoring for stock water or pasture equipment?
