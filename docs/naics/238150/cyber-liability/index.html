Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 238150

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 238150, Glass and Glazing Contractors, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Curtain wall is engineered and fabricated to field dimensions that cannot be guessed.

    WHY
    Curtain wall and storefront are detailed from field measurements into shop drawings and fabrication files, with glass cut to size and units assembled to order. Insulated glass units are made specifically for the opening. Nothing is stock.
    LOSS
    Wrongly fabricated units are a total loss and carry lead times of six to twelve weeks, holding the building open to weather and delaying every interior trade behind the enclosure.
    ASK
    How are field dimensions captured and transferred into your fabrication files?
  2. 02

    Performance testing and warranty documentation follow the building for decades.

    WHY
    Glazing systems carry air, water, and structural performance requirements verified by mock-up and field testing, with results documented. Manufacturer warranties on insulated units and sealants run ten to twenty years and depend on documented installation.
    LOSS
    A water intrusion claim years later where the test records and installation documentation cannot be produced, leaving the glazier to absorb remediation of a facade.
    ASK
    How long do you retain performance test and installation records after project closeout?
  3. 03

    Glass handling is a high-consequence operation planned in advance.

    WHY
    Large units are set with cranes, vacuum lifters, and specialised rigging under engineered lift plans. Units are heavy, awkward, and expensive, and dropped glass is both a total material loss and a severe injury exposure below.
    LOSS
    A dropped unit during setting, with material loss, potential injury to workers or the public below, and investigation against the lift plan.
    ASK
    Do you use engineered lift plans for setting large glass units?
