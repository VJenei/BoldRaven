Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 111331

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 111331, Apple Orchards, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Controlled atmosphere storage holds the crop for most of the year.

    • Apples are stored for months under controlled oxygen, carbon dioxide, and temperature.
    • Those rooms are held by automated gas and refrigeration controls.
    • A control failure ruins an entire room of fruit.
    ASK
    Are your storage rooms run by automated atmosphere and temperature controls?
  2. 02

    Packing lines sort and grade every apple electronically.

    • Optical graders sort by size, colour, and defect at high speed.
    • Pack-out records tie every bin to a grower lot and a customer.
    • A line down at harvest backs fruit up until it is unpackable.
    ASK
    Is your packing line run by automated grading or sorting equipment?
  3. 03

    Retail buyers require traceability and food safety records.

    • Lot codes link packed fruit back to block and harvest date.
    • Third party audit certificates are a condition of supply.
    • Without traceability a single problem recalls everything you shipped.
    ASK
    Could you trace a packed lot back to a specific block and harvest date?
