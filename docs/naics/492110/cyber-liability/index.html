Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 492110

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 492110, Couriers and Express Delivery Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Express networks are time-definite and fail as a whole.

    WHY
    Express delivery runs hub-and-spoke networks with overnight sorts, aircraft connections, and delivery commitments backed by money-back guarantees. Sortation, routing, and tracking are entirely automated. A missed sort displaces packages across the whole network.
    LOSS
    Service guarantee refunds on every affected shipment plus displaced freight taking days to recover. Time-critical medical and industrial shipments generate claims far beyond the transportation charge.
    ASK
    What proportion of your shipments carry a time-definite service guarantee?
  2. 02

    Tracking and address data maps deliveries to individuals.

    WHY
    Courier systems hold sender and recipient names, addresses, contact details, signatures, and delivery photographs across enormous volumes. Delivery data reveals when people are home and what they receive.
    LOSS
    Breach notification at extreme scale given the number of parties involved, plus exposure of delivery patterns and signature images usable for porch theft and fraud.
    ASK
    How many sender and recipient records are held in your tracking system?
  3. 03

    Customs and dangerous goods data must move ahead of the shipment.

    WHY
    International express requires advance electronic customs data, restricted party screening, and dangerous goods acceptance checks before shipments are loaded. Regulatory data must be transmitted before departure.
    LOSS
    Shipments unable to clear or load, plus penalties for inaccurate customs data and a dangerous goods acceptance failure that becomes an in-flight safety event.
    ASK
    How is advance customs and screening data generated and transmitted?
