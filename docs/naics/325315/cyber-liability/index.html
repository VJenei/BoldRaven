Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 325315

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 325315, Compost Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Pathogen reduction depends on recorded temperature.

    • Compost must reach and hold specific temperatures to destroy pathogens.
    • Temperature monitoring and turning records document that process.
    • Under-processed compost carries pathogens into food crops.
    ASK
    How are your compost temperature and turning records recorded?
  2. 02

    Feedstock contamination follows into the finished product.

    • Incoming material can carry persistent herbicides, plastics, and contaminants.
    • Feedstock records and testing establish what went in.
    • Contaminated compost damages every crop it is applied to.
    ASK
    What feedstock records and testing do you maintain?
  3. 03

    Permits require monitoring and reporting.

    • Facility permits require odour, runoff, and operational monitoring with records.
    • Regulators and neighbours both act on those records.
    • Violations carry penalties and can suspend operations.
    ASK
    What monitoring and reporting does your facility permit require?
