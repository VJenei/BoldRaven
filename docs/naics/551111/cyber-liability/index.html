Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 551111

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 551111, Offices of Bank Holding Companies, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Holding company systems reach every subsidiary bank.

    WHY
    Bank holding companies frequently centralise IT, treasury, risk, and reporting for their subsidiary institutions. A compromise at the holding company level propagates to every bank in the group at once, and the group is supervised as a consolidated organisation.
    LOSS
    Simultaneous incidents across multiple insured institutions, each with its own 36-hour regulatory notification obligation and each subject to examination of its information security programme.
    ASK
    Which systems are shared across your subsidiary institutions?
  2. 02

    Consolidated financial and regulatory reporting must be produced on schedule.

    WHY
    Holding companies file consolidated reports with the Federal Reserve on fixed schedules, and larger organisations face capital planning and stress testing requirements. Those filings depend on consolidating data from every subsidiary.
    LOSS
    Missed or restated regulatory filings, which draw supervisory attention independent of the incident and can affect capital distribution approvals.
    ASK
    What regulatory filings would be delayed if your consolidation systems were unavailable?
  3. 03

    Strategic and supervisory information is highly sensitive.

    WHY
    Holding companies hold merger and acquisition analysis, capital plans, and confidential supervisory information from examiners. Confidential supervisory information is protected and its unauthorised disclosure is a serious regulatory matter.
    LOSS
    Disclosure of confidential supervisory information or unannounced transaction plans, which brings regulatory action separate from the breach and, for a public company, securities exposure.
    ASK
    Where is confidential supervisory information stored within your organisation?
