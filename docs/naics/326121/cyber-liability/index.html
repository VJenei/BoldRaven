Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 326121

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 326121, Unlaminated Plastics Profile Shape Manufacturing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Profiles are extruded through tooling to customer dimensions.

    WHY
    Profile extrusion runs product through customer-specific dies with dimensional tolerances required for assembly into windows, doors, and building components. Tooling and process settings are specific to each profile.
    LOSS
    Out of tolerance profile that will not assemble into customer products, with material and line time lost and the customer's own production halted for want of components.
    ASK
    How many customer-specific profile dies and process recipes do you hold?
  2. 02

    Building product profiles carry code and performance requirements.

    WHY
    Window, door, and siding profiles carry structural, weathering, and in some cases fire performance requirements certified by third parties. Building officials and specifiers rely on those certifications.
    LOSS
    Product failing performance certification installed in buildings, with removal and replacement required and, in a weather or fire event, liability where performance was relied upon.
    ASK
    Which of your profiles carry third-party performance certification?
  3. 03

    Continuous extrusion supplies customers on schedule.

    WHY
    Profile extrusion runs continuously supplying fabricators who assemble windows, doors, and components to their own customer schedules with limited component inventory.
    LOSS
    Fabricators without profile stopping their assembly, with construction projects behind them delayed and claims flowing back through the chain.
    ASK
    How many days of profile inventory do your fabricator customers hold?
