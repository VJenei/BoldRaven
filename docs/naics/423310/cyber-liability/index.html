Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 423310

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 423310, Lumber, Plywood, Millwork, and Wood Panel Merchant Wholesalers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Commodity positions and futures exposure are managed against live data.

    WHY
    Lumber prices move sharply and wholesalers carry inventory and forward commitments, sometimes hedged with futures. Position, cost basis, and open order data determine exposure. A distributor who cannot see its position cannot manage it.
    LOSS
    Unmanaged inventory exposure through a price move, where a 20 percent swing on a large inventory position wipes out a year of margin.
    ASK
    How do you track your inventory position and forward commitments against market price?
  2. 02

    Truckloads of lumber are a high value cargo theft target.

    WHY
    A truckload of finished lumber or panel product is valuable, easily resold, and untraceable once off the truck. Fictitious pickup, where a criminal poses as a booked carrier using spoofed credentials, is a documented and growing pattern against building products.
    LOSS
    A stolen load of $40,000 to $120,000, released voluntarily to someone presenting valid-looking documents, which is generally not covered as a property theft.
    ASK
    How do you verify the driver and carrier at the gate before releasing a load?
  3. 03

    Contractor and dealer accounts run on credit with lien rights attached.

    WHY
    Material sold to contractors and yards is supplied on credit, with preliminary notices and mechanics lien rights against the project protecting the balance. Lien deadlines run from the date of last delivery and require delivery records to establish.
    LOSS
    Loss of lien rights on unpaid balances because delivery dates and notice records were unavailable, converting secured exposure into an unsecured receivable.
    ASK
    How do you track delivery dates for lien notice deadlines on contractor accounts?
