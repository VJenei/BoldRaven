Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 522130

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 522130, Credit Unions, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    NCUA requires notification of a reportable cyber incident within 72 hours.

    WHY
    Federally insured credit unions must notify the NCUA as soon as possible and no later than 72 hours after reasonably believing a reportable cyber incident has occurred, including incidents at third-party service providers. The rule covers substantial loss of confidentiality, integrity, or availability and disruptions to business operations.
    LOSS
    Missed or late notification is itself an examination finding, and the incident triggers review of the whole information security programme, vendor management, and business continuity plan.
    ASK
    Who is responsible for making a 72-hour NCUA notification, including for vendor incidents?
  2. 02

    Core processing is outsourced and its failure is your failure.

    WHY
    Most credit unions run core banking, digital banking, and card processing through third-party service providers. Members experience an outage at the provider as an outage at the credit union, and the regulatory obligation to notify and to manage the vendor sits with the credit union.
    LOSS
    Members unable to access accounts, cards declining, and no ability to fix it directly. Contingent business interruption from a provider outage is a cyber coverage, and vendor contracts rarely make the credit union whole.
    ASK
    Which third-party providers would take your member services down if they failed?
  3. 03

    Member data includes complete financial and identity profiles.

    WHY
    Credit unions hold Social Security numbers, account and loan data, income documentation, and often employment and family information given the common bond membership model. Members frequently belong to a single employer or community, so a breach affects a defined and connected population.
    LOSS
    Notification across a membership that knows each other, in an institution whose entire premise is member trust and community relationship rather than price competition.
    ASK
    How many member records with Social Security numbers do you hold?
