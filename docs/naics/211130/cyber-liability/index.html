Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 211130

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 211130, Natural Gas Extraction, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Gas production is continuous and depends on compression and control that cannot pause.

    WHY
    Gas wells, compressor stations, dehydrators, and gathering systems run on SCADA with remote start, stop, and setpoint control. Compression is the throughput constraint. Nominations to the pipeline are made daily on a schedule, and a missed nomination means gas that cannot be delivered.
    LOSS
    Lost compression across a gathering system shuts in every well behind it. Imbalance penalties from the pipeline apply on top of deferred production, and neither is covered by a property policy because nothing was physically damaged.
    ASK
    Do you operate compressor stations or gathering systems under remote SCADA control?
  2. 02

    Methane leak detection and emissions reporting are now regulated and data driven.

    WHY
    EPA rules require leak detection and repair surveys, continuous monitoring at some sites, and annual greenhouse gas reporting under Subpart W. Compliance rests on survey records, monitor data, and repair logs held in environmental management software. The Waste Emissions Charge is calculated from reported volumes.
    LOSS
    Inability to produce survey and repair records during an inspection results in penalties and a higher assessed emissions charge, because unsupported emissions are estimated against you rather than in your favor.
    ASK
    How do you store your leak detection survey and repair records?
  3. 03

    Gas sales settle monthly on measured volume and heating value from third-party systems.

    WHY
    Payment is based on volume and BTU content measured at the delivery point, recorded by the purchaser or midstream operator, and settled 30 to 60 days later. The producer has no independent measurement. Nominations, confirmations, and imbalance statements all move electronically.
    LOSS
    Unresolvable settlement across one or more production months, with imbalance positions that cannot be verified. On a mid-size producer this is $500,000 or more of receivables with no supporting records.
    ASK
    Do you keep an independent record of delivered volumes and BTU content?
