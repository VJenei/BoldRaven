Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 624310

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 624310, Vocational Rehabilitation Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Client records carry disability and medical information.

    • Records document disabilities, medical history, and employment barriers.
    • That information carries privacy protection and stigma.
    • Every affected client must be notified.
    ASK
    What client disability and medical records do you hold?
  2. 02

    Services are delivered on scheduled plans.

    • Training, placement, and support services run on individual plans.
    • Clients arrange their lives around those schedules.
    • A disruption sets back employment progress.
    ASK
    How many clients depend on your scheduled services?
  3. 03

    Funding compliance depends on records.

    • Programme funding carries eligibility, outcome, and reporting requirements.
    • Funders audit those records directly.
    • Records you cannot produce put the funding at risk.
    ASK
    What eligibility and outcome records does your funding require?
