Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 523160

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 523160, Commodity Contracts Intermediation, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Customer segregated funds carry absolute regulatory protection.

    WHY
    Futures commission merchants must segregate customer funds and report on them daily to the CFTC and their designated self-regulatory organisation. Shortfalls in segregated funds are among the most serious violations in the industry and have ended firms.
    LOSS
    An inability to compute and report segregated fund positions is a regulatory event in itself, and any apparent shortfall triggers immediate intervention regardless of the underlying cause.
    ASK
    How is your daily segregated funds computation produced and verified?
  2. 02

    Margin calls and position management run continuously with markets.

    WHY
    Commodity positions are marked to market with margin calls issued and met on short deadlines. Positions move with markets that trade nearly around the clock, and a firm that cannot compute exposure cannot manage it or call customers for margin.
    LOSS
    Undercollateralised positions through a market move, where the firm absorbs customer losses it could not call margin against, and clearing house obligations must be met regardless.
    ASK
    How quickly can you compute customer margin requirements after a large market move?
  3. 03

    Customer account data includes complete financial profiles.

    WHY
    Account opening for derivatives trading requires identity verification, financial condition, trading experience, and suitability documentation, plus bank details for funding. Firms are financial institutions with GLBA and SEC or CFTC privacy obligations.
    LOSS
    Breach notification across a customer base of sophisticated investors, with regulatory examination of controls and reputational damage in a market where firm selection is driven by trust in operational integrity.
    ASK
    How many customer account files with financial disclosures do you hold?
