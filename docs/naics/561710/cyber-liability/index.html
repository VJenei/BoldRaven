Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 561710

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 561710, Exterminating and Pest Control Services, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Pesticide application records are legally required and inspected.

    WHY
    Applicators must maintain records of restricted use pesticide applications including product, rate, location, date, and applicator licence, retained for periods set by federal and state rules and produced during inspection. Records are also the defence in an exposure claim.
    LOSS
    State enforcement for records that cannot be produced, and in a human or pet exposure claim the absence of application records leaves the applicator without a defence.
    ASK
    Where are your pesticide application records stored, and for how long?
  2. 02

    Technicians have keys and access to customer premises.

    WHY
    Service routes involve scheduled entry to homes and businesses, frequently when occupants are absent, with access codes and key arrangements recorded in the route system. Commercial accounts include food facilities with after-hours access.
    LOSS
    Exposure of customer addresses, access codes, and service schedules provides a route into premises and reveals when they are empty, which is a burglary targeting resource.
    ASK
    Do you hold access codes or keys for customer premises in your route system?
  3. 03

    Food facility and regulated account service supports client compliance.

    WHY
    Food processors, restaurants, and healthcare facilities rely on documented pest management as part of their own regulatory and audit compliance, with service reports, trend data, and corrective actions reviewed by auditors and inspectors.
    LOSS
    Client audit failures where pest management documentation cannot be produced, with the client's certification or licence at risk and the consequences claimed against the service provider.
    ASK
    Do your clients rely on your service documentation for their own audits?
