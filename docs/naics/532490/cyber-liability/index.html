Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 532490

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 532490, Other Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Customers cannot operate without the equipment.

    • Equipment is rented for production, projects, and operations.
    • Customers have no alternative available.
    • Their operations stop, not just yours.
    ASK
    What customer operations depend on the equipment you rent?
  2. 02

    Inspection and maintenance records matter after an incident.

    • Records are kept per machine covering inspection and service.
    • Those records are examined when equipment fails or injures.
    • Records you cannot produce are a defence you do not have.
    ASK
    How are your equipment inspection and maintenance records maintained?
  3. 03

    Reservations and delivery run the operation.

    • Equipment is booked and delivered against customer schedules.
    • Crews and operations are planned around delivery.
    • A missed delivery idles the customer's operation.
    ASK
    How are your reservations and deliveries scheduled?
