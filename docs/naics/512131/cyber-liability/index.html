Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 512131

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 512131, Motion Picture Theaters (except Drive-Ins), needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Films will not play without valid digital keys.

    WHY
    Digital cinema content is encrypted and plays only with a key delivery message issued to a specific projector for a specific date range. Keys are managed through the theatre management system. Without valid keys and a functioning system the projector will not show the film.
    LOSS
    Showtimes cancelled with audiences already ticketed, on opening weekend when a title earns the majority of its theatrical revenue. Refunds and lost concession sales compound the loss.
    ASK
    How are your digital cinema keys delivered and managed?
  2. 02

    Ticketing and loyalty hold payment credentials and subscription billing.

    WHY
    Theatres sell through their own apps and third-party platforms, with reserved seating, loyalty accounts, gift cards, and increasingly subscription programmes billed to stored cards. PCI DSS 4.0 became fully mandatory on March 31, 2025.
    LOSS
    Card compromise across a subscriber base with stored credentials, plus gift card and loyalty balance theft that converts to admissions immediately.
    ASK
    How many subscribers have payment methods stored for recurring billing?
  3. 03

    Concession and admission systems are the entire revenue capture.

    WHY
    Theatres earn a large share of profit at the concession stand, with point of sale, kiosks, and mobile ordering integrated to the same platform as ticketing. Manual operation at a busy multiplex is not practical.
    LOSS
    The site cannot sell tickets or concessions during peak periods, and audiences who cannot be served on a Friday night do not return later that weekend.
    ASK
    What share of your profit comes from concession sales?
