Cyber Liability Insurance for NAICS 423610

CYB

Ranked reasons a business in NAICS 423610, Electrical Apparatus and Equipment, Wiring Supplies, and Related Equipment Merchant Wholesalers, needs Cyber Liability coverage. Each reason is specific to how this industry actually loses money, and ends with the question to ask before the objection comes up.

  1. 01

    Long lead time gear is quoted, reserved, and released against project schedules.

    WHY
    Switchgear, transformers, and distribution equipment carry lead times measured in many months and are engineered to order. Distributors hold submittal packages, approved drawings, and release schedules tied to construction milestones.
    LOSS
    Lost release and submittal records delay engineered gear on projects where the equipment is already on the critical path, with the contractor claiming delay against the distributor.
    ASK
    What is the current lead time on the engineered gear you supply?
  2. 02

    Copper content makes inventory a persistent theft target.

    WHY
    Wire and cable carry high copper value, resell easily through scrap channels, and are stocked in bulk. Inventory records show exactly what is held and where, and shipment records show what is moving.
    LOSS
    Theft of wire inventory, or a fictitious pickup of a wire load worth $100,000 or more, released voluntarily to a fraudulent carrier and therefore not a covered theft.
    ASK
    How is your wire and cable inventory secured and verified against records?
  3. 03

    Contractor project quotes carry your pricing across the market.

    WHY
    Distributors quote the same project to multiple bidding contractors, holding pricing for a period after award. Those quotes disclose your cost basis and manufacturer support levels, and are the contractual basis for pricing after the contractor wins.
    LOSS
    Disclosed pricing damages your competitive position across the market, and lost quote records leave disputes over honoured pricing on awarded projects.
    ASK
    How many project quotes are outstanding and price-protected at any time?
